**Question (unchanged):** "When a student wants to use a different name or gender identity at school,
who decides whether parents are told?"

**Poles (L1):** *The student decides* ↔ *Parents decide first.*

| rung | text | stated act | change from v1 |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Require staff to keep a student's gender identity from parents unless the student consents. | require confidentiality | unchanged |
| 2 | Forbid rules that require schools to tell parents. | bar notice rules | unchanged |
| 3 | Require schools to tell parents when parents ask. | disclose on request | **new**: replaces "notice, except when danger" |
| 4 | Require schools to notify parents. | notice duty | **simplified**: "every time" removed; a stated safety exception no longer moves a law off this rung |
| 5 | Require parental permission before staff use a different name or pronouns. | consent first | "and" → "or" (L7: one act) |

**What each neighbouring pair differs by (T6):**
- 1 vs 2: a rule that requires secrecy vs a rule that forbids notice mandates.
- 2 vs 3: forbids notice rules vs requires disclosure when asked.
- 3 vs 4: disclosure on request vs a duty to notify unasked.
- 4 vs 5: notice vs permission before staff act.

**Cost of this redraft.** It merges two real positions — notice with a safety exception, and notice
with none — onto rung 4. The safety exception is a common and debated feature; this ladder no longer
separates it. That is the price of the T6 rule here, and it brings back "tell parents when they ask",
which v1 had dropped.

**Risks for the test:** rung 1 vs 2 may still read as adjacent for a statewide bar that also forbids
disclosure (watch for `adjacent-chairs`); rung 5 may attract consent laws that also impose notice
(seat 5 — the further rung — per the annex convention).

## Option A1 — the parents ladder: who controls telling parents

**Question:** "When a student wants to use a different name or gender identity at school, who decides
whether parents are told?"

**Poles (L1, stored internally):** *The student decides* ↔ *Parents decide first.*

| rung | text | kind of act |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Require staff to keep a student's gender identity from parents unless the student consents. | require confidentiality (pole) |
| 2 | Forbid rules that require schools to tell parents. | bar the other side's policy |
| 3 | Require schools to tell parents, except when staff believe telling would put the student in danger. | require notice, with a stated exception |
| 4 | Require schools to tell parents every time a student asks to change their name or gender. | require notice on every request |
| 5 | Require parental permission before staff use a different name or pronouns. | require consent first (pole) |

## Option A2b — the pronoun ladder: name and pronoun use

**Question:** "Should school staff use the name and pronouns a student asks for?"

**Poles (L1):** *Staff must use them* ↔ *Staff may not use them.*

| rung | text | kind of act |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Require staff to use the name and pronouns a student asks for. | require use (pole) |
| 2 | Let each staff member decide whether to use them. | leave to discretion |
| 3 | Allow staff to use them only with a parent's permission. | permit with a stated condition |
| 4 | Forbid rules that require staff to use them. | bar the other side's policy |
| 5 | Forbid staff to use a name or pronouns that do not match the student's sex, even with a parent's permission. | forbid use (pole) |

Either option replaces the current ladder on the same topic. Neither adds a topic. Whichever loses
leaves its question without a ladder: the pronoun ladder has no rung for notification laws or bars on
them; the parents ladder covers pronoun use only through its rung 5 (parental permission first).

## Trade-offs the test should settle

1. **"Tell parents only when they ask" has no rung in A1.** The current rung 2 held it, as a
   compound with the name clause. Six points exist on this axis (confidentiality, bar on notice
   rules, on request, notice with exception, notice always, consent first) and L10 allows five. The
   draft keeps the five that a law states as an act. The exposed evidence had nobody stating the
   on-request case. If the frozen set shows on-request policies often, swap it in for rung 2 and
   re-test.
2. **"Name and pronouns" is one object, not two clauses.** Sources and laws state them together. If
   the test shows records that act on pronouns alone, split the object with "or" (L7: same act).
3. **A2b rungs 3 and 4 may not hold their order.** A law that requires parental permission and a law
   that protects a teacher who refuses can both exist in one state. T3 (ordered pairs) checks this.
4. **The local level is own-words only** (CA_0302), and the staging list asks whether `local` should
   be dropped for all education topics (`.planning/todos/2026-10-03-local-ladder-defects-season-3.md` §7).
   This draft does not change roles.
