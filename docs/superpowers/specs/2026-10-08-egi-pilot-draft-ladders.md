## A1 — one topic: who controls telling parents

**Question:** "When a student wants to use a different name or gender identity at school, who decides
whether parents are told?"

**Poles (L1, stored internally):** *The student decides* ↔ *Parents decide first.*

| rung | text | kind of act |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Require staff to keep a student's gender identity from parents unless the student consents. | require confidentiality (pole) |
| 2 | Forbid rules that require schools to tell parents. | bar the other side's policy |
| 3 | Require schools to tell parents, except when staff believe telling would put the student in danger. | require notice, with a stated exception |
| 4 | Require schools to tell parents every time a student asks to change their name or gender. | require notice on every request |
| 5 | Require parental permission before staff use a different name or pronouns. | require consent first (pole) |

### A2b — new topic: name and pronoun use

**Question:** "Should school staff use the name and pronouns a student asks for?"

**Poles (L1):** *Staff must use them* ↔ *Staff may not use them.*

| rung | text | kind of act |

## Trade-offs the test should settle

1. **"Tell parents only when they ask" has no rung in A1/A2a.** The current rung 2 held it, as a
   compound with the name clause. Six points exist on this axis (confidentiality, bar on notice
   rules, on request, notice with exception, notice always, consent first) and L10 allows five. The
   draft keeps the five that a law states as an act. The exposed evidence had nobody stating the
   on-request case. If the frozen set shows on-request policies often, swap it in for rung 2 and
   re-test.
2. **"Name and pronouns" is one object, not two clauses.** Sources and laws state them together. If
   the test shows records that act on pronouns alone, split the object with "or" (L7: same act).
3. **A2b rungs 3 and 4 may not hold their order.** A law that requires parental permission and a law
   that protects a teacher who refuses can both exist in one state. T3 (ordered pairs) checks this.
4. **One record can seat both A2 topics.** An Arizona-type law (permission before use) meets A2a rung
   5 and A2b rung 3. That is two true facts about one act, not double counting, but reviewers should
   know it.
5. **The local level is own-words only** (CA_0302), and the staging list asks whether `local` should
   be dropped for all education topics (`.planning/todos/2026-10-03-local-ladder-defects-season-3.md` §7).
   This draft does not change roles.
