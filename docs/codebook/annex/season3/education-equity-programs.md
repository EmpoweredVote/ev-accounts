**Question:** "What should schools do about equity programs, such as equity offices, plans and staff training?"

**Guidance:** "Whether government should require, support, limit or end school programs aimed at equity,
diversity and inclusion."

**Orientation:** standard. Each rung names **the furthest act government takes on equity programmes in K-12
schools**: require them (1), set up or fund them (2), bar required training (3), replace them with supports
open to every student (4), end them (5). Poles (L1, stored in the revision's `rationale`, not shown to voters):
*schools must run equity programs* ↔ *equity programs are banned in schools.*

**Levels with a lever:** school (board policy, budget, staffing) and state (a statute or rule that binds every
district).

**Asked at:** federal `own-words`, state `record`, school `record`, local `own-words` (`compass_topic_roles`,
CA_0302, CA_0312). Own words only at: federal and local (codebook V2 "No-lever level").

**Scope — what an "equity program" is** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Q2; staff wording R3)_. An office, officer or
staff role, a committee or council, a plan, or training of staff **or board members**, whose stated purpose is
equity, diversity, inclusion, anti-bias, or cultural competence or responsiveness, in K-12 schools.
Out of scope:
- What students are taught → `adjacent` (topic `education-curriculum`).
- Subgroup reporting and accountability rules; funding weights by student category; admissions; discipline-
  disparity rules → `adjacent` (the Season 2 annex hard cases, kept). Targeting rules were rungs 3–4 of
  revision 1 and left the ladder with Q2.
- Hiring rules (diversity statements, identity-based hiring preferences) → `adjacent` _(ruled 2026-10-19, Q4)_.
- A study or task force → `study-directive`. An annual budget line → V4 `multi-subject`.

**Single-subject, this topic** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Q3 + R1; codebook V4.1 line written with this revision)_.
One operative part that is evidence for both this topic and a broader topic (`civil-rights`) does not make a
bill multi-subject. A separate operative part makes it multi-subject **only when it acts outside K-12
schools**: colleges' or state agencies' own operations, or other employers. Parts of a school equity
instrument on racial-harassment reporting, hiring or Title VI compliance, and preparation or licensure
standards for K-12 staff, stay inside this topic (hiring itself is `adjacent`, Q4, so it neither seats a
rung nor splits the bill). A K-12-only equity ban → single-subject; a ban that also covers colleges or state
agencies → `multi-subject`. Whether this reading extends to other topics is not ruled.

**Furthest act** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Q5; carries over the library Q2 ruling of 2026-10-15)_. A record that
states the acts of two neighbouring rungs sits on the rung of its **furthest** act: creates an office and
requires training → 1; closes offices and states a replacement open to all students → 4. Each lower act must
still be stated; nothing is inferred from silence (V4.2). Acts from both sides of rung 2/3 in one record →
BLANK `adjacent-chairs`.

**Synonyms:** "equity office", "chief equity officer", "diversity, equity and inclusion" (DEI), "equity
plan", "equity advisory committee", "equity council", "anti-bias training", "antiracism policy",
"implicit-bias training", "cultural competency training", "culturally responsive training", "equity
continuing education" (certificate renewal), "divisive concepts" (content-limit laws).

1. **"Require schools to have an equity office, committee or plan, or equity training for staff or board
   members"**
   - Means: a law or policy puts a duty on schools to have an equity body or plan, or to train their people
     in equity.
   - Operative clause: [a] a stated duty to have an equity office, committee or plan, or to give equity
     training to staff or board members.
   - Establishing evidence looks like: a statute or rule that requires every district to have an equity
     plan, office or committee, or to train staff or board members; a board policy that requires all its
     staff to take equity training or each school to adopt an equity plan.
   - Levels that hold a lever: school; state.
   - **Rung 1 needs a stated duty** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4)_: staff or board members **must** attend; schools **must**
     adopt a plan or have an office or committee. A board that adopts its own plan with no duty on staff → 2.
   - **Bodies: 1 vs 2** _(ruled 2026-10-19, R4)_. A statute or policy that requires every district to have an
     equity committee → 1. A board that sets up its own committee with no duty → 2.
   - **Board members** _(ruled 2026-10-19, R3)_. Required equity training for school board members meets [a]
     at state (a statute binding every board) and at school (a board binding itself).
   - Equity continuing education required to renew a K-12 teacher or administrator certificate is a stated
     training duty → [a] _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4; licensure standards for K-12 staff stay in scope under R1)_.

2. **"Set up or fund an equity office, officer, committee or plan, or equity training for staff or board
   members, in schools"**
   - Means: government creates or pays for an equity programme, with no duty on schools or staff.
   - Operative clause: [a] creates or funds an equity office, officer, committee or plan, or equity training
     for staff or board members.
   - Establishing evidence looks like: a board vote that creates or funds a position, office, committee or
     plan, or approves a training contract; a single-subject state grant programme that funds district
     equity offices, plans or training.
   - Levels that hold a lever: school; state.
   - **Own action item** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4)_: the creation or funding is its own item. Hiring a named person into an
     existing post → `adjacent`. A line in the annual budget → V4 `multi-subject`.
   - A board that adopts its own equity policy, plan or professional development with no duty on staff → 2.
   - Optional training, offered but not required → 2 (not 3: an offer is not a bar).

3. **"Bar schools from requiring staff to take equity training"**
   - Means: schools may keep equity programmes but may not make staff attend equity training.
   - Operative clause: [a] a stated bar on requiring staff to attend equity training.
   - Establishing evidence looks like: a statute or board policy that says no employee may be required to
     attend equity training.
   - Levels that hold a lever: school; state.
   - **Stated bar** _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4)_: the bar must be stated ("no employee may be required to attend …"). A bar is
     an act (rulebook L3 clarification, 2026-10-08).
   - A bar inside a wider ban sits on the furthest act (4 or 5).
   - No record in the Stage 1 frozen set; a blank spoke is correct.

4. **"Replace equity offices or programs with supports open to every student"**
   - Means: an equity programme ends and its support is re-opened to all students.
   - Operative clause: [a] the end of an equity office or programme **and**, as one change, the support that
     replaces it, open to all students.
   - Establishing evidence looks like: a board vote that renames or re-scopes an equity office or programme to
     serve all students; a statute that converts district equity offices into offices open to all students.
   - Levels that hold a lever: school; state.
   - **Rung 4 is the replacement act** _(Draft B, R2)_. The record must state both the end and the
     replacement. A ban with a savings clause ("nothing here prohibits services open to all students") is
     **5**, not 4: a savings clause permits, it does not replace (V4.2 "Ruling out the other rungs is not
     evidence").
   - **Rung 4 vs 5** _(Draft B, R2)_. A closure that states a replacement open to all → 4. A closure that
     states none → 5. Rung 5 has no limit clause, so the silence rule does not apply to it.
   - No record in the Stage 1 frozen set; a blank spoke is correct.

5. **"Close equity offices or ban equity programs in schools"**
   - Means: government ends equity programmes in schools.
   - Operative clause: [a] closes an equity office, or bans or ends an equity programme (office, officer,
     committee, plan or training).
   - Establishing evidence looks like: a statute that bans equity offices, officers or training in K-12
     schools; a board vote that closes an equity office or ends its equity plan or training.
   - Levels that hold a lever: school; state.
   - Ending **any one kind** of equity programme meets [a] (L3: general in the kind).
   - **A suspension** of an equity plan, policy or office by the body's own vote reads as ending it → 5
     _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4; Stage 3 default reading D3)_.
   - Commonly confused with rung 4: see rung 4.

**Hard cases:**
- **Content limits on training** _(Draft B, R2)_. A rule that lists concepts equity training may not promote
  matches no rung → `direction-only` (restriction side). Most such laws also limit classroom teaching, which
  makes them `multi-subject` (curriculum) in any case.
- **Votes to pause or delay** a closure or suspension until a court rules → `direction-only` on the pro side:
  no rung states keeping or restoring a programme, and ruling out rung 5 is not evidence _(ruled 2026-10-19, Stage 4; Stage 3
  default, 3 of 3 blind coders)_.
- **Higher-law duty (codebook 0.6, small bodies).** A board vote that carries out a state mandate or ban shows
  compliance: that provision is `direction-only`. Board provisions that go beyond the state duty are coded on
  their own. State duties boards carry out, found so far _(from the Stage 1 frozen set; not exhaustive; add
  as found)_:
  - **Maryland:** county boards must give employees antibias training every other year (HB 1386); board
    members must take antibias training (HB 324).
  - **Oregon:** every district must set up an equity advisory committee (SB 732).
  - **Virginia:** each school board must require cultural-competency training every two years (HB 1904 /
    SB 1196).
  - **Washington:** districts must train staff in equity on a learning day (ESSB 5044); equity continuing
    education is required to renew teacher and administrator certificates (ESHB 1426).
  - **Indiana:** none found.
- **State law and school boards.** A state law that requires or bans equity offices, training or programmes in
  every district states the rule → `on-question`. A law that only moves the decision to another body →
  `adjacent` (V2, H12) _(ruled 2026-10-01)_. A state law is the legislator's act, not a school-board member's.
- **Study, audit or task force** on equity → `study-directive`.
- **Annual budget and omnibus votes** → V4 `multi-subject`.
- **Small bodies.** Codebook 0.6 (V4.1 "Small bodies"): on a body of 10 or fewer voting members a vote seats
  only if it was its own action item and the provision is the body's own choice. Bodies of 11 or more keep the
  10% rule.
