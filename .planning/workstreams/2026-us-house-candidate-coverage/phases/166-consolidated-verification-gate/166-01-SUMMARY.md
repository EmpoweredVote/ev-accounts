# 166-01: Live derivation of the Phase-166 universe and pin lists

## What this establishes

The five per-phase Wave-3 gates froze their pin lists at their own authoring dates (161 and 162 on
2026-07-04/05, 163 on 2026-07-06, 164 and 165 on 2026-07-07). This plan re-derived every one of them
live against production on **2026-07-26**, so no pin id, PROVISIONAL claim or withholding claim in
Phase 166 traces back to an early-July snapshot without having been re-confirmed.

Both artifacts are committed and re-runnable. Two consecutive runs are byte-identical (737 lines of
stdout; artifact md5 `407e63bc537fb74425d3cd2abc42ef8c`).

## Scope correction — 42 elections became 43

The plan enumerated 42 elections from the 163 snapshot. Live derivation showed that is no longer the
in-scope universe:

- Phase 163 seeded WI onto the 8 `WI 2026 Statewide General` races on 2026-07-05.
- On **2026-07-25** — 19 days after 163's gate froze — a party-split `WI 2026 Partisan Primary`
  election (2026-08-11, still upcoming) was created and WI's field moved onto it.
- Today the WI general holds **5 active candidates with 4 of its 8 races empty**, while the primary
  holds **32 active including 7 incumbents**.

Scoping WI to the general alone would have asserted USHC3-06 coverage over 5 candidates while
silently ignoring 32 — green for precisely the reason this plan exists to prevent. `WI 2026 Partisan
Primary` is therefore in scope.

IN and UT also carry House primary races, but theirs are **past** (2026-05-05 and 2026-06-23) and hold
only 3 and 5 primary-only, mostly UUID-keyed politicians. Concluded contests stay out of a
forward-looking general-election gate.

Consequence: the 178 assertion counts **distinct `geo_id`**, not `race_id`. Those coincided in every
prior gate because each state ran one race per district; WI now runs three. The per-state race count
is still printed so a genuine race explosion stays visible.
