<objective>
Close the third, previously-uncovered FEC HTTP call site: `fecResearch.ts`'s `searchFecCandidates()` (reached from the admin-triggered `runFecAutoMatch`) currently does a bare `fetch` to `api.open.fec.gov` with no lock and no limiter, and can run concurrently with the 6-hourly cron against the same shared key. Purpose: Without this, a manually-triggered auto-match colliding with a cron run can reintroduce the 429 tail even after Plans 01/02 ship for the two cron sites.
Output: Modified `fecResearch.ts` + new `fecResearch.test.ts`.
