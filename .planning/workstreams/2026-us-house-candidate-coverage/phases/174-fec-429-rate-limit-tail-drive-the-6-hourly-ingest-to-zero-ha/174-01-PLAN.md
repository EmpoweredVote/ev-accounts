    - acquireFecSlot() resolves immediately (no sleep) when the current UTC-minute bucket count is <= the budget.
    - Once the bucket count exceeds the budget, acquireFecSlot() polls (short sleep, re-check) and resolves on a later tick when a fresh bucket is available — it never resolves while over budget and never stalls > a single poll interval per loop.
    - With Redis available: `.incr(key)` is called with key `fec:ratelimit:<YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM>`; on the first increment of a bucket (count === 1) `.expire(key, 90)` is called; the returned count gates the slot.
    - When the Redis client is absent (env vars unset) OR `.incr` throws, the limiter falls back to an in-process Map counter keyed the same way and still gates correctly (degrade, do not throw).
    - Budget reads from `process.env.FEC_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MINUTE` (parseInt, default 15).
  </behavior>
