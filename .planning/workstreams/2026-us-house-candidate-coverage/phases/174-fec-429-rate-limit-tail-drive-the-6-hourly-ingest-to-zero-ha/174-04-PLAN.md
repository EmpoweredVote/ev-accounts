<objective>
Fix the pre-existing amendment double-count latent bug surfaced by the live-API research: an amended Schedule A row arrives with a new sub_id (so the existing ON CONFLICT dedup does not catch it) and a populated original_sub_id pointing at the row it replaces — which is never retired. This plan retires the superseded row, removes the dead skip check, and gates the delete behind one live confirmation of the original_sub_id linkage.

Purpose: FEC-04 correctness — itemized totals must not double-count amended transactions; the incremental refresh (174-02) makes amended rows arrive routinely, so this must be correct.
Output: supersession retirement in the FEC upsert path, gated by a live-confirmed linkage.
</objective>
