<objective>
Produce the AL and LA old-vs-new congressional map correspondence audits (locked pattern D-01a) — the phase's FIRST artifacts, front-loaded, on which AL seeding (163-04) and LA seeding (163-06) depend. This is the first Wave-3 phase needing TWO audits: AL's 2023 GOP-drawn map was reinstated by a SCOTUS stay (June 2026) for the 2026 cycle, moving AL-1/2/6/7's lines and triggering an Aug-11 special primary; LA's SB121/Act 2 (signed 2026-05-29) dissolved the majority-Black CD-6 and reverted toward the ~2022 map. essentials.districts still carries the OLD polygons for both states (and those drive the reps feed until Jan 2027). Each audit scores every district by boundary-shift severity so the seed plans know which districts to seed-but-withhold from /elections (D-01b) rather than surface a new-map-correct slate against a stale polygon.

Purpose: Prevent serving wrong House races to AL/LA voters while keeping the reps feed correct. Mirrors the TN audit (161-01) and MO audit (162-01) exactly.
Output: 163-al-correspondence-audit.md and 163-la-correspondence-audit.md, each with a per-district severity table, an enumerated severe-geo_id list, the verbatim-cloned TN rubric, and source URLs. No DB writes, no code.
</objective>
