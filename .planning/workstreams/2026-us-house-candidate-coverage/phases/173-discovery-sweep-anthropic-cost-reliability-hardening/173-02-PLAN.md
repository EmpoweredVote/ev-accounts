<objective>
Wire the OPS-01 pre-flight and the OPS-02 typed retry classifier into the sweep orchestrator (`discoveryCron.ts`), and document the OPS-04 horizon constant. Before iterating any jurisdiction, `runDiscoverySweep` calls the `checkAnthropicAvailability()` canary once; a conclusive-unusable result (missing key or 401/402/403) aborts with exactly one operator alert and zero paid per-jurisdiction calls, while an inconclusive failure lets the sweep proceed. Separately, replace the fragile message-regex `isTransient` with a typed `isRetryable` so credit/auth errors can never multiply spend via `withRetry`.

Purpose: Eliminate the 144× "credit balance too low" and 45× key-not-configured per-jurisdiction floods (OPS-01) and the 3× retry multiplication on non-retryable errors (OPS-02); make the weekly cost cadence visible (OPS-04).
Output: Modified `discoveryCron.ts` + new `discoveryCron.test.ts`.
</objective>
