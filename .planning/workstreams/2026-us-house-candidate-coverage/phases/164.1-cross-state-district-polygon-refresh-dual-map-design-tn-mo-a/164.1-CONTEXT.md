# Phase 164.1: Cross-State District Polygon Refresh + Dual-Map Design (TN/MO/AL/LA/UT) - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-06
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Import the enacted 2026 congressional-district polygons for the 5 mid-cycle-redistricted states (**TN, MO, AL, LA, UT**) and design/build the **dual-map** so `/elections` resolves addresses on **2026 boundaries** while the reps feed stays on **current-representation boundaries until Jan 2027** — then **un-withhold the 13 seeded-but-hidden races** parked on past-dated "Polygon Pending" elections: **TN 4704/4705/4706/4708/4709, MO 2902–2906, AL 0102, LA 2202/2206**.

This is the milestone's **first backend-code phase** (everything since Phase 160 was pure data). Both feeds currently share one polygon per district via `districts.geo_id → essentials.geofence_boundaries` + `ST_Covers`; the dual-map requires schema + query changes and a **Render deploy** (unlike the seeding phases).

Deliverables:
1. 2026-vintage polygons for TN (9) / AL (7) / LA (6) / UT (4) now; MO (8) behind a date-gate (see D-05/D-06).
2. Dual-map backend: parallel-layer boundary vintages + opt-in by the elections-content consumers (D-01/D-02).
3. Un-withhold flips (severe races' `election_id` → the state's general election) once the per-state verify bar passes (D-10); flip the 161/162/163 gate severe-zero assertions + negative smoke samples to positive per state; re-verify Edmonds (−220605) surfaces correctly in refreshed LA-6.
4. UT 2026 polygons + a written **UT wiring contract** consumed by Phase 165 (D-07) — **UT is the only part of this phase that blocks Phase 165**.
5. A queued, date-gated **Jan-2027 boundary-promotion phase** spec (D-03) — designed here, executed ≥ 2027-01-03.

**Out of scope:** seeding any candidate/race data (Phases 161–165); the Jan-2027 flip execution itself; the MO field re-resolution if the referendum reverts the map (diverts to Phase 167 — D-06); UT incumbent re-key/re-link and UT race seeding (Phase 165, per the wiring contract); `essentials.offices` changes of any kind; user-district cache re-resolution.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Dual-map data model & consumers
- **D-01 (parallel layer):** 2026 polygons coexist with current ones as **parallel-layer rows in `essentials.geofence_boundaries`** — same `geo_id`, a distinct vintage discriminator (mtfcc variant or a vintage column with the unique index scoped so existing queries cannot match new rows; planner picks the exact mechanism). Chosen for the fail-safe default: **untouched consumers keep resolving on old boundaries automatically; only elections queries opt in.** Rejected: vintage-filter-everywhere via `valid_from`/`valid_to` (any missed consumer double-matches → duplicate rows, fail-dangerous) and a separate table (forks GIST/import infra).
- **D-02 (content-type rule):** Which consumers flip to 2026 boundaries is decided by rule, not hand-picked list: **content answering "what's on your 2026 ballot" resolves on 2026 boundaries; content answering "who represents you now" stays on current boundaries.** Researcher audits ALL `ST_Covers` call sites against the rule (`electionService.ts`, `electionsMapService.ts`, `coverageMapService.ts`, `essentialsService.ts`, `essentialsBrowseService.ts`, `readrankService.ts`, `informBoundaryService.ts`, plus `resolve_user_districts`/`cache_user_districts` RPCs); planner enumerates the exact flip set.
- **D-03 (Jan-2027 flip = queued phase):** The reps-feed transition to new boundaries is an **explicit date-gated phase (≥ 2027-01-03)** — promote 2026 polygons to canonical, retire the old vintage, re-key `essentials.offices` per the UT wiring contract + the other 4 states' correspondence, re-resolve `connect.user_districts` for affected states. 164.1 designs for the flip but does NOT automate it. Rejected: `CURRENT_DATE`-driven query branch (untested path firing unattended in prod) and a config flag. Matches the 153/159-05/167 date-gated-phase precedent — record the re-entry date in ROADMAP + STATE.md Operator Next Steps.
- **D-04 (cache/offices no-touch + gate assert):** `connect.user_districts` and `essentials.offices` are **untouched by this phase**, and the phase gate ASSERTS both byte-identical pre/post (same pattern as 161/162's offices-untouched assertions). Elections-by-coordinate is live `ST_Covers` (uncached), so no user-facing cache work is needed until the Jan-2027 phase.

### MO referendum hedge
- **D-05 (date-gate the whole MO slice):** MO gets its **own plan, executable ≥ the referendum-certification decision (~Jul-28)** — NO MO polygon import or un-withhold before then. Rationale: importing new MO polygons early would also move the three currently-surfacing districts (2901/2907/2908) onto legally-unsettled boundaries. TN/AL/LA/UT proceed now. **MO does NOT block Phase 165** (only UT does).
- **D-06 (pre-committed revert branch):** If certification qualifies the referendum and MO's general reverts to OLD boundaries: **MO exits 164.1 entirely** (old polygons are already correct — zero polygon work) and the real work — field re-resolution vs old districts, `race_candidates` re-wire, then un-withhold on existing polygons — **diverts to Phase 167's MO cluster** (already a mandatory re-pull). 164.1 closes documenting the divert; the withholding stays in place until 167 re-wires the field. If the map holds (frozen new): the MO plan = whole-state import + verify bar + un-withhold 2902–2906, inside 164.1.

### UT scope split vs Phase 165
- **D-07 (polygons + wiring contract):** 164.1 delivers **UT 2026-vintage polygons (geo_ids 4901–4904 persist; shapes + incumbent mapping change)** PLUS a written **UT wiring contract** as a phase artifact: the old→new incumbent correspondence (Moore old-D1→new-D2, Maloy D2→D3, Kennedy D3→D4, Owens retired, new D1 open — McAdams D field per NOTE-UT-REDISTRICTED-2026); Phase 165 wires new-map races to the **existing geo_id offices**; incumbents go on their NEW district's race as `race_candidates` (candidacy ≠ office); **`essentials.offices` is never touched until the Jan-2027 flip phase re-keys it**. No UT withholding mechanism is ever needed — UT races don't exist until 165 and are born onto correct 2026 boundaries. This resolves the roadmap-note vs 160-06 ambiguity: 164.1 = polygons + design; 165 = seeding + pid re-link per the contract.

### Polygon sourcing & verification
- **D-08 (official-first + RDH fallback):** Shapefile source standard: **official state GIS / legislative / court-filed shapefiles preferred; Redistricting Data Hub acceptable fallback.** Per-state provenance recorded in the phase artifact (source URL + enactment instrument — TN HB 7003/SB 7001 May-7-2026; LA SB121-2026; UT LWV v. Utah Legislature map eff. Nov-2025; AL per 163-01 audit basis; MO pending certification). None of these maps exist in TIGER 2024 (the `load-national-house-districts.ts` source) or TIGER 2025.
- **D-09 (whole-state refresh, per-state atomic):** Every district in each state gets a 2026-vintage polygon (TN 9, AL 7, LA 6, UT 4; MO 8 when gated) — never severe-only (intra-state vintage mixing creates overlaps/gaps and breaks the tiling check). The elections vintage flips **per state atomically**; non-severe surfacing districts move from approximately-right to exactly-right shapes.
- **D-10 (3-layer verify bar before un-withholding):** Per state, ALL THREE must pass before any withheld race's `election_id` flips to the general: (1) **topology sanity** — the state's 2026 districts tile the state (ST_Union coverage vs state boundary, per-district area sanity); (2) **per-district positive anchor coordinate** — every un-withheld district returns the right race on `/elections` from a known in-district point under the NEW map; (3) **≥1 differential point per state** — a coordinate that changed districts old→new resolves to the NEW district on elections AND the OLD district on the reps feed simultaneously (the only end-to-end dual-map proof).

### Connected-tier gap (added 2026-07-06 post-research, USER-APPROVED)
- **D-11 (Connected-tier live fallback):** Research discovered `connect.connected_profiles.congressional_geo_id` (Path 1 — `getElectionsByGeoIds`, the logged-in Connected-tier `/elections` lookup) is populated from single-vintage sources and structurally can never reflect 2026 geometry, so differential-zone Connected users would see OLD-district races through the Nov-2026 general. **User decision: fix in-phase.** Path 1 falls back to a live geometry resolution for the 5 refreshed states (TN/MO/AL/LA/UT) ONLY — scoped behind the same vintage discriminator, no cache mutation, no change for other states — so the D-02 rule holds for all user tiers. The D-10 Layer-3 differential test MUST also exercise the Connected-tier path (do not steer the gate away from it). Rejected: accept-and-document (gap window spans the Nov-2026 general election, hitting the most engaged tier at the worst time).

### Carried forward (locked — do not re-litigate)
- The election_id-substitution withholding mechanism and the exact severe sets (TN 5 / MO 5 / AL 1 / LA 2) are as shipped in 161-06 / 162-02 / 163-04 / 163-06 — un-withholding = re-pointing `election_id` to the state's general election row; `office_id` was never null and never changes.
- Seeded TN/MO/AL/LA candidate/race/stance data is keyed to the NEW map and needs **zero rework** on polygon refresh (161 D-01 rationale).
- Prod ref `kxsdzaojfaibhuzmclfq`; migrations idempotent (NOT EXISTS guards); PostGIS in RPCs = `public.ST_*`; backend code changes require a Render deploy (`RENDER_DEPLOY_HOOK`); `cd /c/EV-Accounts/backend &&` in the same compound Bash call.

- LA-6 Edmonds (−220605, re-keyed LA-5→LA-6 in mig 1230): re-verify she surfaces correctly in the refreshed LA-6.

## Specific Ideas

- The differential-point check (D-10 layer 3) is the operator's dual-map acceptance test: one address that changed districts must show its NEW district's race on `/elections` and its OLD district's rep on the reps feed **at the same time**.
- MO subtlety that drove D-05: the referendum risk isn't only about the 5 withheld districts — MO-1/7/8 currently surface on old polygons, and importing new MO polygons early would move them onto legally-unsettled boundaries.
- UT insight that made D-07 clean: geo_ids 4901–4904 persist; only shapes + incumbent↔geo_id mapping change, and candidacy (race_candidates) is independent of office-holding (offices) — so 165 can seed the full new-map field with zero office edits.
- TN "Polygon Pending" election is dated 2026-05-07 (map enactment); MO's is 2026-03-24 (MO Supreme Court date) — the un-withhold flip must target each state's real general-election row, not delete these marker elections (they're historical artifacts of the mechanism).
