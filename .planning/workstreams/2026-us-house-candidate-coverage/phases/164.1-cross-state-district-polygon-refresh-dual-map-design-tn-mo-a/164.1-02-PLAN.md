Layer-3 differential: discover a point in NEW district A that falls in OLD district B (A!=B) via ST_Difference/ST_Intersection; then assert NEW district's race surfaces on /elections AND OLD district's rep surfaces on the reps feed for the same point.

Severe sets (pass as a per-state parameter — Layer-3 discovery must AVOID picking a severe NEW district for the guaranteed pre-flip assertion, because a still-withheld severe race legitimately has NO surfacing race and would spuriously fail):

Withholding detection: a severe race is "surfacing" (un-withheld) when its office→district geo_id's race points at the state's general election, and "withheld" when it points at the '{STATE} 2026 Congressional Redistricting - Polygon Pending' election. Detect this from the DB at runtime — do NOT hardcode a flip date.

D-11 Connected-tier assertion: after discovering a differential point, resolve the NEW congressional geo_id for that point against mtfcc='G5200V26' (the same resolution connect.resolve_congressional_2026 performs), then feed that corrected (geo_id, 'G5200') pair through a getElectionsByGeoIds-equivalent query and assert the NEW district's race surfaces — proving the Connected-tier geo-id path, not only the uncached coordinate path. Do NOT require a real authenticated Connected session; assert the geo-id path directly with the corrected geo_id.

D-04 NOTOUCH: per-row md5 checksum (not just count) over essentials.offices for the 5 states' NATIONAL_LOWER offices and over essentials.geo_districts WHERE layer='us_house' AND geoid LIKE '{fips}%' (each asserted equal to a documented baseline literal); PLUS a DISTINCT (layer,geoid) district-SET md5 + cardinality over connect.user_districts us_house rows for the 5 states, pinned to a dated baseline literal — RAISE EXCEPTION on set change (a Pitfall-2 breach), RAISE NOTICE for benign per-user-row churn (the weekly districtStalenessService cron legitimately adds/removes user rows; assert the SET shape, never row contents).
