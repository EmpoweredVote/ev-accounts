Adjust these settings to change how you see your races. You'll be able to change these settings at any time while you are viewing your races.

PARTY SELECTION

Selecting your preferred party here will by default filter closed primary race candidates to only show those in your party. However, you will still see all candidates for non-partisan or open races. If you'd like to see all candidates for each race and not default to a party, select the "See All" option!
