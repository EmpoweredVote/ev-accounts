State Profile
Indiana

The next election in Indiana is Indiana Primary Election on Tuesday, May 5, 2026.

More than 2,260 positions are up for election ranging from federal positions including U.S. House of Representatives - Indiana 7th Congressional District to local offices like Marion County: Decatur Township Small Claims Court Judge.

Following this election, the Indiana General Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Important dates for 2026 Indiana Primary Election

There may be additional rules or exceptions to the deadlines shown. Please see voting information below for more details.

Registering to Vote

Online applications must be submitted by April 6, 2026

In-person applications must be submitted by April 6, 2026

Mail-in applications must be received by April 6, 2026

Requesting an Absentee Ballot

Absentee ballots may be requested in person until May 4, 2026

Mail-in requests must be received by April 23, 2026

Voting

Mail-in ballots must be received by May 5, 2026

Election Day is May 5, 2026
