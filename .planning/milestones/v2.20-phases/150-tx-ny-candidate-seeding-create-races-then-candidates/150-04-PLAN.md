<objective>
Wire the full Nov-3 general field onto all 26 NY US House races as `essentials.race_candidates`, creating new `essentials.politicians` records ONLY for genuinely-new candidates and reusing existing records for every incumbent / previously-seeded figure -- after a per-candidate live-DB dedup check (D-03). Seed ALL minor-line candidates (D-02); surface primary WINNERS, not lost incumbents (D-05).

Output: One idempotent seed migration + a live-confirmed reconciliation CSV; after it runs, every NY House race surfaces its field on `/elections`.
</objective>
