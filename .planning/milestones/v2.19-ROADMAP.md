## v2.19 Local Civic Coverage (Phases 145–147)

**Milestone goal:** Full-stack local coverage for three new jurisdictions — Falls Church VA, Greene County MO, and Springfield MO — records, geofence boundaries, sourced stances, headshots, and essentials discoverability. Pure data + coverage; no backend code in this repo.

**Execution note:** All three phases were executed **inline** (at user request — not GSD-phased), so they have no plan directories. The per-jurisdiction deep-dive memory files are the authoritative build record. Formalized retroactively 2026-06-23.

**Git range:** `a488232a` (Falls Church seed) → `ef1a364f` (Springfield headshots). Timeline 2026-06-22 → 2026-06-23.

### Phase 145 — Falls Church VA Coverage ✅

Independent VA city on the **Alexandria template**. Two governments (`City of Falls Church, Virginia, US` + `Falls Church City Public Schools, Virginia, US`). 17 elected officials: 7 City Council (Mayor in LOCAL_EXEC + at-large in LOCAL, both at place FIPS), 3 constitutional officers (Sheriff/Treasurer/Commissioner of Revenue on the county-equiv COUNTY district), 7 FCCPS School Board (SCHOOL district at NCES geo_id). Created a coterminous school `G5420` geofence by copying the place polygon. 55 evidence-only stances (10 of 17 stanced; 7 honest blanks), 17 official `.gov`/fccps headshots. essentials `COVERAGE_STATES` entry (purple chip). Migration 1047.

- Commits: `a488232a` (records) / `0d388c07` (stances) / `cf9adc6e` (headshots)

### Phase 146 — Greene County MO Coverage ✅

County on the **LA County template** (Government type LOCAL, geo_id = 5-digit county FIPS 29077). 13 elected officials across County Commission + Sheriff + Prosecuting Attorney + County Officers chambers, all on one shared `COUNTY` district. Imported the county `G4020` polygon from Census TIGERweb (`State_County` layer 1) — MO had zero loaded county boundaries. 26 evidence-only stances (5 of 13 stanced; 8 ministerial officers honest-blank), 13 headshots from `greenecountymo.gov`. essentials `COVERAGE_COUNTIES` entry (search-only; `skip_overlap=1`). Migration 1048.

- Commits: `448c3761` (records + geofence) / `3d77073a` (stances) / `951b9310` (headshots)

### Phase 147 — Springfield MO Coverage ✅

City + school board (Greene County seat; MO's 3rd-largest city). 16 officials: 9 City Council (Council-Manager, directly-elected Mayor + 8) + 7 SPS R-XII Board. Imported place `G4110` (TIGERweb Places layer 4) + school-district `G5420` (School layer 0, fetched separately — not coterminous). 37 evidence-only stances (9 of 16 stanced; 7 honest blanks — SPS questionnaire source 429-walled). 16 headshots via a clean-sourcing pass (rejected ringed CivicEngage thumbnails; SPS board from `sps.org/meet-the-board`). essentials `COVERAGE_STATES` entry. Migration 1049.

- Commits: `dad06979` (records + boundaries) / `a3f9269b` (stances) / `ef1a364f` (headshots)
