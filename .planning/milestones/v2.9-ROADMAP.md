## Phase 108: LA County City Officials

**Goal:** Every major LA County city has its full elected governing body (mayor + city council + other separately elected citywide officers per city charter) seeded in `essentials.politicians` + `essentials.offices`, with photos populated and FIPS geo_ids on the related district rows. Coverage spans 14 partial cities (gap-fill), Beverly Hills + Santa Monica (structure-exists), 3 LA City citywide offices (offices exist), and 10 new cities (full structure created from scratch).

**Dependencies:** Pre-existing essentials schema from v1.6 Phase 38; existing LA County BOS + LA City council records (migrations 074-082, 115).

**Requirements:** LAOF-01, LAOF-02, LAOF-03, LAOF-04, LAOF-05, LAOF-06

**Plans:** 4 plans

Plans:

- [ ] 108-01-PLAN.md — Wave 1: gap-fill 14 partial LA County cities (Long Beach Mayor, Glendale 3 at-large members, Pasadena Mayor + missing district members, Burbank/Downey/El Monte/Inglewood/Lancaster/Norwalk/Palmdale/Pomona/Santa Clarita/Torrance/West Covina); migrations 293-299
- [ ] 108-02-PLAN.md — Wave 2: Beverly Hills (5 council + elected Treasurer Howard Fisher), Santa Monica (7 council), LA City Controller Kenneth Mejia + appointed City Clerk Patrice Lattimore; explicitly leave City Attorney office vacant (RESEARCH.md Critical Finding 1); migrations 300-303
- [ ] 108-03-PLAN.md — Wave 3: 10 new cities (South Gate, Compton, Carson, Hawthorne, Whittier, Alhambra, Gardena, Culver City, West Hollywood, El Segundo) — government structure + politicians; migrations 304-309
- [ ] 108-04-PLAN.md — Wave 4: Phase verification SQL script + representatives-me smoke test + final audit migration 310

**Success Criteria:**

1. Every Phase 108 politician row has non-null `photo_origin_url`, `office_id`, `party=NULL`, `is_incumbent=true` (LAOF-05).
2. Every Phase 108 city district row has its 7-digit Census FIPS geo_id populated (LAOF-04).
3. 10 new `essentials.governments` rows exist for the 10 new cities; all have their full elected governing body seeded (LAOF-03).
4. LA City Controller Kenneth Mejia linked to existing office UUID `e5435b0e-c7a7-4c93-9b4f-cc647db0b9f6`; City Clerk Patrice Lattimore seeded as appointed; City Attorney office (UUID `5a873c59-72ac-488f-8b2c-44dfd04d065c`) explicitly left vacant pending November 2026 runoff (LAOF-02).
5. All migrations idempotent (re-application yields zero new rows).
6. `backend/scripts/verify-la-county-108.sql` runs all 8 assertions green.
7. `backend/scripts/smoke-la-representatives-me.ts` returns >= 1 Phase 108 politician for a known LA-area test user.
8. Any seat whose current incumbent name was not verified at execution time is logged as `-- VERIFICATION-PENDING:` rather than guessed (LAOF-06).
