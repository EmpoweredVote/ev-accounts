# Milestone v2.9: LA County Expansion

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-06-08
**Phase:** 108 (Phase 109 deferred to future milestone)
**Total Plans:** 5

## Overview

Expanded LA County civic data coverage to 27 cities in a single phase. Phase 108 delivered complete elected governing bodies — mayor, city council, and separately elected citywide officers per charter — across four waves: gap-fill for 14 partial Tier 1 cities, Beverly Hills + Santa Monica structure completion + 3 LA City citywide offices, and 10 new cities built from scratch with full government stack. All officials have photos, Census FIPS geo_ids on district rows, and office linkage.

Phase 109 (LA County Finance Data via CAL-ACCESS + Netfile) was descoped from v2.9 after Phase 108 completed; requirements deferred to a future milestone.
