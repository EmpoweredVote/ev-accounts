<objective>
Create load-worcester-council-boundaries.ts to fetch 5 pre-dissolved council district polygons from Worcester's city-owned ArcGIS FeatureServer, insert into geofence_boundaries with mtfcc='X0014'. Then write and apply migration 660 which inserts 5 per-district LOCAL rows, backfills tiger_geoid on all Worcester district rows (per-district + citywide), and re-links the 5 district councillors' offices from the citywide '2582000' LOCAL district to their per-ward rows.

Purpose: Upgrades Worcester from Tier 2 (citywide blob) to Tier 3 (per-district geofencing). A user in Worcester District 3 will now resolve to John Fresolo rather than all 10 councillors.

Output: load-worcester-council-boundaries.ts script (run once), 660_worcester_council_district_geofencing.sql applied to production.
</objective>
