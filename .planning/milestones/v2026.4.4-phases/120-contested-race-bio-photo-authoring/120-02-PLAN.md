For each candidate in 120-CANDIDATE-SCOPE.md that needs bio and/or photo content:

**Bio research (per D-05, D-14):**
1. Search for the candidate online using their name + office they are running for + "Monroe County Indiana"
2. Find ONE authoritative source URL (priority per D-08: official gov page > campaign site > news > social media)
3. Extract key facts: current role/occupation, background, reason for running
4. Compress into one sentence, 120-180 characters (D-12)
5. Apply antipartisan constraint (D-13): omit party affiliation unless it is the candidate's literal job title
6. If insufficient public info exists, use fallback: "Candidate for [office title]." (D-15)

**Photo sourcing (per D-08, D-09):**
1. Find a photo of the candidate (priority: official gov photo > campaign site > social media > news)
2. Record the direct image URL as `photo_source_url`
3. If no photo is findable, mark as `NO_PHOTO` -- do not block (D-10)

**Compile into 120-REVIEW-DATA.md with this format (per D-06):**
