For each politician and topic:

1. Search for documented positions — House votes, bills sponsored/cosponsored, floor speeches, official statements, committee actions, interviews, news coverage

**Research guidelines (from CONTEXT.md decisions — these are NON-NEGOTIABLE):**
- Integers only (1-5), no decimals
- When evidence conflicts across time, use the MOST RECENT position
- Only use the individual politician's documented positions — NO party platform fallbacks
- Prefer public statements and speeches over roll call votes as primary sources
- When citing votes, prefer standalone bills over omnibus/bundled legislation
- Valid sources: congress.gov, GovTrack, senate.gov, house.gov, official statements/speeches, news from major outlets
- Advocacy group ratings are NOT valid sources
- Omit the row entirely if no documented position exists for a topic
- Minimum 1 source URL per row (required); up to 3 source URLs
- Sources should point to specific articles/votes/speeches — general "Issues" pages acceptable as fallback only
