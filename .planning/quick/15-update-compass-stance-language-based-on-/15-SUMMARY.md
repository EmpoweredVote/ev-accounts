## Design Decisions

- Target reading level: ~8th grade (accessible but not condescending)
- Extremes (1 and 5) should represent real policy positions, not strawmen
- Middle stances should form a clear gradient, not just vary by topic/reason
- Avoid dated references that will age poorly
- Use recognizable policy names where they exist (Medicare for All, Citizens United)
