<objective>
Wire the full Nov-3 general field onto all 17 IL US House races as `essentials.race_candidates`, creating new `essentials.politicians` records ONLY for genuinely-new candidates and reusing existing records for every incumbent / previously-seeded figure — after a MANDATORY per-candidate live-DB dedup check AND a certified-general re-confirm (D-03, sharpened for IL).

Purpose: USHC2-02 + USHC2-03 for IL. IL is the highest-risk seeding district set this phase: 5 incumbents are retired/ran-for-Senate (IL-2/4/7/8/9) so their seats have non-incumbent nominees, and the 154 field lists the PRIMARY field (IL-4 = 7 names, IL-2 = 3 names) not the certified general — the certified Nov-3 nominees MUST be re-confirmed from the IL Secretary of State certified ballot list before seeding.
Output: One idempotent seed migration + a live-confirmed, certified-general-re-confirmed reconciliation CSV; after it runs, every IL House race surfaces its true ballot field on `/elections`.
</objective>
