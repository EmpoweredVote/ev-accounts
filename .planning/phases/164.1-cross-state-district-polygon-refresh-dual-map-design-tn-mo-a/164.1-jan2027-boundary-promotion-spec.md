## Why this phase exists

Phase 164.1 shipped a **dual-map** design: 2026-vintage congressional polygons live in `essentials.geofence_boundaries` as `mtfcc='G5200V26'` rows, consumed ONLY by the elections opt-in join (`electionService.ts` vintage-preference LATERAL) and the D-11 Connected-tier live fallback. Everything else — the reps feed, `essentials.geo_districts`, `connect.user_districts`, `connected_profiles.congressional_geo_id`, `essentials.offices` — deliberately stayed on the pre-2026 (`'G5200'`) vintage, because until Jan-2027 the OLD map still describes actual seated representation. This phase performs the one-time promotion that makes the 2026 map the single canonical vintage.

## D-11 was FIXED IN-PHASE in 164.1 — shipped, not an accepted gap

Per the user-approved decision (2026-07-06), differential-zone Connected-tier users' `/elections` was corrected **in 164.1 itself**: `connect.resolve_congressional_2026(uuid)` (migration 1246; read-only SECURITY DEFINER, decrypts the stored coord server-side, ST_Covers against `G5200V26` for FIPS 47/29/01/22/49 only, no cache mutation) + the Path-1/Path-1.5 substitution in `backend/src/routes/essentials.ts`. Verified live on PROD by the 1641 smoke's direct-RPC sentinel probe for TN/AL/LA/UT. **This spec's step 5 is the point that RETIRES that temporary fallback** — after step 4 makes the cached geo_ids authoritative, the fallback is redundant and must be removed so there is a single source of truth.

## Promotion steps (concrete, in order)

1. **Promote G5200V26 → canonical.** For the 5 states' congressional geo_ids: retire the old `G5200` vintage rows (or repoint consumers) so `geofence_boundaries` has a single congressional geometry per geo_id again. Recommended: DELETE the old `G5200` rows for the affected geo_ids and UPDATE the `G5200V26` rows' `mtfcc` → `'G5200'` in one transaction (the `(geo_id, mtfcc)` unique key requires delete-before-rename). Then simplify/remove the vintage-preference LATERAL in `electionService.ts` and the two `'G5200V26'` `NOT IN` exclusions (geoIdGuard.ts + essentialsService.ts) once no V26 rows remain.
2. **Re-key `essentials.offices`** per the old→new incumbent correspondence: **UT per `164.1-ut-wiring-contract.md`** (Moore 4901→4902, Maloy 4902→4903, Kennedy 4903→4904, Owens retired, new 4901 open) + the TN/AL/LA(/MO) correspondences (161-tn-correspondence-audit.md and the 163/162 audit artifacts; note most TN/AL/LA incumbents keep their number — the re-key is about which politician holds which district office AFTER the Jan-2027 swearing-in, driven by the Nov-2026 results, so this step consumes the election outcomes, not just the maps).
3. **Re-resolve `connect.user_districts`** for users in the 5 states. `essentials.geo_districts` is SINGLE-vintage and was deliberately untouched by 164.1 (D-04); refresh its us_house rows for FIPS 47/29/01/22/49 from the promoted geometry FIRST, then re-run the resolution path (`resolve_user_districts`/`cache_user_districts` RPCs — the weekly `districtStalenessService` cron's machinery) for affected users.
4. **Refresh `connected_profiles.congressional_geo_id`** (+ `congressional_district_name`): `connect.resolve_user_jurisdiction` hardcodes `mtfcc='G5200'`, so after step 1's rename it resolves against the promoted 2026 geometry naturally — bulk re-run it (or a targeted UPDATE from step 3's results) for users with coords in the 5 states. The weekly cron will NOT do this on its own for congressional slots — it must be an explicit promotion step.
5. **RETIRE the D-11 read-path fallback.** Remove `connect.resolve_congressional_2026` (DROP FUNCTION migration) and the `resolveCongressional2026()` helper + `REFRESHED_2026_FIPS` substitution in Paths 1/1.5 of `backend/src/routes/essentials.ts` (shipped in 164.1-01). Deploy. After this, the cached `congressional_geo_id` is the single source of truth again.
