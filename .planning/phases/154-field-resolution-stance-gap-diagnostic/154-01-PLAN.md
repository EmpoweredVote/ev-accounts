<objective>
Produce the DB half of the Wave-2 (PA/IL/OH/GA/NC/MI/NJ/VA) US House field diagnostic: a read-only `diag-154-incumbent-stance-gap.ts` script that maps every district incumbent to its existing `politician_id`, reports each incumbent's federal-24 stance count, and enumerates every district with a non-1 holder count (vacancies / post-v2.17 special seats).
