1                   AN ACT

2 RELATING TO REDISTRICTING OF THE PUBLIC EDUCATION COMMISSION;

8 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF NEW MEXICO:

9   SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.--This act may be cited as the

10 "2021 Educational Redistricting Act".

11  SECTION 2. COMMISSION MEMBERS.--The public education

12 commission is composed of ten members to be elected from

13 districts established by law.

14  SECTION 3. ELECTION FOR STAGGERED TERMS.--

15             A. Members of the public education commission

16 shall be elected for staggered terms of four years.

17             B. Members shall be elected at the general

18 election for terms commencing on January 1 next succeeding

19 their election.

20             C. Members from districts two, three, five, six

21 and seven shall be elected from the districts described in

22 Sections 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12 of the 2021 Educational

23 Redistricting Act at the 2022 and subsequent general

24 elections.

25             D. Members from districts one, four, eight, nine HFL/HSEIC/HB 9

1 and ten shall be elected from the districts described in

2 Sections 6, 9, 13, 14 and 15 of the 2021 Educational

3 Redistricting Act at the 2024 and subsequent general

4 elections.

5   SECTION 4. RESIDENCE.--A candidate for the office of

6 public education commissioner shall reside in the district

7 for which the candidate files a declaration of candidacy at

8 the time of such filing. A public education commissioner

9 shall reside in the district from which the commissioner was

10  elected or appointed and shall be deemed to have resigned if

11  the commissioner changes residence to a place outside the

12  district. Vacancies shall be filled as provided in Section

13  16 of the 2021 Educational Redistricting Act.

14  SECTION 5. PRECINCTS.--

15             A. Precinct designations and boundaries used in

16  the 2021 Educational Redistricting Act are those precinct

17  designations and boundaries established pursuant to the

18  Precinct Boundary Adjustment Act as revised and approved

19  pursuant to that act by the secretary of state as of November

20  30, 2021.

21             B. A board of county commissioners shall not

22  create any precinct that lies in more than one public

23  education commission district and shall not divide any

24  precinct so that the divided parts of the precinct are

25  situated in two or more public education commission            HFL/HSEIC/HB 9

1 districts. Votes cast in a public education commission

2 election from precincts created or divided in violation of

3 this subsection shall be invalid and shall not be counted or

4 canvassed.

2               B. The governor shall by appointment fill a

4 to fill a vacancy on the public education commission shall be
