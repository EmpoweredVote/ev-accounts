## What the LRC publishes, read 2026-09-26

**The only restriction anywhere on the site is a commercial-use clause**, stated on
<https://legislature.ky.gov/policies-security/Pages/default.aspx>:

> Under KRS 61.874, it is unlawful to use any records available on this site for a commercial
> purpose without agreement with the Legislative Research Commission.

**KRS 61.870(4)(a)**, read from the statute's own PDF (`krs-61-870.pdf`, fetched as bytes —
`statute.aspx` serves a PDF, not HTML):

> "Commercial purpose" means the direct or indirect use of any part of a public record or records,
> in any form, for sale, resale, solicitation, rent, or lease of a service, or any use by which the
> user expects a profit either through commission, salary, or fee.

**KRS 61.870(4)(b)** excludes publication by a newspaper or periodical, use by a radio or
television station in its news or other informational programs, and litigation use. ⚠ **We are none
of those three**, so the wave does not rest on the carve-out. It rests on the head of the
definition: Empowered Vote expects no profit from these portraits. The site is free, carries no
advertising and sells nothing.

**Everything else on the site is silent.** Checked, not assumed:

- The **Disclaimer**, **Security** and **Accessibility** pages were each read in full. **None
  mentions copyright or reuse.** NC-5's rule — *read the disclaimer, not the footer* — was followed
  and produced nothing here.
- The Policies page's **Legal Notice** is a generic DMCA safe-harbour procedure about third-party
  infringement, with `copyrightagent@egov.com` as Kentucky.gov's agent. It is neither a grant nor a
  refusal about the LRC's own photographs.
- The only copyright claim is boilerplate in the footer of every page: *"Copyright 2026 Kentucky
  Legislative Research Commission / All rights reserved"*, repeated as *"© 2026 Commonwealth of
  Kentucky."*
- 🔴 **The image bytes are silent too, and that is the opposite of Tennessee.** TN's rights
  statement lived in a PNG `tEXt` chunk after every page had come up empty. Here the EXIF, the
  Photoshop IPTC block and the XMP packet of a full-resolution portrait were all read. They carry a
  camera body, a lens, a capture date, the caption *"Legislator portrait"* and a subject name — and
  **no `Copyright` field, no `dc:rights`, no `xmpRights:UsageTerms` and no by-line.**
  ▶ Reading the bytes is still right. Here it returned a genuine absence rather than a find.
- ⚠ **`robots.txt` advertises `Sitemap: sitemap.xml` and that file 404s**, so TN's method of
  sweeping a sitemap for a hidden policy page cannot be run on this host. The footer links were
  walked instead.

🟢 **And the publisher invites the download.** Every member profile carries an explicit
**"Download Full Resolution Image"** link to the same file we import. The PIO's own news-photo
service states *"All photos are attributed to 'LRC Public Information'."* — a credit convention,
which is the credit this wave offers to carry.

**Subject:** Confirming permitted use of Kentucky General Assembly member portraits on a
non-commercial civic information site

> Empowered Vote is a non-profit civic information service. We publish a free, non-partisan
> directory that lets a resident enter an address and see every official who represents them, from
> their city council to Congress, each with a portrait so the person is recognisable.
>
> We have seated all 138 members of the Kentucky General Assembly on that directory, along with
> Lexington-Fayette's city offices and Fayette County's elected officers. We are writing about the
> official member portraits published at `legislature.ky.gov`, in the *Legislators Full Res Images*
> library and linked from each member's own profile page as "Download Full Resolution Image".
>
> **We read your terms of use as permitting our use, and we would like to confirm that reading with
> you.** Your Policies page states that under KRS 61.874 it is unlawful to use records from the site
> for a commercial purpose without an agreement with the Commission. KRS 61.870(4)(a) defines a
> commercial purpose as use for sale, resale, solicitation, rent or lease of a service, or any use
> by which the user expects a profit. **Our use is none of those.** The site is free to the public,
> carries no advertising and sells nothing, and we expect no profit from these portraits. If you
> read the clause differently, please tell us and we will act on your reading.
>
> What we do, stated plainly:
>
> - We store our own copy rather than hotlink, so that our pages add no load to Commission servers
>   and do not break when files are re-organised.
> - We crop each portrait to a common 4:5 frame, which is how they are displayed beside one another.
>   No other editing is applied — no retouching, no recolouring, and nothing that changes what the
>   photograph depicts.
> - **We would like to carry the credit you prefer.** Your Public Information Office states that
>   General Assembly news photos are attributed to "LRC Public Information". If you would like that
>   credit, or a photographer's name, recorded with each member portrait, tell us and we will use it.
> - **What sits beside the portrait, said plainly now rather than discovered later.** Alongside the
>   office, the district and the term, we publish a legislator's positions on policy topics wherever
>   we can evidence them — each one a specific stated position, with the bill, the vote or the
>   quotation we drew it from cited next to it, so a reader can check our work. We do not publish a
>   position we cannot cite, we do not infer one from party membership, and we correct what we get
>   wrong. **For these 138 members we publish no positions today** — we have recorded only the
>   office, the district and the term — but that research is planned. We would rather you knew that
>   now than met it later.
> - **The portrait itself is never used as a mark of approval.** It identifies the person, and
>   nothing on the site suggests that the Commission, any member or any staff endorse Empowered
>   Vote, or any candidate, party, campaign, product or cause. That is the only sense in which we
>   say the image carries no endorsement — it is not a claim that the page beside it is silent about
>   the member's own positions, because it is not.
>
> One practical note, offered in case it is useful to you: your GIS layer
> `Ky_Legislative_Districts_WGS84WM` still names the former member at Senate District 37. Each
> member's own profile page is correct.
>
> If this belongs with another office, **we would be grateful if you would tell us which, and we
> will write to them instead.** A pointer is as useful to us as an answer.
