## 🔴 Source defects found

- 🔴🔴 **Source A carries a STALE ROSTER.** Senate District 37 reads `Yates, David`; B and C both
  read **Gary Clemons**. David Yates resigned 2025-10-08 to become Jefferson County Clerk;
  Clemons won the 2025-12-16 special election. **KY-1 used this same layer as its GEOMETRY
  authority and proved it 138/138 — a source can be authoritative for one field and stale for
  another.**
- 🔴 **A departed Kentucky legislator has NO page.** The profile URL is keyed to the SEAT
  (`DistrictNumber` only), so a successor replaces the predecessor and a departure marker can
  never appear. The cross-source diff is the only change-check that works here.
- 🔴 **`legislature.ky.gov` SOFT-404s**: `DistrictNumber` 139, 200, 0 and `abc` all return
  HTTP 200 at ~61.7 KB against 68–69.5 KB for a real page. Status is not a validity signal.
- 🔴 **The Senate is offset by +100 in the profile URL** (Senate 1 = `DistrictNumber=101`).
  A naive `DistrictNumber=<district>` silently fetches a HOUSE member for every Senate seat.
- ⚠ **Five name-form disagreements** between A and B, all settled from C:
  `Donworth, Anne` vs `Anne Gay Donworth`; `Meade , David` (stray space in A);
  `Frazier Gordon, Deanna` vs `Deanna Gordon`; `Thomas, Reginald` vs `Reginald L. Thomas`;
  `Herron, Keturah` vs `Keturah J. Herron`.
- 🔴 **One member has held one seat under THREE published names.** House District 81:
  `Frazier, Deanna` (2019–2021) → `Frazier Gordon, Deanna` (2021–2025) → `Gordon, Deanna`
  (2025– ). A name-keyed diff reads that as two departures and two arrivals.
  **Matching Kentucky legislators by name across time is unsafe.**

## Charter rulings

- **Ky. Const. § 33**: 100 House districts and 38 Senate districts. Both chambers are
  single-member, so polygon count IS seat count — unlike ND and SD.
- **Ky. Const. § 30**: terms begin 1 January of the year succeeding the election. Used only
  as background; see the term-date note below for why it is NOT applied as a blanket day.
- House terms are 2 years; Senate terms are 4 years and staggered.
- **Party is not written.** Party is antipartisan in this schema and lives on
  `races.primary_party`.
