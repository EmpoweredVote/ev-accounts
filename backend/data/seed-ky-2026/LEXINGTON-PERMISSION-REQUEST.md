# Lexington portraits — permission request, and why KY-5 holds the 16 city seats

**Status: OPEN. 16 seated Lexington-Fayette officials carry no portrait, deliberately.**
Ruling: Cantrell, 2026-09-26 — **ask LFUCG in writing and hold.** The same ruling let the 138
Kentucky General Assembly portraits ship, because the LRC restricts only *commercial* use and our
use is not commercial. Lexington is a different publisher with a different, express prohibition,
and the two must not be summed. This follows MN-5 and PA-5's route, which MN-6 closed in a day
when the Minnesota House answered — see
`backend/data/seed-mn-headshots-2026/HOUSE-PERMISSION-REQUEST.md` and
`backend/data/seed-pa-headshots-2026/PHILADELPHIA-PERMISSION-REQUEST.md`.

## The sixteen seats

The Mayor, the Vice Mayor, two other At-Large members and the twelve district Councilmembers —
every city office KY-3 seated. All sixteen currently render with no face.

## What the City publishes, and what it forbids

Read live 2026-09-26 at <https://www.lexingtonky.gov/terms-use>. The clause is not in the page's
visible HTML — the site is a Next.js app and the body lives in `__NEXT_DATA__`, which is why a
plain text scrape of that URL returns only the footer. The operative sentences:

> While the City/LFUCG provides the information on all its publication channels to everyone, all
> information and materials provided on this website are the property of the City/LFUCG. You may
> print copies of the information for your own personal use or reference this server from your own
> documents without our permission. **Users may not: Modify or reuse any text, photographs, images,
> video or any other content from any city website … without the express written permission of the
> City/LFUCG. Copy and/or distribute text, photographs, images, video, or any other content from
> any city website … without the express written permission of the City/LFUCG. Frame or otherwise
> incorporate into another website any of the content or other materials from any city websites.
> Mirror any city-owned website on any other server without the city's permission. Any exceptions
> to this policy are the images available on the free image gallery page.**

🔴 **Four clauses bite at once, and our pipeline trips all four**: it reuses, it copies, it
distributes, and it mirrors onto another server. The 4:5 crop is a modification on top of that.
This is the most explicit refusal any slice in this programme has met — more specific than
Philadelphia's, which at least grants residents a narrow copying right.

## 🟡 The one carve-out, and why it is a question rather than an answer

The final sentence names an exception: *"the images available on the free image gallery page."*
**The Terms never say which page that is, and no page on the site calls itself that.**

What exists, and is the plausible referent: the City's **Media center**
(<https://www.lexingtonky.gov/media-center>) lists, under *Photo and video*, a link titled
**"Headshots — Headshots of city officials."** It points at a public PhotoShelter gallery,
<https://lexington.photoshelter.com/galleries/C0000d9QNxmtYQds/Headshots>, whose sub-gallery
**"Current councilmembers" holds exactly 16 files** — one per city office. Read 2026-09-26:

| Field | Value, as the gallery states it |
| --- | --- |
| Caption | e.g. *"Vice Mayor Dan Wu / Dec. 20, 2022 / Photo by Amy Wallot"* |
| Credit/Provider | **`Amy Wallot/LFUCG`** |
| Copyright | **`Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government`** |
| Native size | 2849x3989 to 3861x5405 |
| Controls | Share, **Download**, Add to Lightbox, Add to Cart |

The gallery sits under a parent called **Public**, alongside nine years of press-conference
galleries, and is reached from a page headed *"Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government resources
for the media."* It requires no login to view.

⚠ **A plausible referent is not a grant.** The gallery states a copyright holder and states no
terms; the Terms state an exception and name no gallery. Nothing published joins the two. So the
question below is put to the City rather than answered by us.

⚠ **The gallery is also the better source on the merits**, independent of the rights question: the
copies on `content.lexingtonky.gov` that the council page serves are downsized versions of these
same masters (`DanWu.JPG` appears in both), and only the gallery carries a photographer credit and
a caption naming the person.

## The request to send

### Where to send it — every address below was read off the City's own pages, 2026-09-26

**▶ TO: `pio@lexingtonky.gov`** — the **Public Information Office**, which the Terms of Use page
itself names as the office that manages the site, and which publishes the Media center that links
the Headshots gallery. That makes it the best-evidenced first address, not a guess.

**Subject:** Permission to use official portraits of Lexington-Fayette elected officials on a
non-commercial civic information site

> Empowered Vote is a non-profit civic information service. We publish a free, non-partisan
> directory that lets a resident enter an address and see every official who represents them, from
> their council district to Congress, each with a portrait so the person is recognisable.
>
> We have seated all sixteen of Lexington-Fayette's elected city offices on that directory — the
> Mayor, the Vice Mayor, the At-Large members and all twelve district Councilmembers — along with
> Fayette County's separately elected officers and all 138 members of the Kentucky General
> Assembly. The sixteen city officials are the only ones currently shown without a face.
>
> We would like permission to use the official portraits in your Media center's **Headshots →
> Current councilmembers** gallery, and an official portrait of the Mayor.
>
> **Our specific question about your Terms of Use.** The Terms say users may not reuse, copy,
> distribute or mirror images from city websites without express written permission, and then add:
> *"Any exceptions to this policy are the images available on the free image gallery page."* We
> could not find a page that calls itself that. **Is the Headshots gallery linked from the Media
> center the free image gallery the Terms refer to?** If it is, we would still rather have your
> confirmation in writing than assume it.
>
> We are asking on these terms:
>
> - We would store our own copy rather than hotlink, so that our pages add no load to City servers
>   and do not break when files are re-organised. We understand the Terms speak to copying and to
>   mirroring, and **this is part of the specific permission we are asking for.**
> - We would crop each portrait to a common 4:5 frame, which is how they are displayed beside one
>   another. No other editing is applied — no retouching, no recolouring, and nothing that changes
>   what the photograph depicts.
> - We would carry the credit your gallery already states, **"Amy Wallot/LFUCG"**, recorded with
>   each image. If you would prefer different wording, or a different credit for the Mayor's
>   portrait, tell us and we will use yours.
> - The site is free to the public, carries no advertising and sells nothing.
> - **What sits beside the portrait, said plainly now rather than discovered later.** Alongside the
>   office and the term, we publish an official's positions on policy topics wherever we can
>   evidence them — each one a specific stated position, with the bill, the vote or the quotation we
>   drew it from cited next to it, so a reader can check our work. We do not publish a position we
>   cannot cite, we do not infer one from party membership, and we correct what we get wrong.
>   **For these sixteen officials we publish no positions today** — we have recorded only the office,
>   the district and the term. But that research is planned, so the portrait would in time appear
>   beside a page asserting what we have found this official's positions to be. We would rather you
>   weighed that before granting permission than met it afterwards, and we are glad to show you the
>   live record for any of the sixteen first.
> - **The portrait itself is never used as a mark of approval.** It identifies the person, and
>   nothing on the site suggests that the City, this official or City staff endorse Empowered Vote,
>   or any candidate, party, campaign, product or cause. That is the only sense in which we say the
>   image carries no endorsement — it is not a claim that the page beside it is silent about the
>   official's own positions, because it is not.
>
> If this belongs with another office, **we would be grateful if you would tell us which, and we
> will write to them instead.** A pointer is as useful to us as an answer.
>
> If a blanket permission is not possible, we would also welcome any alternative you prefer: an
> uncropped display, a different credit, or a different source file.

## Until a reply arrives

- The sixteen seats stay **blank**. A blank is honest; a portrait we have no right to copy is not.
- **Do not** substitute a copy from a news site, a campaign site or a social account. A different
  publisher is a different rights question, and press and campaign images are not ours either.
- **Do not** hotlink as a workaround. It dodges the copying question, still trips the framing
  clause, and re-creates the hotlink defect `CC_0019` removed.
- 🔴 **Do not read the 138 shipped General Assembly portraits as precedent for these sixteen.**
  Different publisher, different terms, and that is the whole ruling. The LRC restricts commercial
  use under KRS 61.874 and says nothing else; LFUCG prohibits reuse outright and requires express
  written permission.
- Fayette County's 18 officers are a **third, separate** rights question across several publishers,
  none of them read yet. Three problems; do not sum them.

## If it is granted

Record the grant **verbatim**, with the grantor's name and the date, in this file and in the
migration or import run that writes the URLs — the way MN-6 recorded Mike Cook's answer.
🔴 **A grant is to us, not to the files.** A later session must not read "Lexington portraits are
fine" as a property of the image. Anyone else copying them is in exactly the position we are in now.
