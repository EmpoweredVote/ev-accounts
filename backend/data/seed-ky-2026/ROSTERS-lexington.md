## 🔴 Source defects found

- 🔴🔴 **The Councilmembers page prose produces a wrong inventory.** It describes the Council
  as *"The vice mayor / Two at-large councilmembers / 12 district councilmembers"*, which reads
  as a separately elected **Vice Mayor** office. There is none. The Government page states
  *"There are 12 district council members and three at-large council members"*, and the roster
  lists Dan Wu as *"Council At-Large and Vice Mayor"*. **Vice Mayor is a title, not an office.**
- 🔴 **Member pages carry no term data** — they are biographies. Only the two APPOINTED members
  have a date on their page, in the form *"appointed by Mayor … on <date>"*.
- 🔴 **The archived roster lives in an escaped JSON menu blob, not in anchors**, and one snapshot
  returned undecoded gzip. A parser that reads anchors scores both as zero.
- ⚠ **A name with an embedded comma breaks a naive parser**: `Shayla Lynch, J.D.` was silently
  dropped, leaving 14 of 15, until the parser keyed on the `href`. The credential is not part
  of the name and is not written.

## Charter rulings

- **16 elected offices**: Mayor (1), Council At-Large (3, four-year terms), Council District
  1–12 (12, two-year terms). Vice Mayor is the at-large member with the most votes — a title.
- **KRS 67A does not set an urban-county council’s structure**; the charter does. But the
  statute requires the government to retain the county offices named in the Kentucky
  Constitution — that is stage 4, and it is why consolidation does not empty it.
- The Mayor heads a separate executive office; the Council is the legislative branch. The Mayor
  therefore gets his own chamber, as at Duluth, Grand Forks and Macon-Bibb.

## 🔴🔴 The oath date is not a rule

Whitney Elliott Baxter assumed office 2021-01-04 (a Monday) and Lisa Higgins-Hord’s appointment
runs through 2027-01-04 (a Monday), which invites *"the first Monday in January"*. The city’s own
record says the 2025-26 district members took the oath on **Sunday, January 12, 2025**.
Computing the first Monday would have written **2025-01-06** — wrong by six days.

⚠ That ceremony **re-swore all twelve district members**, incumbents included, so `2025-01-12` is
the start of the 2025-26 *term*, not of continuous occupancy. It is used only for the five whom
the archived timeline shows arriving then. `office_terms` carries continuous occupancy.

council-district layers and **all four carry exactly 12 features** — a count cannot tell them

area: **all 12 districts differ from 2012 by 1.0%–44.9% while the total is preserved to 0.03%**.
