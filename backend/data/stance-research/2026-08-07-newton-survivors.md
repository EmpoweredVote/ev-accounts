## Two factual corrections that apply to both clusters

1. **The vote was 21-2-1 on 4 December 2023, not October 2023.** Two rows date it to October; October
   was the **ZAP committee** vote. This is the same October/December confusion migration 1548 found
   and fixed elsewhere — it survived in these rows because 1548 did not touch them.
2. 🔑 **A 21-2-1 vote is weak stance evidence.** Twenty-one of twenty-four councillors voted yes; the
   only NO votes were Leary and Noel, with Ryan absent. "Voted for MBTA Communities compliance"
   therefore distinguishes almost nobody, and cannot carry a housing or zoning chair on its own.
   Worth generalising: **near-unanimous votes are poor evidence of an individual position.**
