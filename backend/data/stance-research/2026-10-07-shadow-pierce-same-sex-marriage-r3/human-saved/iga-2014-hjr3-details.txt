IGA | House Joint Resolution 3 (2014) Indiana General Assembly 2014 Session. Joint Resolution 3 Marriage. Engrossed House Resolution (H) Authored by: Rep. P Eric Turner. Co-Authored by: Rep. Timothy Wesco, Rep. Wes Culver, Rep. Rhonda Rhoads, Rep. Robert Heaton, Rep. Don Lehe, Rep. Matthew Lehman, Rep. Randall Frye. Sponsored by: Sen. Dennis Kruse. Digest Provides that only marriage between one man and one woman shall be valid or recognized as a marriage in Indiana. This proposed amendment has not been agreed to by the previous general assembly. Latest Bill Actions: S 02/17/2014 Third reading: Passed: Roll Call 201: yeas 32 and nays 17. H 03/13/2014 Public Law 225.
