This row has six sources about two bills, two years apart.

SB 1164 (2022)
- Overview: Shope is a co-sponsor. Signed by the Governor (Chapter 105).
- Senate vote: SHOPE Y.
- Text: "Except in a medical emergency, a physician may not ... perform ... an abortion if the probable gestational age ... has been determined to be greater than fifteen weeks." "Medical emergency" is defined as a condition that requires an immediate abortion "to avert her death" or where delay "will create serious risk of substantial and irreversible impairment of a major bodily function." Section 2 says the act does not "Repeal, by implication or otherwise, section 13-3603, Arizona Revised Statutes, or any other applicable state law regulating or restricting abortion."

HB 2677 (2024)
- Senate vote: SHOPE Y (passed 16-14).
- Text, in full: "Section 13-3603, Arizona Revised Statutes, is repealed." The short title is "abortion ban; repeal." The text does not quote what section 13-3603 said.
