This row has seven sources about two measures from 2024.

SB 1231 (2024) — "state crime; illegal border crossings"
- Overview: Shamp is the prime sponsor. Passed both chambers; the Governor vetoed it.
- Senate vote: SHAMP Y.
- Text (the version the Senate passed; the House did not change it; long — most of it amends other code sections). It makes it a state crime for an alien to enter Arizona from a foreign nation at a place other than a lawful port of entry, and a crime to be found in the state after a removal. It lists affirmative defenses, including when "THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT HAS GRANTED THE DEFENDANT LAWFUL PRESENCE IN THE uNITED STATES OR ASYLUM UNDER 8 United states code SECTION 1158." On conviction "the judge shall enter an order that requires the person to return to the foreign nation from which the person entered or attempted to enter"; a court may also issue such an order instead of prosecution if the person agrees.

HCR 2060 (2024) — a measure referred to the voters
- Senate vote: SHAMP Y. The vote was on the Senate's amended text, which is the text shown.
- Text: a similar state crime of entry outside a port of entry, the same asylum affirmative defense, and the same kind of return order. It also has other parts (for example, on public benefits and on fentanyl sales).
