This row has five sources about one bill, SB 1001 (2023).

- Overview: Kavanagh is the only sponsor ("Kavanagh (Prime)"). The Senate passed it 16-12; the House passed it 31-27; the Governor vetoed it.
- Senate vote: KAVANAGH Y.
- Bill text (the version the Senate passed; the House did not change it). It adds section 15-509. A school employee or contractor "may not knowingly address, identify or refer to a student who is under eighteen years of age" by (1) "a pronoun that differs from the pronoun that aligns with the student's biological sex" or (2) "a first name other than the first or middle name that is listed on the student's official school records" (nicknames of the record name are allowed) — "unless the school district or charter school receives written permission from the student's parent." It also says a school may not require an employee to use such a pronoun if that is "contrary to the employee's ... religious or moral convictions."
- Two news stories (KAWC, January 2023; KJZZ, April 2023) report Kavanagh's spoken comments on this same bill. Read his quoted words directly.
