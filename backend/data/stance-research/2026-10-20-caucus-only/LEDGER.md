## ⚖⚖ THE RULE THIS PASS SETTLED

**A caucus organised around a TOPIC or a COUNTRY cannot seat a rung defined by DEGREE.**
The Congressional Ukraine Caucus takes no position on the LEVEL of aid, so it cannot separate
"significantly increase" from "continue current levels". The Equality Caucus advocates full
equality, so where it is the only evidence it argues for rung 1 — and several rows here cite it
and seat rung 2 or 3 anyway. The Freedom Caucus is an ideological bloc.

**Narrow exception, and it kept exactly one row:** a **SINGLE-POLICY** caucus whose policy IS the
rung's own text can seat that rung. The Medicare for All Caucus maps to medicare/aid rung 1,
"expand Medicare to cover everyone regardless of age", word for word (C040). Contrast the Expand
Social Security Caucus, which names a DIRECTION spanning rungs 1 and 2 and so seats neither (C004).

**The discriminator in practice:** a clause naming WHAT the person supports is a position
(C019 Goldman, "backs U.S. aid to Ukraine and Russian sanctions" → KEEP); a clause calling them
"an advocate for X" characterises the affiliation (C011 Fitzpatrick → BLANK, though he co-chairs
the caucus and the repair is cheap).
