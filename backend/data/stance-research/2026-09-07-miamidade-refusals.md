# Miami-Dade — topics read and REFUSED

A refusal is a result. Writing it down stops the next pass paying for the same reading, and
stops a later pass mistaking "no row" for "not looked at". An empty compass is honest; a
confabulated one is a false statement about a real person.

## Marleine Bastien (District 2) — `economic-development` — REFUSED 2026-09-07

**24 leads read. No chair seated. The record does not answer the question this ladder asks.**

The ladder asks how government should **attract businesses**:

| rung | position |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | No tax breaks or subsidies; invest in public services and infrastructure so businesses come on their own |
| 2 | Help small and local businesses grow, but no subsidies to attract large outside companies |
| 3 | Incentives, but only with wage and local-hiring commitments, and clawbacks if undelivered |
| 4 | Large tax breaks and infrastructure for major employers, with limits |
| 5 | The largest incentives for any large employer, with no conditions or spending limits |

Her 24 leads are **Community Redevelopment Agency work**: the N.W. 7th Avenue, N.W. 79th Street,
North Miami, Naranja Lakes and West Perrine CRAs — budgets, findings of necessity, boundary
expansions, board appointments — plus HUD action and consolidated plans, a street improvement, AARP
grant funding and volunteer income tax assistance.

A CRA is a tax-increment district that reinvests incremental property tax inside a designated area.
**That is not a company-specific incentive to attract an employer.** It is a different question, and
the ladder has no rung for it. Direction is not the problem here; the axis is.

Searched all 24 operative titles for `attract`, `major employer`, `job creation`, `targeted jobs`,
`relocat`, `corporate`, `headquarters`, `tax abatement`, `ad valorem tax exemption`, `enterprise
zone`, `opportunity zone`, `business incentive`, `clawback`, `wage requirement`, `local hiring`.
Three matched and **all three are boilerplate**: `CORPORATE` from "A FLORIDA NOT-FOR-PROFIT
CORPORATION", `RELOCAT` from relocating occupants of an acquired parcel.

Two items looked decisive and were not:

- **R-877-25** (`252009`, adopted, her prime sponsorship) — "FEASIBILITY OF IMPLEMENTING
  INCENTIVE-BASED PROGRAMS" is about **waste diversion and recycling**: pay-as-you-throw and waste
  reduction rebates. Matched on the word "incentive". It is a `city-sanitation` item.
- **`250695`** (before the Board, her prime sponsorship) — urges the Florida Legislature to **oppose**
  SB 1242 / HB 991, which would prohibit creating CRAs, bar new CRA projects and debt, and terminate
  existing agencies. This is a real, stated position and it is hers, but it is a position about
  *preserving redevelopment agencies*, not about incentives to attract businesses. It would evidence
  a CRA ladder. There isn't one.

**What would seat this topic later:** an instrument granting or refusing a company-specific
incentive — an ad valorem tax exemption, a targeted-jobs award, a Beacon Council incentive
agreement — or a stated position on conditioning such awards on wages, local hiring or clawbacks.
None exists in the 2025-01-01 → 2026-09-05 window.
