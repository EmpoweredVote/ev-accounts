READING AID — written by Claude from the text before the coders ran. It says what the text does. It does not suggest a rung; the judgment is yours.

What the House passed (engrossed HJR 3, 2014): it adds one sentence to Article 1 of the Indiana Constitution — "Only a marriage between one (1) man and one (1) woman shall be valid or recognized as a marriage in Indiana."

What it does not say: it does not mention civil unions or any other legal status for unmarried couples, and it sets no penalty. (The bill page shows the House vote on this text.)

How it takes effect: it is a proposed constitutional amendment. A second, separately elected General Assembly must pass it, and then the voters decide. The Governor does not sign it.
