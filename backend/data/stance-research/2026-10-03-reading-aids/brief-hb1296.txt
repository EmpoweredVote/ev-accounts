READING AID — written by Claude from the text before the coders ran. It says what the text does. It does not suggest a rung; the judgment is yours.

The vote shown adopts the conference committee report — the final text of HEA 1296 (2022).

The central change: a person "who is at least eighteen (18) years of age and is not otherwise prohibited from carrying or possessing a handgun ... under state or federal law is not required to obtain or possess a license or permit from the state to carry a handgun in Indiana." Before this act, a license was needed (the struck words show the old requirement).

What stays: a person may still get a license, for example to carry in another state under a reciprocity agreement. People the law bars from having a gun (the act lists them, for example people under 18) are still barred.

The rest of the act: many other sections amend the firearms and criminal-law code to fit the change (for example, records, the license process, and offences). Struck words are removed from the old law; they are not the law.
