> ⚖⚖ **THIS IS THE CHAIR PASS A REPAIR IS NOT ALLOWED TO DO.** The rule *a repair must not move a
> chair* is right, and its consequence was never queued: a repair fixes the citation and leaves the
> seating untouched, so a row with a wrong chair comes out of the pass **looking sourced**. Nothing
> flags it afterwards.

> 🔑 **THE REMEDY IS MOSTLY A RE-SEAT — 9 of 22.** The opposite of the caucus-only queue (mig 1920),
> where 60 of 60 defects blanked. The reason is structural: a repaired row HAS evidence and what
> failed is the MAPPING, so blanking would throw away good research. Disposition follows the
> held-row playbook (mig 1913): re-read against the Season 2 ladder, re-seat where a rung describes
> the evidence, blank only where no rung asks the question.

## The inserted-rung probe was run FIRST, and its limit matters

Eight rows carry a value that changed between seasons. Every topic's S1 and S2 ladders were compared
rung by rung: **no literal renumbering anywhere**, so those eight are deliberate moves, not artefacts.
⚠ **But the probe tests TEXT equality, and that is not enough.** `same-sex-marriage` had a
**semantic +1 insertion** the probe scored as "rewritten": S1 rung 1 ("require all states to
recognize … full federal benefits") is now S2 rung **2**, and S2 rung 1 is a genuinely new top rung
adding jobs-and-housing protection. The earlier passes remapped all six SSM rows correctly.
**A future probe needs a meaning test, not a string test.**

## 🔴 Four findings about the repair passes themselves

**1. A repair skipped a dedicated policy section on the page it was reading.** Kathy Tran's page

worked for "Campaign for a Family Friendly Economy, *an organization that advocates for* … affordable
healthcare". The page carries no statement of her own on healthcare at all. (R02)

**3. A season remap moved a chair onto a rung that NEGATES its own evidence.** Eunisses Hernandez
pushed LA to build a large-scale **social housing** programme. S1 rung 2 included "publicly fund new
housing" and fitted. S2 rung 3 reads "**Build no public housing**, but set binding rules". The remap
left her asserting the opposite of her own record. (R12)

**4. 🔴 THE SAME ROLL-CALL VOTE SEATS TWO DIFFERENT RUNGS.** Hernandez and Soto-Martinez both voted
against the LAPD raises package, both rows cite it, and the quoted argument — the money "would pull
money away from mental health clinicians, homeless outreach workers" — is rung 1 word for word.
Hernandez sits at **1** (R15, sound); Soto-Martinez sat at **2** and moves here (R25). **Two rows
citing one vote cannot both be right, and nothing in the corpus compares them.**
