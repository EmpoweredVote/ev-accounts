House Bill 1374
Feed-in tariff for renewable energy facilities.
Introduced House Bill (H)

Authored by: 

Rep. Matt Pierce. 
Digest

Requires the utility regulatory commission (IURC) to adopt rules to establish an electric utility feed-in tariff (FIT) program. Provides that the rules adopted must do the following: (1) Require all jurisdictional electric utilities (utilities) to offer a FIT to eligible customers (including persons that are not existing customers of the electric utility) not later than July 1, 2015.
