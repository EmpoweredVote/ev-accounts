IGA | House Bill 1393 (2025) Indiana General Assembly 2025 Session. House Bill 1393 Immigration notice. Enrolled House Bill (H) Authored by: Rep. Garrett Bascom. Co-Authored by: Rep. J.D. Prescott, Rep. Jim Lucas, Rep. Zach Payne. Sponsored by: Sen. Aaron Freeman, Sen. Scott Baldwin, Sen. Eric Koch, Sen. Randy Maxwell, Sen. Michael Young. Digest Provides that if a law enforcement officer arrests an individual for a felony or a misdemeanor and there is probable cause to believe that the individual is not lawfully present in the United States, the jail or detention facility shall notify the county sheriff of the probable cause during the individual's intake process, and the county sheriff shall notify the proper authority. Latest Bill Actions: H 04/30/2025 Public Law 121. H 04/30/2025 Signed by the Governor.
