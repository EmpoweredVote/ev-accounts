 About 

 Representative Ava Flanell proudly serves House District 14, representing families and communities in El Paso County. A Colorado Springs native, she graduated from a local charter high school and earned dual undergraduate degrees from Fordham University.

Rep. Flanell is a small business owner and firearms instructor who brings firsthand experience with the challenges facing entrepreneurs and working families. Her background in business and advocacy for individual freedoms informs her commitment to limited government and personal responsibility.

In the legislature, Rep. Flanell focuses on making Colorado safer and more affordable, defending constitutional rights, supporting parents and families, and holding government accountable. She works to ensure state policies reflect the values and priorities of the people she represents.
