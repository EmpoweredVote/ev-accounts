# Growth & development — every council member's own answer, side by side

Built for a single adjudication pass. The topic was seated inconsistently across six independent
agents (2, 3, 3, and three skips) off very similar language, so the point of this file is to decide
it **once, comparatively**, rather than per-agent.

The two chairs in contention:

    2 = Allow growth only where existing infrastructure can support it; slow approvals until
        capacity catches up
    3 = Plan proactively — invest in infrastructure ahead of growth to support responsible
        expansion

🔴 Chair 3's distinguishing content is **investing AHEAD of growth**. Chair 2's is **conditioning
growth on existing capacity**. "We should plan carefully and consider infrastructure" is what every
Colorado Springs politician says and separates neither.

⚠ Each excerpt below starts at the candidate's own answer. The reporter-written bio paragraph at the
top of each source page is excluded on purpose — it is not evidence.

Infill is an approach to develop unused or underutilized areas within a city. I would be an advocate for more thorough considerations of infill before annexations. Decisions related to our water should be grounded in science and data.
