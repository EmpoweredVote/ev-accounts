 About 

 Representative Scott Bottoms is a native of Colorado. He currently serves as the Lead Pastor at Church at Briargate since June 2012. Scott has over 30 years of ministry, most of that as a lead or senior pastor in various locations in Texas and Colorado. He has a passion for seeing souls come to know Christ and develop a personal relationship with Him.

 Rep. Bottoms earned his Bachelor of Arts from Southwestern Assemblies of God University, his Master of Arts from Denver Seminar, and his Doctor of Ministry from the Assemblies of God Theological Seminary. He received his ministerial license and ordination through the Assemblies of God.

 He is also a veteran of the U.S. Navy and serves as Colorado House Representative for District 15.

 Frequently Asked Questions 

 What year was Representative Bottoms first elected? 
 
 Representative Scott Bottoms was first elected to the Colorado House of Representatives on November 8, 2022 to serve House District 15.

 What area of Colorado does Representative Scott Bottoms represent? 
 
 Representative Scott Bottoms was elected to serve the people of El Paso County in House District 15.

 The communities included in House District 15 are Colorado Springs, Cimarron Hills, and Schriever Air Force Base.

 What did Representative Scott Bottoms study in school? 
 
 Rep. Bottoms received his undergraduate degree in Church Ministries from Southwestern Assemblies of God University. His Master of Arts is in Intercultural Leadership and his Doctor of Ministry degree is in Intercultural Spiritual Leadership from the same school.
