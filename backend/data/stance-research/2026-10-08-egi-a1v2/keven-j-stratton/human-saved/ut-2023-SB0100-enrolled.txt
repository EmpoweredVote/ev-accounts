Weiler 5 House Sponsor: Stephanie Gricius 6 7 LONG TITLE 8 General Description: 9 This bill enacts provisions ensuring a parent's access to information related to a parent's 10 child, including gender identity. 11 Highlighted Provisions: 12 This bill: 13 < defines terms; 14 < requires each school and each local governing board to ensure a parent's right to 15 access the education record of the parent's child; and 16 < prohibits a school or local education agency from prohibiting a parent's access to the 17 education record of the parent's child. 18 Money Appropriated in this Bill: 19 None 20 Other Special Clauses: 21 This bill provides a special effective date. 22 Utah Code Sections Affected: 23 ENACTS: 24 53E-9-205 , Utah Code Annotated 1953 25 26 Be it enacted by the Legislature of the state of Utah: 27 Section 1. Section 53E-9-205 is enacted to read: 28 53E-9-205. Parental right to student information.
S.B. 100 Enrolled Copy 29 (1) As used in this section: 30 (a) "Education record" means the same as that term is defined in Section 53E-9-204. 31 (b) "Gender identity" means the same as that term is defined in Section 34A-5-102. 32 (c) "Parent" means a parent or legal guardian with legal custody of the child in 33 question. 34 (d) "Sex" means the biological, physical condition of being male or female, determined 35 by an individual's genetics and anatomy at birth. 36 (2) In accordance with Section 53E-2-201, each school and each local governing board 37 shall ensure that no policy or action of the school or LEA: 38 (a) except as provided in Subsection 53E-9-203(6), operates to shield a student's 39 education record from the student's parent; and 40 (b) interferes with a parent's: 41 (i) fundamental parental right and primary responsibility to direct the education of the 42 parent's child; and 43 (ii) freedom of access to information regarding the parent's child. 44 (3) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a school or LEA may not: 45 (a) prohibit a parent of a child from accessing the child's education record; or 46 (b) without written parental consent make changes to a student's education record 47 regarding a student's gender identity that does not conform with the student's sex. 48 Section 2.
