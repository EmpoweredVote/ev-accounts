## Scored Topics (values under national scale, June 2026)
- **abortion:** 4 — Voted YES on HB467 (2023, closing abortion clinics). Voted for all major Utah abortion restriction bills.
- **religious-freedom:** 4 — Voted YES on SB0150 (2024, Utah RFRA equivalent, passed 27-0).
- **trans-athletes:** 4 — Voted YES on HB11 veto override (March 2022, 21-8 Senate).
- **fossil-fuels:** 4 — Sponsored SCR009 (2023) opposing ESG investing for fossil fuels/climate reasons.
- **voting-rights:** 4 — Voted YES on HB300 (2025, voter ID + phasing out auto mail voting, 19-10).
- **climate-change:** 4 — SCR009 sponsor, opposes climate-based ESG criteria; let market forces decide.
- **redistricting:** 5 — Co-sponsored HJR201 (Dec 2025 special session) asserting legislative authority over redistricting, opposing courts.
- **school-vouchers:** 4 — Voted YES on HB215 (2023, Utah Fits All scholarship, 20-8 Senate).
- **data-centers:** 2 — Voted YES on SB132 (2025), requiring large energy users to fund own power, protecting ratepayers.
- **immigration:** 4 — Voted YES on SB90 (2025, mandatory jail for criminal reentrants, 22-0).
- **deportation:** 4 — Voted YES on SB90 (2025).
- **taxes:** 4 — Sponsored SB69 (2024) income tax cut 4.65%→4.55%; quote: "we do not want to tax productivity."
