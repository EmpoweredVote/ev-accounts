# Calvin R. Musselman — Utah State Senator District 4

**Party:** Republican  
**District:** Senate District 4 (Weber/Davis Counties, West Haven area)  
**Prior service:** Utah House District 9 (approx. 2019–2024)  
**Senate term start:** January 1, 2025  
**politician_id:** 5ad69df3-3a03-45ed-9156-bcac82c93167

## Decisive Evidence by Topic

**abortion (value=4):** Spoke in March 2025 IVF debate (SB 242) contrasting IVF ("bring life into the world") with abortion ("intent to end that pregnancy") — supporting complete abortion restrictions with only rare exceptions. Utah's SB 174 (trigger ban, 2020) allows exceptions for rape/incest/life of mother — he supported that framework. No evidence of sponsoring a total ban; his words suggest alignment with existing Utah law rather than an absolute ban.

**trans-athletes (value=4):** Voted YES to override Gov. Cox's veto of HB 11 (Student Eligibility in Interscholastic Activities) on 3/25/2022; roll call at le.utah.gov confirms Musselman in the Yea column (56-18 vote). However, in 2026 he voted AGAINST HB 193 in Senate committee (1-5 against) which would have banned public employee insurance from covering transgender healthcare — calling it "reaching too far" and "dictating to everybody carte blanche." Nuanced: supports sports restrictions by biological sex but not blanket trans healthcare bans.

**voting-rights (value=4):** Utah Senate passed HB 300 (voter ID + phase out auto mail voting) 19-10 in March 2025. Musselman voted with Republican majority. Bill requires last 4 digits of ID on mail ballots (2026), full opt-in for mail voting (2029), photo ID for in-person (2029).

**school-vouchers (value=4):** Utah's HB 215 (Utah Fits All universal voucher scholarship) passed House 54-20 in January 2023; Musselman was a House Republican in the majority. No evidence of him opposing it. As Senate floor sponsor for SB 268 (First Home Investment Zones), showed alignment with market-based approach to housing and education.

**homelessness (value=4):** Senate sponsor of HB 266 (Veteran Housing Amendments, 2025) — agencies must collaborate on veteran housing. SB268 (2024) House sponsor (first home investment zones). Utah passed graduated enforcement anti-camping legislation (HB 465) with broad Republican support. Favors services paired with enforcement.

**taxes (value=4):** Cosponsor of HB 54 (2023, $400M tax cut, largest in Utah history). Senate supported HB 106 (2025, income tax cut 4.55%→4.5%, passed 23-6). Consistent tax-cutting record as both House and Senate member.

**immigration (value=4):** Sponsored SB 90 (2025) — mandatory jail sentences for criminal reentrant undocumented immigrants (90-360 days). Passed Senate 22-0. Initially voted against HB 226 (deportation enforcement) due to concerns about legal immigrants; changed vote after bill was narrowed. Not a mass-deportation absolutist but firmly supports mandatory enforcement for criminal undocumented immigrants.

**deportation (value=4):** Same SB 90 evidence. Opposed broadest deportation measures when they reached too far (legal immigrants with green cards); supported targeted deportation after criminal sentence.

**data-centers (value=3):** Voted for SB 132 (2025, electric utility amendments for large loads) which passed 22-1 in Senate. Bill requires impact assessments, data centers to pay for infrastructure, and Rocky Mountain Power gets 90 days to evaluate. Moderate protection for rate payers.

**healthcare (value=3):** Sponsored SB 242 (2025) expanding IVF coverage under PEHP (public employee health insurance). Acknowledged he normally votes against insurance mandates but made an exception for IVF. Suggests limited government healthcare approach with targeted exceptions.
