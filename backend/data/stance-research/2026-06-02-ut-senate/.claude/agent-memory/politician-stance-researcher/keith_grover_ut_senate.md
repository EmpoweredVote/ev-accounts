**politician_id:** dd75cf82-f0b2-4820-9356-28ccf0ee58f9

## Key Facts
- Served in Utah House 2007–2018 (District 61), NOT the Senate until Jan 2023
- Was NOT in the legislature during March 2022 HB11 transgender veto override — prior research was wrong on this
- iVoterGuide profile at https://ivoterguide.com/candidate/57813/race/16239/election/882 is a strong source
- Senate floor sponsor of HB261 (anti-DEI Equal Opportunity Initiatives, 2024) — confirmed via Deseret News + Senate press release
- Voted YES on HB215 (Utah Fits All school vouchers, 2023) in Senate Education Committee
- Voted YES on HB209 (2023 transgender athletes bill); said "I'm not too worried about refugees and the other kids" (SLTrib opinion 2023-02-15)
- Voted YES on SB332 (AI Policy Act extension, 2025)
- Co-sponsored HB15 (Medicaid Amendments, 2026) — addresses contingency if federal funds reduced
- Sponsored SB236 (2026, Property Tax Exemption Process Amendments)
