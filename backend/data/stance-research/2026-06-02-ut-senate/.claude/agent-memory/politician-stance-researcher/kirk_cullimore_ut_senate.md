Kirk A. Cullimore Jr. — Utah Senate District 19 (R-Sandy/Draper area), Senate Majority Leader since January 2025, attorney specializing in business/Indian law.

**Why:** Research completed 2026-06-03 for Empowered Vote compass research.
**How to apply:** Use this data to avoid re-researching; update if new session data changes positions.

## Topics Scored (13)
- abortion: 4 — voted YES on SB174 (2020 trigger ban) and HB467 (2023 abortion clinic closure)
- trans-athletes: 4 — supported HB11 debate, in 21-8 veto override majority; voted YES SB16 (2023 gender-affirming care ban for minors)
- voting-rights: 4 — voted YES HB300 (2025 in-person ID requirement), HB313 (2022 voter ID), sponsored SJR007 (state election authority)
- school-vouchers: 5 — Senate floor sponsor HB215 (Utah Fits All, 2023), universal ESA program; "ecstatic" over passage
- redistricting: 5 — worked to overturn voter-approved Prop 4 anti-gerrymandering law; argued for legislative supremacy over initiative
- campaign-finance: 3 — introduced resolution to give states authority to regulate campaign finance; framed as fighting dark money/foreign money with disclosure
- taxes: 4 — ALEC champion, supports annual income tax cuts, "we'll be looking at tax cuts again" (2025)
- data-centers: 4 — sponsored SB227 (2025) energy-agnostic data center electricity supply bill, streamlined permitting with ratepayer transparency
- ai-regulation: 3 — sponsored UAIPA (2024) requiring AI disclosure; revised in 2025 to narrow to high-risk interactions only (SB0332)
- homelessness: 4 — wants cities to "enforce their prohibition on unsanctioned camping," committed hundreds of millions to services
- childcare: 4 — voted NO on SB248 (2026 state-assisted childcare expansion)
- immigration: 4 — voted YES HB165 (2024) requiring federal notification before releasing undocumented immigrants in Utah
- misinformation: 4 — delayed/narrowed social media restrictions citing free speech; wants to "not trample on the free speech issues"
