## IMPORTANT NOTE ON VOTING-RIGHTS

The Freedom Index recorded Hinkins as YES on HB300 (Election Integrity). However, the ACTUAL le.utah.gov roll call vote for HB300 (2025, voteid=1901) shows Hinkins voted NO. He was in the 10-senator NO minority. The Freedom Index scoring appears to have been incorrect on this vote. Hinkins voted NO on HB300 because it was too restrictive (phasing out mail voting). However he DID vote YES on HB313 (2022 election security). His voting-rights stance is complicated — he supports voter ID but opposed the most restrictive version. Value 4 may be marginally appropriate but this is a nuanced case.
