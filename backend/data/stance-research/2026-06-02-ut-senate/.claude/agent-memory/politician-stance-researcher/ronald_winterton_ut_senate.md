Ronald M. Winterton, Republican, Utah State Senate District 20 (covers Duchesne, Uintah, Daggett, and parts of Summit/Wasatch counties — Uinta Basin oil and gas country). Elected 2018 as District 26, redistricted to District 20 in 2023. Chairs Senate Natural Resources, Agriculture, and Environment Committee. Serves on Transportation, Public Utilities, Energy, and Technology Committee.

## Key Stances Researched (2026-06-03)

- **abortion**: 4 — voted Yea on SB174 (2020) Utah trigger ban, nearly total ban with minimal exceptions
- **fossil-fuels**: 5 — strongest fossil fuel advocate; sponsored SB176 (2021) redirecting mineral revenue to Uinta Basin Railway (4x oil expansion); sponsored SB146 (2022) Oil, Gas, Mining amendments; former county commissioner who rushed $6M grant to railway while employed as its engineering consultant
- **climate-change**: 5 — sponsored SB126/SB23 "chemtrails" geoengineering criminalization bills (2025-26); no documented support for climate action
- **school-vouchers**: 5 — voted Yea on HB215 (2023), Utah Fits All $8,000 universal scholarship
- **religious-freedom**: 4 — voted Yea on SB150 RFRA (2024), passed unanimously
- **taxes**: 4 — voted Yea on HB106 (2025) income tax cut 4.55%→4.5%, 5th consecutive year of cuts
- **data-centers**: 2 — voted Yea on SB132 (2025), requires large-load energy users (100+ MW data centers) to self-fund power without passing costs to residential ratepayers
- **same-sex-marriage**: 5 — inferred from full record (conservative rural LDS district, culture-war voting pattern), no direct floor vote found
- **trans-athletes**: 4 — inferred from HB11 (2022) Senate vote pattern; bill passed 21-8 to override Cox veto; no documented dissent from Winterton
- **misinformation**: 4 — sponsored chemtrails bills based on conspiracy theory, skeptic of government-mandated content moderation
