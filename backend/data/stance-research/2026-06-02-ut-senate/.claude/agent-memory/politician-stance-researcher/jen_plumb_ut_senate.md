Jen Plumb, Utah State Senate District 9 (Salt Lake City/Emigration Canyon), Democrat, Senate Assistant Minority Whip (later Minority Whip). Pediatric emergency medicine physician and professor, University of Utah / Primary Children's Hospital. Single mother of a transgender child. First elected 2022.

**Why:** State senator with limited visibility on federal-only topics; very consistent progressive record on state issues.

**How to apply:** Look for state-level votes on le.utah.gov for reliable vote records. She is a reliable progressive Democratic vote — near 100% party alignment. KSL, KUER, Utah News Dispatch, and Salt Lake Tribune are the best sources for quotes.

## Decisive Evidence by Topic (2026-06-03)

- **abortion (1):** Nay on HB467 (2023); proposed amendment expanding mental-health exceptions; cited ectopic pregnancy death of rural patient. Sources: KSL, Deseret News.
- **same-sex-marriage (1):** Explicitly stated "Codifying gay marriage in Utah is something we obviously need to be doing" (2022). jenplumb.com, fox13now.com.
- **religious-freedom (2):** No direct votes; frames equity/dignity as paramount, consistent with limiting religious carve-outs from civil rights.
- **trans-athletes (1):** Nay on HJR301 (June 2024 special session) — Title IX transgender student protections; floor speech called it "salt in the wound." KSL, Herald Extra.
- **medicare/aid (2):** Warned against ACA subsidy expiration (Nov 2025); opposed HB178 stripping CHIP from immigrant children. KPCW, Standard-Examiner.
- **fossil-fuels (2):** Advocates stopping big business from polluting; protects air/water/watersheds. Not an immediate-ban position. jenplumb.com.
- **voting-rights (2):** Nay on SJR401 and SB4002 (Aug 2024 special session) — both restricting citizen ballot initiatives. myreptracker.com, fox13now.com.
- **climate-change (3):** Pro-environment, anti-pollution, but no bill sponsored calling for rapid fossil fuel phase-out; cast bipartisan HB3004 energy vote (2024).
- **civil-rights (2):** ERA, gender pay gap, ERA, LGBTQ+ anti-discrimination, criminal justice reform. jenplumb.com, utahsenatedemocrats.org.
- **redistricting (1):** Nay on SJR401 (Aug 2024) — consistently opposed GOP legislative power consolidation; favors independent processes.
- **school-vouchers (1):** Nay on HB215 (Jan 2023, vote record at le.utah.gov/DynaBill/svotes.jsp?sessionid=2023GS&voteid=105&house=S). All Senate Democrats voted Nay.
- **data-centers (2):** Caucus position: data centers must fund own power, not pass costs to residents; supports SB132-type requirements.
- **homelessness (2):** Chief sponsor SB121 (2025) — property loss/homelessness fund; concerned about homelessness facility becoming "prison or warehouse." psychologytoday.com.
- **childcare (2):** "Build on existing legislation to make childcare more affordable." jenplumb.com.
- **immigration (2):** Opposed HB178 (CHIP for immigrant children); Democrats opposed framing immigration as public safety issue. KUER.
- **deportation (2):** Nay on HB226 (2025 deportation-facilitation bill, passed 21-8). Democrats uniformly opposed; characterized as violation of Utah Compact.
- **healthcare (2):** ER pediatrician; "increase access to health care services"; "No one should have to go into debt." ACA defense Nov 2025. jenplumb.com, KPCW.
- **taxes (2):** "We don't see this as a time where less money available to support Utahns would be a good idea." Opposed income tax cut proposals (2024). Deseret News.
