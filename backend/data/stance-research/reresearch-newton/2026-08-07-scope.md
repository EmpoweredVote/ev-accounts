## The evidence base — what exists, tested this session

### ✅ 1. Fig City News committee reports — the real find

`figcitynews.com` publishes per-meeting committee reports that record **named attendance, per-item
vote tallies, and named dissent**. From the Zoning & Planning report of 2025-11-10:

> *"Reduce or remove parking minimums for commercial uses … **(7-1, Councilor Baker abstained)**"*
> *"Remove or reduce parking minimums … outside of BU1 and BU2 zoning **(8-0)**"*
> *"**Present:** Councilors Baker (Chair), Oliver, Albright, Wright, Kalis, Getz, Danberg, and
> Krintzman. **Also Present:** Councilor Malakie"*

This is the per-member evidence the cluster has always lacked, and it covers the **current** term, so
it cannot recreate a pre-tenure claim. **Six of the 18 owed appear in that single meeting** — Baker,
Oliver, Wright, Kalis, Krintzman, Malakie.
⚠ But note the tallies: 8-0 and 7-1. The near-unanimity problem follows us here. **Named abstentions
and dissents are the parts that actually distinguish anyone** (Baker abstaining twice, above).
