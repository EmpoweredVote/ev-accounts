# Wave 6 — Bend Metro Park & Recreation District (BPRD) board, research notes

Date: 2026-07-24. Topics in scope: `local-environment`, `growth-and-development`,
`climate-change`, `childcare`, `taxes`. Result: **4 stances across 3 of 5 directors.**

## Headline finding

The one place this board splits on the record — and therefore the only place individual
stances are recoverable — is the fight over **City of Bend property-tax and SDC exemptions
for affordable-housing projects**, which cost BPRD park revenue. On **Sept. 23, 2025** two
20-year exemption requests failed on identical 2-2 roll calls:

| | Cascade Landing (98 units) | Verde Pines (128 units) |
|---|---|---|
| In favor | Schoen (moved), Schneider | Schoen (moved), Schneider |
| Opposed | Hovekamp, Owens | Hovekamp, Owens |
| Absent | Schiffman | Schiffman |

Everything else in the 2026 board record is procedural or collectively attributed
("Directors discussed…", "the Board expressed general support"), which is not scoreable.

## Sources that fought back (and the workarounds)

- **`bendoregon.gov` 403s every non-browser request**, including the 2026-03-17 joint minutes
  PDF that carries the individual director attributions. Plain `curl` with a browser UA still
  returns 403. **Workaround that worked: `r.jina.ai/<url>` with an `x-no-cache: true` header.**
  Without that header r.jina.ai returns a cached snapshot with an *empty* body — a silent
  failure that looks like a 200. This URL is cited in the payload and is real, but an auditor
  re-fetching it will need the same header.
- **`bendparksandrec.org/about/board_meetings/` is JS-rendered**; r.jina.ai strips the
  document links entirely. WebFetch renders the table with URLs intact — use WebFetch for that
  page. The uploaded PDFs/DOCX themselves are directly `curl`-able (200, no UA needed), so
  `pdftotext -layout` is the fast path once you have the URLs.
- **Deschutes County meeting portal** (`/1506/…`, `deschutes.org/meetings`) is a CivicPlus
  shell wrapping a Municode portal — it renders no meeting rows to any text fetcher, so PC
  minutes cannot be enumerated that way. The minutes live at
  `mccmeetings.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/deschutes-pubu/MEET-Minutes-<guid>.pdf` (directly
  fetchable, 200) and are only discoverable via search-engine indexing of the blob domain.
  Playwright would be needed to walk the portal systematically.
- `bendbulletin.com` articles render via r.jina.ai but are paywall-truncated below the lede;
  enough for authorship and vote totals, not for extended quotes.
- **Deschutes County PC minutes carry a printed disclaimer** that they are "DERIVED FROM AN
  AUTOMATED TRANSCRIPTION SERVICE AND HAVE BEEN SUMMARIZED THROUGH AN AUTOMATED PROCESS."
  Nothing in them may be used as a verbatim quote — hence `quote_text: ""` on both Hovekamp
  rows despite strong stance evidence.
- The **May 2025 voters' pamphlet is two-column**, so `pdftotext -layout` interleaves adjacent
  candidates' text on every line. Extract **without** `-layout` to get reflowed, de-hyphenated
  single-line paragraphs before lifting any quote. Both quotes in this payload were
  string-matched against that extraction.

## Quote discipline

Two of four rows carry `quote_text`; both are verbatim single-line substrings of the May 2025
voters' pamphlet (self-authored, "This information furnished by …") and were programmatically
string-matched. The two Hovekamp rows have **empty** `quote_text` by design — every reference
to him in board and commission minutes is the clerk's indirect speech. Their
`quote_deidentified` fields hold indirect-speech renderings, clearly not presented as quotes.
