READING AID — written by Claude from the sources before the coders ran. It says what each source does. It does not suggest a rung; the judgment is yours.

This row has two sources: two proposed constitutional amendments with the same operative text. Neither got a floor vote.

- HCR 2017 (2024): "Introduced by Representatives De Los Santos: Austin, Contreras L, Contreras P, Gutierrez, Hernandez M, Ortiz." Ortiz was then a Representative.
- SCR 1024 (2025): "Introduced by Senators Kuby: Alston, Gabaldón, Ortiz; Representatives Garcia, Márquez." Ortiz is now a Senator.
- Both texts repeal Article XXX, section 1 of the Arizona Constitution ("relating to marriage") and add: "Marriage between two individuals shall not be prohibited or void on the basis of the sex, race, ethnicity or national origin of those individuals." Each would go to the voters. The texts do not quote the section they repeal.
