READING AID — written by Claude from the sources before the coders ran. It says what each source does. It does not suggest a rung; the judgment is yours.

This row has three sources about two 2025 measures. Neither got a committee hearing or a vote.

- HB 2621 overview and text: Márquez is the prime sponsor. It adds section 13-3123: "A person may not sell or transfer a firearm unless the person is a licensed firearms dealer, the purchaser or transferee is a licensed firearms dealer or a licensed firearms dealer facilitates the transfer." The dealer runs "a criminal background check on the transferee." The text lists exceptions (for example, law enforcement, gunsmiths, and loans at a shooting range) and a penalty.
- HCR 2034 text: Márquez is the prime sponsor. It would send a similar background-check law to the voters.
