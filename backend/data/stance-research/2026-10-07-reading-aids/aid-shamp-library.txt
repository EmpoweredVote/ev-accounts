This row has two sources about SB 1700 (2023). Shamp did not sponsor it (Wadsack is the prime sponsor).

- Senate vote: SHAMP Y (16-12). The House did not act on it.
- Text (long; most of it amends other school sections). The library parts:
  - A parent "who objects to a book that is available to students in the school library or that will be used for classroom instruction may request that the public educational institution remove the book from the library or classroom."
  - New section 15-249.01: the Department of Education keeps "a list of books that public educational institutions in this state are prohibited from using or making available to students." A parent "may submit a book ... if the parent finds the book to be lewd or sexual in nature, to promote gender fluidity or gender pronouns or to groom children into normalizing pedophilia." "If the department agrees with the parent's findings, the department shall add the book to the list."
  - School boards shall "exclude from schools" materials in those same categories.
- Struck words are removed from the old law.
