This row has five sources about two bills from 2023 with the same text.

- SB 1265 overview: Rogers is a co-sponsor (Kern is the prime sponsor). Passed both chambers; the Governor vetoed it.
- SB 1265 Senate vote: ROGERS Y (16-13).
- SB 1265 text (the House did not change it). It adds section 16-605: "the person who receives the highest number of legal votes shall be declared elected." The state "or a city, town, county or political subdivision" "may not use" a voting method that lets voters "rank ... more candidates than are eligible to be declared elected," or that tabulates ballots by "the elimination of candidates through multiple rounds of tabulation or the transfer or redistribution of votes."
- HB 2552 Senate vote: ROGERS Y (16-14).
- HB 2552 text: the same section 16-605, word for word.
