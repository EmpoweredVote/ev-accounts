This row has four sources about SB 1229 (2025), the "Arizona Starter Homes Act".

- Overview: Bolick is the prime sponsor. The Senate passed it 16-13 (amended); it was held in the House.
- Senate vote: BOLICK Y.
- Text (the version the Senate passed). For cities over 70,000 people, a municipality "may not interfere with a home buyer's right to choose the features, amenities, structure, floor plan and interior and exterior design of a home," and "may not adopt or enforce" rules setting, for example, minimum lot sizes over 3,000 square feet (in new single-family developments of five or more acres), certain minimum home sizes, lot-coverage limits and setbacks. Building codes, fire codes and health and safety rules still apply.
- Senate Republicans press release (March 5, 2025). It quotes Bolick by name, and also Petersen and others. Only Bolick's quoted words are hers.
