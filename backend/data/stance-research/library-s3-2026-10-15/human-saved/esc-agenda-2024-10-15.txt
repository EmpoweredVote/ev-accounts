 1.12 - PUBLIC PARTICIPATION AT BOARD MEETINGS PILOT PROJECT 
 The School Board recognizes the value to school governance of public comment on educational issues and the importance of allowing members of the public to express themselves on school matters of community interest. 
 The Board provides two opportunities for public input at its business meetings – Agenda Public Forum and Public Forum. Neither of these public participation opportunities is an open or unlimited forum. Agenda Public Forum is a limited public forum for the sole purpose of allowing public comment on agenda items. Public Forum is a limited public forum for the sole purpose of permitting citizens to address specific matters or concerns to the Board related to Board/School District business, governance, operations, or other matters related to public education. 
 Public participation and comment on agenda items shall be permitted as indicated on the order of business and before the Board takes official position on any action item under consideration.  The opportunity to be heard  on a particular agenda item  need not occur at the same meeting at which the Board takes official action on the proposition if the opportunity occurs at a meeting that is during the decision-making process and is within reasonable proximity in time before the meeting at which the Board takes the official action. This policy does not prohibit the Board from maintaining orderly conduct or proper decorum in a public meeting. 
 To maintain orderly conduct, the Public Forum segment of the meeting may not be used for presentation of political and/or campaign issues unrelated to education; announcing political candidacy and/or campaigning; or addressing matters involving disciplinary actions, pending employee disciplinary matters, pending claims, complaints, or litigation against the School Board, District or District personnel. 
 The opportunity to be heard is subject to policies adopted by the Board as follows: 
 A.  Guidelines regarding the amount of time an individual has to address the Board: 
  Each statement made by a member of the public shall be limited to three (3) minutes duration. Persons will be recognized in the order in which the requests were received. 
 An individual speaker may donate his/her time to another speaker so that an individual speaker may have a maximum of six (6) minutes. The individual donating time must complete a speaker form with all of the required information and clearly identify the individual to whom the time is donated. The individual donating time must be present at the time his/her speaker form is considered by the presiding officer. 
  No speaker may yield a portion of his/her time to any other person. 
 B.  Procedures for the allowing representatives of groups or factions on a proposition to address the Board: 
     Rather than all members of groups or factions for or against a proposition addressing the Board at meetings in which a large number of individuals wish to be heard, the Board encourages representatives of such groups or factions to address the proposition in their representative capacity. 
 C.   Procedures or forms for an individual to use in order to inform the Board of a desire to be heard: 
  Attendees must register their intention to participate in the public portion of the meeting upon their arrival at the meeting by completing a speaker's form at the entry of the meeting hall indicating his/her support, opposition, or neutrality on a proposition; and indicating his/her designation of a representative to speak for him/her or his/her group on a proposition if s/he so chooses. 
  An individual may speak at Agenda Public Forum and also at Public Forum; however, a separate form must be completed for each. 
  All forms must be submitted prior to the presiding officer’s call for Agenda Public Forum. Once the presiding officer calls for Agenda Public Forum no further speaker forms will be accepted. 
 D.   Designated period of time for public comment: 
  Agenda Public Forum - The Board will accept public input on agenda items at the conclusion of the Call to Order. This comment period is strictly limited to addressing items on the business agenda. 
  Public Forum – The Board will accept public comment on any matter related to Board/ School District business, governance, or operations once all business is concluded but prior to adjournment. 
 E.  The presiding officer of each Board meeting at which public participation is permitted shall administer the rules of the Board for its conduct, guided by the following rules: 
 Participants must be recognized by the presiding officer and will be requested to preface their comments by an announcement of their name and group affiliation, if and when appropriate. 
 All statements shall be directed to the presiding officer; no person may address or question Board members individually. Staff members shall not be expected to answer questions from the audience unless called upon by the Superintendent. 
 Personal attacks on Board members, District staff, or other members of the public will not be tolerated. 
 Tape or video recordings are permitted under the following conditions: 
  a.   No obstructions are created between the Board and the audience. 
  b.  No interviews are conducted in the meeting room while the Board is in session. 
  c.  No commentary, adjustment of equipment, or positioning of operators is made that would distract either the Board or members of the audience while the Board is in session. 
 F .   The presiding officer may: 
  interrupt, warn, or terminate a participant's statement when the statement is too lengthy, personally directed, abusive, obscene, or irrelevant; 
 request any individual to leave the meeting when that person does not observe reasonable decorum; 
 request the assistance of law enforcement officers in the removal of a disorderly person when that person's conduct interferes with the orderly progress of the meeting; 
 call for a recess or an adjournment to another time when the lack of public decorum so interferes with the orderly conduct of the meeting as to warrant such action. 
 No Board action shall be taken on requests made during public discussion unless the Board declares the matter to be an emergency in accordance with current law. Any other requests that the Board chooses to take action on shall be placed on an agenda for a subsequent meeting. 
 The requirement for public comment on action items does not apply to: 
    A.  an official act that must be taken to deal with an emergency situation affecting the public health, welfare, or safety, if compliance with the requirements would cause an unreasonable delay in the ability of the Board to act; 
    B.  an official act involving no more than a ministerial act, including, but not limited to, approval of minutes and ceremonial proclamations; 
    C.  a meeting that is exempt from F.S. 286.011 (the Public Meetings Law); or 
    D.  a meeting at which the Board is sitting in its quasi-judicial capacity. 
 These exceptions do not affect the right of a person to be heard as otherwise required by law or Board policy.

 P.2. To retain and sustain a viable, competent work force
 Due to anticipated plan design changes in the Medical Plans for the 2025 plan year, a competitive solicitation was conducted to improve coverages offered to employees with a potential higher deductible and out-of-pocket limits. The purpose of the solicitation was to evaluate if more current benefit plans and pricing exist that would benefit employees.    
 Multiple meetings were conducted by Aon, Inc. and results were reviewed. While the existing carrier, AFLAC, made improvements in their offering, a new insurance carrier, Cigna, provided a much more favorable proposal that included a 5-year rate guarantee and coverage which results in a much more valuable product with no change in premiums.   
 These products are all group plans, so employees and retirees with a current coverage with AFLAC will automatically be moved to the new group plan provider Cigna. This will be communicated with all participants with current coverage and to those that want to take out a new or a higher level of coverage to be enrolled at the upcoming Open Enrollment from October 30 – November 13, 2024. 
 We are recommending that the Board approve replacing the group voluntary plans currently offered by AFLAC with the same voluntary benefit plans listed above with Cigna, with an effective date of January 1, 2025.

 1.12 - PUBLIC PARTICIPATION AT BOARD MEETINGS PILOT PROJECT 
 The School Board recognizes the value to school governance of public comment on educational issues and the importance of allowing members of the public to express themselves on school matters of community interest. 
 The Board provides two opportunities for public input at its business meetings – Agenda Public Forum and Public Forum. Neither of these public participation opportunities is an open or unlimited forum. Agenda Public Forum is a limited public forum for the sole purpose of allowing public comment on agenda items. Public Forum is a limited public forum for the sole purpose of permitting citizens to address specific matters or concerns to the Board related to Board/School District business, governance, operations, or other matters related to public education. 
 Public participation and comment on agenda items shall be permitted as indicated on the order of business and before the Board takes official position on any action item under consideration.  The opportunity to be heard  on a particular agenda item  need not occur at the same meeting at which the Board takes official action on the proposition if the opportunity occurs at a meeting that is during the decision-making process and is within reasonable proximity in time before the meeting at which the Board takes the official action. This policy does not prohibit the Board from maintaining orderly conduct or proper decorum in a public meeting. 
 To maintain orderly conduct, the Public Forum segment of the meeting may not be used for presentation of political and/or campaign issues unrelated to education; announcing political candidacy and/or campaigning; or addressing matters involving disciplinary actions, pending employee disciplinary matters, pending claims, complaints, or litigation against the School Board, District or District personnel. 
 The opportunity to be heard is subject to policies adopted by the Board as follows: 
 A.  Guidelines regarding the amount of time an individual has to address the Board: 
  Each statement made by a member of the public shall be limited to three (3) minutes duration. Persons will be recognized in the order in which the requests were received. 
 An individual speaker may donate his/her time to another speaker so that an individual speaker may have a maximum of six (6) minutes. The individual donating time must complete a speaker form with all of the required information and clearly identify the individual to whom the time is donated. The individual donating time must be present at the time his/her speaker form is considered by the presiding officer. 
  No speaker may yield a portion of his/her time to any other person. 
 B.  Procedures for the allowing representatives of groups or factions on a proposition to address the Board: 
     Rather than all members of groups or factions for or against a proposition addressing the Board at meetings in which a large number of individuals wish to be heard, the Board encourages representatives of such groups or factions to address the proposition in their representative capacity. 
 C.   Procedures or forms for an individual to use in order to inform the Board of a desire to be heard: 
  Attendees must register their intention to participate in the public portion of the meeting upon their arrival at the meeting by completing a speaker's form at the entry of the meeting hall indicating his/her support, opposition, or neutrality on a proposition; and indicating his/her designation of a representative to speak for him/her or his/her group on a proposition if s/he so chooses. 
  An individual may speak at Agenda Public Forum and also at Public Forum; however, a separate form must be completed for each. 
  All forms must be submitted prior to the presiding officer’s call for Agenda Public Forum. Once the presiding officer calls for Agenda Public Forum no further speaker forms will be accepted. 
 D.   Designated period of time for public comment: 
  Agenda Public Forum - The Board will accept public input on agenda items at the conclusion of the Call to Order. This comment period is strictly limited to addressing items on the business agenda. 
  Public Forum – The Board will accept public comment on any matter related to Board/ School District business, governance, or operations once all business is concluded but prior to adjournment. 
 E.  The presiding officer of each Board meeting at which public participation is permitted shall administer the rules of the Board for its conduct, guided by the following rules: 
 Participants must be recognized by the presiding officer and will be requested to preface their comments by an announcement of their name and group affiliation, if and when appropriate. 
 All statements shall be directed to the presiding officer; no person may address or question Board members individually. Staff members shall not be expected to answer questions from the audience unless called upon by the Superintendent. 
 Personal attacks on Board members, District staff, or other members of the public will not be tolerated. 
 Tape or video recordings are permitted under the following conditions: 
  a.   No obstructions are created between the Board and the audience. 
  b.  No interviews are conducted in the meeting room while the Board is in session. 
  c.  No commentary, adjustment of equipment, or positioning of operators is made that would distract either the Board or members of the audience while the Board is in session. 
 F .   The presiding officer may: 
  interrupt, warn, or terminate a participant's statement when the statement is too lengthy, personally directed, abusive, obscene, or irrelevant; 
 request any individual to leave the meeting when that person does not observe reasonable decorum; 
 request the assistance of law enforcement officers in the removal of a disorderly person when that person's conduct interferes with the orderly progress of the meeting; 
 call for a recess or an adjournment to another time when the lack of public decorum so interferes with the orderly conduct of the meeting as to warrant such action. 
 No Board action shall be taken on requests made during public discussion unless the Board declares the matter to be an emergency in accordance with current law. Any other requests that the Board chooses to take action on shall be placed on an agenda for a subsequent meeting. 
 The requirement for public comment on action items does not apply to: 
    A.  an official act that must be taken to deal with an emergency situation affecting the public health, welfare, or safety, if compliance with the requirements would cause an unreasonable delay in the ability of the Board to act; 
    B.  an official act involving no more than a ministerial act, including, but not limited to, approval of minutes and ceremonial proclamations; 
    C.  a meeting that is exempt from F.S. 286.011 (the Public Meetings Law); or 
    D.  a meeting at which the Board is sitting in its quasi-judicial capacity. 
 These exceptions do not affect the right of a person to be heard as otherwise required by law or Board policy.
