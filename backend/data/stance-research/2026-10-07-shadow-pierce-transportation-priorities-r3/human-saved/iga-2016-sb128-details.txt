Senate Bill 128
Monroe County public transit funding.
Senate Bill (S)

Authored by: 

Sen. Mark Stoops, 
Sen. Luke Kenley. 

Sponsored by: 

Rep. Jerry Torr, 
Rep. Matt Pierce. 
Digest

Allows the fiscal body of Monroe County to adopt an ordinance to impose an additional local income tax rate to fund one or more county transit projects. Provides that the rate must be at least 0.1% but not more than 0.25%.
