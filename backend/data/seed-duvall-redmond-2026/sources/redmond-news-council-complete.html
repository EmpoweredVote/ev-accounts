Redmond Announces City Council Updates

News Releases Posted on January 21, 2026

REDMOND, WA – The City of Redmond today announced its completed City Council for 2026-2027, including new Council leadership. 

Through 2027, Redmond City Council will be led by President Melissa Stuart and Vice President Angie Nuevacamina. Stuart and Nuevacamina take over leadership positions previously held by Councilmembers Vanessa Kritzer and Jessica Forsythe, respectively. Council leadership is elected by Council in two-year terms.

“I congratulate President Stuart and Vice President Nuevacamina as they step into these important leadership roles on City Council,” said Mayor Angela Birney. “I’m also grateful to Councilmember Kritzer and Councilmember Forsythe for their thoughtfulness and dedication guiding Council the past four years. I look forward to continuing to work together with our entire Council group – including new Councilmembers Parsi, Prakriya, and Soni – on behalf of the Redmond community.” 

In November, voters elected Councilmembers Vivek Prakriya (Position #2) and Menka Soni (Position #6), both of whom are serving on City Council for the first time. The terms for Councilmembers Prakriya and Soni run through 2029.

A new Councilmember has also been selected to fill City Council Position #1, which was left vacant following the resignation of Councilmember Osman Salahuddin in November.

Following a thorough application and interview process over the past two months, Redmond resident Sayna Parsi was selected to fill the open seat in a 6-0 vote by current Councilmembers. A prior member of Redmond’s Parks, Trails, and Recreation Commission, Councilmember Parsi was officially sworn in at last night’s City Council meeting and begins serving immediately.

“The Council would like to express our gratitude for the more than 35 local leaders who participated in this process,” said Council President Melissa Stuart. “The candidates demonstrated an impressive commitment to service and a deep love of community. Councilmember Parsi’s deep community engagement, technical expertise, and prior city service have prepared her to be a great Councilmember. We are thrilled to have her on our team.”

Councilmember Parsi will complete the current Position #1 term, which runs through 2027.
