Five members, which is what Charter Section 8 prescribes ("five councilors elected from the city
at large"). Only "Mayor" and "Council President" carry a role label; the other three carry none.
Under Charter Section 10 the mayor is elected BY THE COUNCIL FROM ITS MEMBERS, so neither label is
a separately elected seat -- both are roles held by one of the five councilors.

Exactly two terms end 12/31/2026: John Miner's and Charlene Gothard's. Charter Section 9 staggers
the five seats three-and-two, and two is what this cycle elects.
