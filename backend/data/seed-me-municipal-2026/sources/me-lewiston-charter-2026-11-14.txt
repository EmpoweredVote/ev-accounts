Read for ONE question -- who elects the mayor (ROSTERS.md R9) -- because Lewiston is one of the
three Maine cities the R9 audit never covered. The answer is unambiguous:

  "The mayor shall be nominated and elected by the qualified voters of the entire city."

  "The mayor and the ward councilors shall be elected to serve two- year staggered terms."

So Lewiston's Mayor is a REAL, directly elected office and our schema is correct to hold one.

Lewiston ALSO has a rotating chair, and we correctly hold no office for it:

  "President of the council. The council shall elect for a one-year term, from among its members, a president who shall act as mayor during the temporary absence or temporary disability of the mayor, but the president shall have appointive powers only when the temporary absence or temporary disability of the mayor exceeds 45 consecutive calendar days."
