The Trust Project What is this? DULUTH — City councilors unanimously elected Terese Tomanek to their top leadership spot Monday. After offering thanks to her colleagues, she wasted little time in saying, “Now, let’s get to work.” ADVERTISEMENT As she handed off the role, outgoing President Roz Randorf acknowledged the commencement of a Minnesota Vikings playoff game that same evening and promised to hold her remarks in check so that no residents would miss the ill-fated loss of their home team in the ensuing contest. Duluth City Councilor Terese Tomanek speaks during a ribbon-cutting ceremony Aug. 6 for the opening of the Medical District East Parking Ramp. Brielle Bredsten / 2024 file / Duluth Media Group Nevertheless, Randorf briefly commented on each councilor’s contributions to the body in the past year. “This was a hard 2024 for us. We had a lot of things on our plate, and it can be a tough job,” she said. related St. Louis County Board elects first woman to chair in 2 decades Jan 7, 2025 Duluth School Board elects first new chairperson in 5 years Jan 6, 2025 Randorf said she will leave the council “in good hands,” as former Vice President Tomanek steps into the body’s leadership role. Randorf referred to Tomanek as “such a giver.” “Anyone who’s ill will get soup," Randorf said of Tomanek. "If you’re in need of support, you’re going to get kind words. If flowers come in, you know they’re coming from Councilor Tomanek. She’s always there." “She always says, ‘Yes.’ And she always leads with her heart,” Randorf said. ADVERTISEMENT Following a unanimous vote in support of Tomanek assuming the council presidency, Randorf presented the gavel to her successor. Tomanek was appointed to the council in June 2020 after cancer forced former at large Councilor Barb Russ to step down from the post. The following year, Tomanek was elected to continue in the same role until January 2026. also read Duluth council sets max tax levy increase at 3.6% 6d ago Duluth OKs tax subsidies for housing project near LSC Sep 17 Duluth bars new vacation rentals from single-family homes Sep 14 A retired chiropractor and former chaplain for Essentia Health-St. Mary’s Medical Center, Tomanek said she has learned much from her council president predecessors. As for Randorf in particular, Tomanek said she “has shown us all how to handle conflict and controversy with grace and perseverance." “She has led this council through a challenging year and poised us to continue her good work into 2025,” Tomanek said of Randorf. The new council president said she also was “delighted” to serve alongside newly elected Vice President Lynn Marie Nephew, referring to her as “a gold mine of information on all things housing.” Tomanek also thanked city administration and staff for providing elected officials with the information needed to make informed decisions.
