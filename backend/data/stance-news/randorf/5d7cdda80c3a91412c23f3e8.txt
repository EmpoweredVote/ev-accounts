Submit a Video or Picture Learning about the process of running for office By WDIO --> [anvplayer video="5175097&#8243; station="998130&#8243;] This year's Duluth Area ballot has several openings from the mayor's race, to the city council, and the school board. The Duluth Area Chamber of Commerce held a public forum to encourage more people from the community to "step up and serve." Former political candidates were able to answer about the process of running. The panel discussion was moderated by Chamber Vice President Daniel Fanning and panelists will includes Duluth City Council President Janet Kennedy, former Duluth City Councilor and Deputy St. Louis County Administrator Gary Eckenberg, Duluth City Council Vice President Roz Randorf, and former Duluth City Councilor Todd Fedora.
