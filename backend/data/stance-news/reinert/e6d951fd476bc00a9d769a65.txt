Submit a Video or Picture Duluth Mayor joins Great Lakes initiative board, cites opportunity for economic growth By WDIO --> Duluth Mayor Roger Reinert has been elected to the board of directors for the Great Lakes and St. Lawrence Cities Initiative, a binational coalition of more than 450 municipalities working to advance economic growth and environmental stewardship across the region. The organization represents communities along all five Great Lakes and the St. Lawrence Seaway, a major shipping corridor that connects the Midwest to global markets. Reinert said the group is focused on strengthening that corridor, which he described as historically central to economic activity in North America. “The Great Lakes–St. Lawrence Seaway was the economic backbone of North America,” Reinert said. He added that the system is currently underutilized, operating at about half of its capacity, which presents an opportunity for communities like Duluth to expand shipping and industrial activity. “In order to use that system more intentionally, there’s a lot of opportunity,” he said. The initiative focuses on three primary goals: promoting economic growth across the corridor, protecting freshwater resources, and fostering collaboration among member cities. Reinert said participating in the group allows Duluth to share ideas with other port cities while highlighting the region’s strengths, including its working waterfront, transportation infrastructure, and emerging tourism development. He also pointed to opportunities to expand international trade through the Port of Duluth-Superior, particularly by increasing container shipping and strengthening ties with manufacturing industries. City leaders say Reinert’s appointment positions Duluth to play a larger leadership role in regional economic planning and environmental protection efforts. Reinert said the work will help connect Duluth’s economy to the broader global market while ensuring long-term sustainability of the Great Lakes system.
