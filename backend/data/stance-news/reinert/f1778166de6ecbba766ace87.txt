Submit a Video or Picture High gas prices squeezing city, could effect levy By WDIO --> High gas prices squeezing city, could effect levy Local news, sports, weather presented by the WDIO News Team Duluth will set its maximum levy in September before adopting the 2027 budget in December, and it has a $5.5 million deficit to make up for. The levy is the amount local property taxes will increase to support the budget. While the final amount will be determined in December, Mayor Roger Reinert said he's never seen it be less than the maximum. Just like everywhere else, he said, Duluth is feeling the pain of inflation, but it's hit hard in other ways. For example, Reinert said the city, a heavy diesel consumer, is $400,000 over budget because of fuel. "Those prices are substantially higher than what we projected that they would be," he said. "And just for a reference point, four hundred thousand dollars is a roughly one percent increase in the city property tax levy. So, fuel alone could account for a one percent property tax increase." He added Duluth has a higher-than-average number of renters and a poorer-than-average population, so citizens sharply feel increases in property taxes. Reinert will discuss the maximum levy on Thursday evening during the finance committee meeting.
