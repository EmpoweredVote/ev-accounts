Peter Passi at the Duluth News Tribune reports: “The results of an independent investigation into the activities of Mayor Roger Reinert and Amber Gurske, a former campaign manager who became his significant other, were released Thursday morning. “The report refutes suggestions that Gurske may have overstepped her authority in helping to orchestrate the first-term mayor’s schedule and assist him with messaging.” Taylor Rivera at WCCO radio reports: “After 60 years, the Uptown Art Fair is no more.
