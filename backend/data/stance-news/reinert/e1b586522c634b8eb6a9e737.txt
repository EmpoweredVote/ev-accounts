Sign up Star Tribune reports Ramsey County has ended all work and future planning on the Riverview Corridor project between downtown St. Paul and the Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport. "During engagement sessions, issues were raised about potential construction impacts, safety and security concerns facing transit and communities overall, among other issues," the county said on the decision. Fox9 reports Minneapolis police shot a suspected active shooter inside an apartment building Saturday. Duluth News Tribune reports Mayor Roger Reinert will propose to the city council that property taxes will increase no more than 1.85% in 2025, though he acknowledged some property owners will see higher taxes due to increased property values. KSTP reports dozens of people across the Midwest, including some in Minnesota, are experiencing illness linked to a salmonella outbreak from eggs out of a Wisconsin farm. KTTC reports the Minnesota State Patrol has terminated trooper Shane Roper who is charged in the fatal crash of an Owatonna teen. CNN reports since Donald Trump claimed at a July rally in St. Cloud that he could turn Minnesota red, he and his running mate JD Vance have yet to return to the state and the campaign is focusing ads and funds outside of Minnesota. Via MPR: Two Minnesotans captured gold at the Paris Paralympic Games over the weekend. Eagan native Mallory Weggemann won a gold and silver medal in swimming while Waseca native Alex Shiflett-Patterson won gold in sitting volleyball. The Department of Natural Resources has released their fall color tracker.
