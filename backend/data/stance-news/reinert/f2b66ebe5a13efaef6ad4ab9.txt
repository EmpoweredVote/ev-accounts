WCCO reports in Hudson, along the St. Croix River, there is an increasing number of beached and abandoned boats along the shore. Via Star Tribune's Mike Scarcella : "Blue Cross Blue Shield insurers have a greed to pay $2.8 billion to resolve antitrust class action claims by hospital systems, physicians and other health providers alleging they were underpaid for reimbursements , the plaintiffs said in an Alabama federal court filing on Monday." KSTP reports Hennepin County Attorney Mary Moriarty has filed a petition to adjust the sentence of Jerome Nunn , convicted of a 1995 murder, from a life sentence in prison to time served.
