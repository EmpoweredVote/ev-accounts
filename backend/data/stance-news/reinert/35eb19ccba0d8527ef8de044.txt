Sign up Via Star Tribune : We finally know what Kathy Cargill was planing with Duluth’s Park Point. “She told the Wall Street Journal she was planning to beautify and modernize the neighborhood , but the pushback, including a message from Mayor Roger Reinert, has made her change her mind.” Bring Me the News is reporting hundreds of flights have been canceled at Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport due to the winter storm. WCCO reports one of the oldest gay bars in the country, the 19 Bar, is closed following a fire Friday. Emily Bauer at KSTP reports Minnesota PWHL Captain Kendall Coyne Schofield will be voicing a character in Disney and Pixar’s Inside Out 2. WDIO reports current and past students and faculty are fundraising to keep Northland College open after it was announced the college will likely close after this school year. The school needs $12 million by April 3 to remain open. MPR News has a story about Alice Lee, a 14-year-old from North Oaks who has claimed the Women’s American Cup title in chess, beating a 40-year-old grandmaster in the process.
