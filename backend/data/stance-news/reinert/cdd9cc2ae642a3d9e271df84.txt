Roger Reinert proposed today that a new Vikings stadium be built in Duluth. He's a DFLer from Duluth. And One with Britt Robson | Minnesota Timberwolves analysis and exlusive commentary from sports columnist Britt Robson. Delivered weekly. Sign up In an apparently serious proposal — which he has sent via letter to Gov. Mark Dayton — Reinert says there's a great site for a stadium in Duluth, "southwest of the downtown Duluth area on 500 acres of what was once the US Steel /Atlas Cement Works site." Sure it's kind of far from the Twin Cities, but that's no problem, he says: "[I]f last year’s Super Bowl champions, the Green Bay Packers, can attract ticket holders from over two hours away in Madison or Milwaukee, the Vikings can certainly do so as well," says a statement about the proposal. “Stationing the Vikings in Duluth is economically advantageous and will help further supplant the message that the Vikings represent all of Minnesota and are not exclusive to the metropolitan areas of Minneapolis and St. Paul," Reinert said. As for funding, he wants to pay the state's share with extra taxes generated by allowing liquor sales on Sundays. Reinert happens to be chief author of a Senate bill (SF 197) that would allow liquor stores to be open on Sundays, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas.
