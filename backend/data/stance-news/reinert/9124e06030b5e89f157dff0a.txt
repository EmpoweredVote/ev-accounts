Job Fair to be held Wednesday By WDIO --> (WDIO) Mayor Roger Reinert will proclaim Wednesday, September 3 as Workforce Development Day in Duluth, honoring September as Workforce Development Month. The proclamation will be made at the Duluth Workforce Development Job Fair at 1 p.m., followed by a Hiring Now! Job Fair at the Duluth Workforce Development Office, 402 West First Street in Duluth. Jobseekers will have the opportunity to meet with representatives from local and regional businesses and organizations. The public is welcome to attend. Businesses and organizations at the Job Fair include Duluth Public Schools ISD 709, Home Instead, Essentia Health, and more. WDIO will be at the event and will provide an update on Wednesday.
