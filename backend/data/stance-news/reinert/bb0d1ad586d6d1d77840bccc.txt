Submit a Video or Picture Duluth City Council approves preliminary property tax levy for 2027 By WDIO --> Duluth preliminary tax levy approved 2027 Local news, sports, weather presented by the WDIO News Team Earlier this afternoon, the Duluth City Council took action on local property taxes during tonight's meeting by approving the 2027 preliminary tax levy, while putting a hold on a separate item regarding leadership rules. Councilors unanimously voted 9 to 0 to approve resolutions setting the 2027 preliminary tax levies for both special parks and the Housing and Redevelopment Authority, also known as the HRA. This vote approves a preliminary property tax levy increase cap of 3.6 percent for 2027. That figure aligns with the maximum limit recommended by Mayor Roger Reinert earlier this month. City officials emphasized that the 3.6% figure acts strictly as a ceiling. This means overall property tax levies cannot increase above that limit, though final property tax rates for individual homeowners could end up lower. Across the region, local governments are setting preliminary caps ahead of mandatory state deadlines. For Duluth, the vote keeps the city on track to meet state requirements. Councilors face a September 30 deadline to submit these preliminary numbers to St. Louis County. After setting the cap, the council will hold public discussions throughout the fall before taking a final vote on the 2027 budget and property tax levy before the end of December. Meanwhile, councilors decided to table a scheduled discussion regarding a proposed Code of Conduct for city officials. The code of conduct is designed to establish clear behavior standards and ethical guidelines for elected leaders during public meetings and city business. Councilors placed a temporary hold on the topic so they can review the rules further at a future meeting.
