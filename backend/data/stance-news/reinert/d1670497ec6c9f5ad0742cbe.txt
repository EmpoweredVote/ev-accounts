This week, the council approved a list of projects they'd like to have funded when the Legislature convenes in January. According to the Duluth News Tribune , those projects include more than $10 million in bonding: • $3.3 million for a sanitary sewer overflow tank. • $2.4 million for maintenance and capital improvement projects to help bring the Lake Superior Zoo back to accreditation standards. • $4.9 million for construction of a new terminal at the Duluth International Airport. The paper asks: Will they get passed? Probably not, says Roger Reinert, the newly elected DFL representative from District 7B, but anything's possible. “I heard some things from my caucus that we want to get some money for projects to get people working or to keep people working,” he said. “There’s a desire to stimulate the economy.” The paper says Reinert hopes the Legislature will restore money lost from police and fire retirement aid, which has been the primary reason for the city’s budget crisis this year.
