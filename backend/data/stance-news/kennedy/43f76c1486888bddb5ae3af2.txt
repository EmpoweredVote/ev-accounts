Sign up Bob Timmons at the Star Tribune is reporting federal offices in Minnesota devoted to the Mississippi River recreation area and the state’s water quality are among five sites targeted for closure in the Trump administration’s massive cost-cutting campaign, according to a recently released list. Estelle Timar-Wilcox and Regina Medina at MPR News report dozens of people gathered outside a St. Louis Park aluminum finishing facility Wednesday night to protest recent federal immigration arrests at the company. Seven people were arrested at HardCoat Inc., according to advocacy organizations working with the families of the people arrested. Jill Burcum at the Star Tribune spoke with meteorologist Paul Douglas about the potential effects cuts to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration and the National Weather Service might have on forecasting and severe weather detection. Nick Longworth at FOX 9 writes a bill proposed by state Rep. Mike Freiberg would align Minnesota’s time in accordance with standard time provided by federal law. It would do away with the long-held daylight saving of "springing forward" one hour each spring, and "falling back" one hour each fall. Adam Uren at Bring Me the News reports WCCO Newstalk 830-AM is ending its local programming on weekday evenings , which has resulted in the cancellation of The Henry Lake Show. Katrina Bailey at the Minnesota Daily reports Dinkytown street construction in 2026 will focus on creating sidewalk ramps compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act. Dan Niepow at Twin Cities Business reports Caribou Coffee CEO John Butcher will step down. CFO Scott Kennedy will become interim CEO. Gordon Severson at KARE 11 has the story of how a high school robotics team lured Niron Magnetics to the city of Sartell. Kilat Fitzgerald at FOX 9 reports a Sun Country flight bound for Mazatlan after leaving Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport was diverted to El Paso, Texas, "in response to a security concern" Wednesday, according to a news release from the airline. The staff of Bring Me the News has compiled the best hockey hair from Day 1 of the Minnesota state tournament.
