Sign up Sahan Journal's Becky Dernbach and Elza Goffaux write the University of Minnesota Board of Regents will not divest from investments related to Israel. "Instead, the board’s resolution said it would adopt a “position of neutrality” regarding the university’s endowment, and it would consider future divestment requests in rare circumstances where “broad consensus” existed within the university community." KSTP reports Blue Earth County officials voted Tuesday to remove the Rapidan Dam and replace the County Road 9 bridge following flooding earlier this summer. Max Nesterak with Minnesota Reformer reports the state minimum wage will increase to $11.13 in 2025, "a 26% increase for workers currently at the lower wage floor." Trey Mewes with the Strib writes Rochester is proposing a 21% increase in spending next year that would have a significant impact on property owners. "Some of the levy will be offset by new property added onto the tax rolls, while property owners with increased valuations will shoulder more of the burden." Also from the Strib : Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan told Josie Albertson-Grove she would be willing to run for governor in 2026 should Gov. Tim Walz and Vice President Kamala Harris win this November. “If the people of Minnesota want me to continue to serve, I am absolutely open to that,” Flanagan said. Axios' Torey Van Oot explains why R obert F. Kennedy Jr. will still appear on Minnesota's ballot despite dropping out.
