Per Smith, "Russ was notable for masterminding a financial rescue plan for NYCHA — and for an unusually generous compensation deal that allowed him to commute regularly to his home in the Midwest while affording him an apartment in a luxury Tribeca high-rise." On Racket today, there's an excerpt from Aaron Shapiro's book on how Minnesota's Arrowhead region became a tourist destination.
