Submit a Video or Picture ICE law enforcement resolution on Duluth City Council agenda William Lien WDIO --> ICE law enforcement resolution on Duluth City Council agenda Duluth City Council is set to vote on several resolutions Monday. These include resolutions with immigration law enforcement. The Duluth City Council is set to vote on several resolutions Monday. These include a request for a temporary eviction moratorium and a measure on ICE law enforcement. One resolution aims to clarify the city's relationship with the federal government regarding civil immigration law enforcement. Duluth City Administrator Matt Staehling and Duluth residents discussed their concerns about law enforcement and immigration at a previous meeting. "I propose that we engage with our Citizens Review Board, Human Rights Commission, Interfaith Groups and other stakeholder groups. So we can engage in a series of good faith collaborative discussions to lay the foundation for community police engagement," Staehling said. Another proposed resolution, brought forward by Councilors Durrwachter and Clanaugh, asks Gov. Tim Walz to issue a temporary eviction moratorium due to concerns about ICE law enforcement. "If DPD truly does not assist in or intend to assist in immigration enforcement, you should have absolutely no problem passing a formal resolution that codifies that policy so we can hold the city accountable," One Duluth resident said. "I don't mean saying they will never, ever, ever, ever work with ICE." Other resolutions on the agenda include infrastructure projects, accepting reimbursement for ballistic vests and an agreement for an electric vehicle charging station. The Duluth City Council will address all these items during its scheduled meeting.
