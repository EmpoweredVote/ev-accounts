Submit a Video or Picture David Clanaugh joins the District 4 Duluth City Council race William Lien WDIO --> David Clanaugh joins the District 4 Duluth City Council race Lincoln Park resident, David Clanaugh is joining the District 4, Duluth City Council race and competing against incumbent Tara Swenson and competitor Brandon Parker. Tara Swenson the current sitting city councilor for Duluth's 4th district will be running against two competitors this election. David Clanaugh, a resident in Lincoln Park announced his candidacy on Tuesday May 20th. He says there are three core issues he wants to address if he becomes city councilor. "Well, definitely housing. The infrastructural pieces, starting with streets, but also just looking at infrastructure in general," Clanuagh said. "We're seeing some good things happening. Lead pipe replacement, for example, is huge progress in our community. Then again, the neighborhood revitalization." What makes David Clanaugh stand out as a candidate? He says based on his experience in human services, and being active in his community, he knows the problems residents are concerned about. "I can say that as somebody who's developed housing for Habitat for Humanity, they want to get permits," Clanaugh said. "Let's move these things along and figure out ways that we can be safe, we can be healthy, but we can also be creative in addressing the needs of the community."
