Submit a Video or Picture Folks at First Witness Child Advocacy Center happy to be moving into their new home By WDIO --> First Witness to move into new home this weekend An exciting moment for First Witness as they celebrate moving in to their new home. First Witness has a new home in time for the holidays. They'll be moving into 1402 E 2nd Street this weekend. The Duluth Chamber and others helped mark the occasion with a ribbon cutting on Tuesday afternoon. There was also a drumming circle. The space is much larger than their original location off Lake Avenue.
