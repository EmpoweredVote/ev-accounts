Submit a Video or Picture Man sentenced to 5 years in Twin Ports drug trafficking case By WDIO --> (US Air Force) Marcus Aaron Johnson of Minneapolis was sentenced to 64 months in prison for felony first degree possession of a controlled substance in St. Louis County. St. Louis County Attorney Kim Maki announced the sentencing, which was imposed by Judge Nicole Hopps and falls within the Minnesota Sentencing Guidelines range. During a plea hearing on July 24, 2026, Johnson admitted that he conspired to sell or possess cocaine and fentanyl between October 16, 2025 and January 6, 2026. Johnson is one of several defendants prosecuted as part of a multi-agency investigation into drug trafficking in the Twin Ports. The Lake Superior Violent Offender Task Force led the investigation, which included court-authorized wiretaps. RELATED STORY: Task force conducts downtown Duluth crime detail, makes 14 arrests Related prosecutions are still pending, and any defendant whose case has not been resolved is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. The St. Louis County Attorney’s Office stated it cannot comment on pending cases. Assistant St. Louis County Attorney Vicky Wanta prosecuted the case.
