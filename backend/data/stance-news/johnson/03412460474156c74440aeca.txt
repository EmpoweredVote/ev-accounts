Submit a Video or Picture Virginia Mayor Larry Cuffe Jr. announces he won't run for re-election, City Councilors file to run By WDIO --> Virginia Mayor Larry Cuffe Jr. announces he won't run for re-election, City Councilors file to run On Wednesday, current Virginia Mayor Larry Cuffe Jr. told WDIO News that he does not intend to run for re-election. Mayor Cuffe Jr. was first elected as Mayor in 2014. Acting City Councilors Annie Bachschneider and Steven B. Johnson have announced that they have both filed to run for Mayor. "As a lifelong resident, current City Councilor, and someone who deeply cares about this community, I’m ready to lead with fresh perspective, transparency, and a strong focus on our future," said Bachschneider in her post. Bachschneider was elected to Virginia City Council in November of 2024. "I intend to lead with confidence, commons sense, and more important, kindness," said Johnson in a post . Steven B. Johnson has served on the Virginia City Council since 2017. He previously ran for Mayor against Cuffe Jr. in 2022.
