Submit a Video or Picture UPDATE: Missing 16-year-old found safe By WDIO --> The Cloquet Police Department is seeking the public’s assistance in locating 16-year-old Johnathon Geno Johnson. UPDATE: After asking for the community's help to locate 16-year-old Johnathan Geno Johnson, the Cloquet Police Department says he has been found safe. The original story is below: Johnson left home on June 3rd and has not been back since. The family has been able to contact Johnson but has not received any information on his whereabouts. Police describe Johnson as an African/Native American male, about 6'3&#8243; and 190 LBS. If you have information on Johnson's whereabouts you can call 218-879-1247, email tips@cloquetmn.gov, or text 888777 starting your message with "TIP CLOQUETPD".
