If it is such a concern, then make the fees large enough to cover any issues. A government cannot make money, but they can charge fees to cover costs. That way the township can hire the people needed for a one-time event or whatever they imagine is going to happen. Whatever that is. It is incredible that they, who think they are incompetent to administer zoning, are going to decide the fate of the township for us.
