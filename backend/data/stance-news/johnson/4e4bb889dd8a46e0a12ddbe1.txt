Sign up Chloe Johnson at the Star Tribune reports St. Louis County Sheriff Gordon Ramsay said in a phone call that a ranger, who had experience and was close to retirement, was trying to rescue a father and his two sons whose boat had been pushed ashore on an island because of the high winds. Shortly after the ranger evacuated the family from the island, the Park Service boat they were in took on water and capsized. Morgan Reddekopp at KSTP reports Christopher Mark Covert , 27, faces one count of attempted first-degree murder of a peace officer, attempted first-degree assault against a peace officer, attempted second-degree assault and using or possessing metal-penetrating bullets while committing a crime. Covert seemingly plotted to kill police officers in Redwood Falls in late August. Elza Goffaux at Sahan Journal spoke with Haitian Minnesotans about Republican vice presidential candidate Sen. JD Vance spreading rumors about Haitian immigrants eating pets. Via the Associated Press: Former Brooklyn Center police officer Kim Potter, who served her sentence for killing Daunte Wright, is delivering presentations at law enforcement conferences. Katie Wright, Daunte’s mother, calls Potter’s actions an enraging scheme where her son’s killer would turn a profit from his death and dredge up painful memories in the process. Democratic vice presidential candidate Gov. Tim Walz was a guest on FOX News Sunday: Katrina Bailey at the Minnesota Daily reports Minneapolis has been ranked the second-best city in Minnesota to fish , according to an analysis by Fishing Booker Blog. Kyle Stokes at Axios reviews the “Dome Dog” from Oza's Bar & Grill in St. Paul to see how it compares to the original Metrodome snack.
