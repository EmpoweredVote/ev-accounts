Submit a Video or Picture Marjorie Johnson, Minnesota's &#8216;Blue Ribbon Baker' has died By WDIO --> Marjorie Johnson (KSTP) Minnesota’s famous "Blue Ribbon Baker," Marjorie Johnson, has died at the age of 106. Hubbard Broadcasting's 5 EYEWITNESS NEWS says the family confirmed the sad news on Thursday. Johnson, who won countless blue ribbons at county and state fairs, regularly visited 5 EYEWITNESS NEWS and Twin Cities Live at the Minnesota State Fair, highlighting her beloved recipes and cookbook. Despite her diminutive, sub-5-foot-tall frame, Johnson was a giant, becoming a darling not only in Minnesota but on many national television shows as well, thanks to her lively personality and passion for baking. For years she appeared on the Tonight Show with Jay Leno. According to her Wikipedia page, Marjorie Johnson won over 2,500 fair ribbons, including over 1,000 blue ribbons during her lifetime.
