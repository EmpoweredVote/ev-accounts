The Trust Project What is this? DULUTH — Jordan Johnson launched his campaign for an at large Duluth City Council seat with a public announcement at Dovetail Cafe and Market on Sunday afternoon. In a news release, Jordan wrote: “I have dedicated my career to working alongside community members to advance their health and wellness. I am running because we need trustworthy, accountable, and transparent leadership who are willing to stand up and fight for our communities. ADVERTISEMENT “As a young child, I lived through housing challenges, economic instability, and know how to live through uncertainty. I am invested in our community, our city, and our future generations. As an elected official, I will listen, show up, and do what is the best interest of us all.” Over 10 years, Johnson, now a consultant, has served in a variety of executive leadership roles. For four years, he worked as co-executive director of Life House, a local nonprofit committed to providing support for local youth at risk of becoming homeless. Johnson also taught in higher education and is an alumnus of a national Collaborative for Health Equity cohort. Two at large seats will be up for grabs in November, as the terms of Councilors Azrin Awal and Terese Tomanek both draw to a close. Neither of those incumbents has yet announced their campaign intentions. In his announcement Sunday, Johnson said: “I believe that by working together and investing in the people of our city, we create a path of prosperity and community safety where everyone is able to thrive.” Johnson is a 1990 Marshall School grad who attended the University of New Mexico, where he earned master’s degrees in both social and cultural anthropology and social work. He also went on to receive a doctorate in American studies from the same school. About the Duluth City Council The Duluth City Council’s regular meetings are on the second and fourth Mondays of each month at 6 p.m., with agenda meetings at 5:15 p.m. each Thursday preceding a regular meeting, unless otherwise scheduled, at City Hall, Room 330. Meetings are also livestreamed and recorded for later viewing at duluthmn.gov/city-council/city-council-meetings-events/council-meeting-media.
