Submit a Video or Picture Changes at MnDOT District 1 following Duane Hill's retirement By WDIO --> Duane Hill (L) and Andy Johnson (R). MnDOT Duane Hill, District Engineer for the Minnesota Department of Transportation’s District 1 office in Duluth, will retire in August after 35 years of service. Hill has led District 1 since 2012, overseeing major projects like the Highway 53 Rukavina Bridge in Virginia, the Twin Ports Interchange in Duluth and the Blatnik Bridge replacement. He also contributed to safety projects and the Northeast Toward Zero Deaths program. Hill started at MnDOT in 1991 as a graduate engineer in Duluth and later worked in Bemidji before returning to Duluth for leadership roles. He earned his civil engineering degree from North Dakota State University. Andy Johnson has been named the new district engineer for District 1 and began the role on July 15. Johnson will oversee program development, construction, maintenance and operations across the district. Johnson has 28 years of engineering experience, joined MnDOT in 2003 and has spent the last 19 years in District 1 working in program delivery and operations. For more information on MnDOT District 1 visit mndot.gov/d1 .
