Sign up If you've been to the Minnesota State Fair, perhaps you've seen a ribbon next to one of Marjorie Johnson's baked goods. The frequent competitor will be remembered for her sweet treats. KARE 11 reports " Johnson, renowned throughout Minnesota for her award-winning baked goods at the Minnesota State Fair, has died at 106 , according to her family. &#8230; Born in Minneapolis in 1919, the woman warmly known as the &#8216;Blue Ribbon Baker' didn't enter baking contests until her 50s — but once she did, she never stopped ." State lawmakers held a hearing Thursday to learn about the ongoing impacts of the federal government shutdown . KSTP writes "many programs and people impacted by the shutdown testified in front of state lawmakers Thursday morning. &#8230; Some young parents who testified said they’ve been forced into a difficult position. They said they’re still paying for childcare even though they’ve gone nearly a month without a full paycheck ." A "significant reduction" in staff hours has meant changes for the Grand Rapids Area Library . KAXE reports "budget cuts are forcing a nearly 30% reduction in staff hours. While the children's library will see the biggest reduction, everything the Grand Rapids Area Library does will be affected. &#8230; The [library’s] budget will be 22% smaller next year, based on the latest numbers from the city ." At Valleyfair, one fan is coasting into a record that will be hard to beat . Bring Me The News writes "think you've been to Valleyfair a lot? &#8216;Renegade Mike' would like a word. &#8230; Valleyfair has announced that the &#8216;super fan' took his 30,000th ride on Renegade, the park's wooden rollercoaster, on Oct. 17 ."
