Sign up Briana Bierschbach and Brooks Johnson at the Star Tribune are reporting a landlord for one of MyPillow's manufacturing facilities has asked a judge to evict the Minnesota-based pillow giant from the premises , saying the company has failed to pay more than $200,000 in rent over the past two months. Kirsti Marohn at MPR News reports a proposal to build a homeless shelter with small, indoor units in St. Cloud is moving forward after a contentious public hearing Monday night. Rochelle Olson at the Star Tribune reports on Monday Minnesota House Republicans and faith community members unsuccessfully sought a religious exemption for gender identity in the state Human Rights Act. Via WCCO News: 21-year-old Dakoda Peplinski faces charges after he allegedly rifled through two dozen cars in the Minneapolis Police Department’s forensic garage and stole evidence. Brooks Johnson at the Star Tribune is reporting enough veterans and military service members have agreed to a $6 billion settlement over allegedly defective earplugs made by a 3M subsidiary for the deal to move forward. Kyle Potter at the Thrifty Traveler explains the “basic economy” price war waging between Delta Air Lines and Sun Country Airlines at Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport. Eder Campuzano at the Star Tribune is tracking the local response to the Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse in Baltimore and how it’s recalling the 2007 Interstate 35W collapse in Minneapolis . Via the Associated Press: The Wisconsin Supreme Court on Tuesday let stand a lower court ruling that declared some delivery drivers for Amazon were employees as the state argued, not independent contractors as the online retail giant contended.
