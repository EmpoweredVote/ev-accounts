One of the reasons for the alley “nonnavigable” status had more to do with the fact that two of the days fell next to major holidays- Christmas and New Years. The trucks sailed by in a rush to get home, given their monopoly status, customer service is low on the totem pole of priorities. There could have been a better plan than they came up with-say two choices per region of the cities, not unchallenged monopolies. Bad mistake.
