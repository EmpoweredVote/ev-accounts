Submit a Video or Picture Tomanek, Johnson capture at-large council seats Alex Sapp WDIO --> The Duluth City Council will see some new faces in 2026, and one returning. Unofficial results in the two at-large races show Terese Tomanek and Jordon Johnson winning the seats. The two will begin their terms in January . Terese Tomanek, the only incumbent in the at-large race, has won another term, after first being appointed to the council back in 2020. During 2025, she’s served as the Council President. A Woodland resident, Tomanek is a retired chiropractor and chaplin. During her campaign, she touted experience, and her connections within the city government. Jordon Johnson works as an executive director at the American Indian Community Housing Organization (AICHO). A resident of Downtown, Johnson describes himself as a community leader – a description that will be especially fitting once he takes his place on the council. During his campaign he prioritized housing and bringing economic opportunities to Duluth. Unofficial results from all races can be found here.
