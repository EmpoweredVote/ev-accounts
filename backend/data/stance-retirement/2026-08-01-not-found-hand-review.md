### The three bugs, each reproduced on a real row before being fixed

**1. 🔴 ONE STRAY `[` DELETED 83% OF A PAGE.** `norm()` is applied to the haystack as well as the
needle, and its bracketed-edit strip (`/\[[^\]]*\]/g`, meant for `advocate[s]`) was unbounded. On
`sendnomoney.org` a `[` at offset 5,517 paired with a `]` at 52,511 and removed **47,177 of 56,732
characters**. John Vail's row was scored NOT_FOUND on two quotes — *"Target luxury of all sorts."*
and *"a percentage wad of the whole taken every year."* — **that are on that page verbatim**, and the
previous hand-check recorded it in the backlog as a genuine failure. Blast radius was **measured, not
assumed**: re-running all 557 rows moves exactly one verdict, and six sampled Ballotpedia pages are
unaffected because their brackets are balanced citation markers.

**2. 🔴 A QUOTED CHAIR LABEL IS NOT A QUOTATION FROM THE SOURCE.** Rows routinely name the chair they
picked in quotation marks — Guithues's healthcare row ends `matching value 1 ("Make healthcare free
and available to everyone, paid for and run by the public sector")`. That string is **our** answer
text; no campaign site contains it. **9 rows, every one of them in QUOTE_ABSENT — 41% of the bucket.**
This is the same error as the `>3-hyphen` chair-label rule in `candidateTerms` one level up: that rule
drops chair labels from TERMS and nothing had ever dropped them from QUOTES.

**3. 🔴 THE CAMPAIGN-FINANCE DISCLAIMER LIVES OUTSIDE `<main>`.** `pageText` returns `main`'s text
when a `<main>` exists, so everything after `</main>` vanishes regardless of markup. Sawant's row
quotes *"Paid for by Kshama For Congress, not corporate cash"* — verbatim on her homepage, invisible
to the extractor. Of all compass topics, Campaign Finance is the one whose best evidence is routinely
a disclaimer rather than a plank. ⚠ **The first fix selected `footer,nav,header` and still missed it**
(her disclaimer is a plain `<p>` in a GenerateBlocks `<div>`) — the fix reproducing the bug it was
written to fix. `chromeText` is now the *complement* of what `pageText` keeps.

`ww19.neighbors4faye.com` (a domain-parking service); `https://` serves a JS bounce with a JWT

🔴 **A citation pointing at a parked ad/malware domain is worse on a live profile than a bare
homepage** — a voter who clicks it lands on a download prompt. This is more urgent than anything else
in this file, and it is **not** a retirement: it is the `DEAD_SITE` class, whose remedy is Wayback
after a human read. Same handling as Faizah Malik.

⚠ **It also means the crawl status of a site is not a stable property.** This domain read as a live
campaign site in the 19:49 run and as parked hours later. Any cohort file older than a day should have
its DEAD/parked classifications re-checked rather than carried forward.
