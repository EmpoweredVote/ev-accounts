## Key Confirmed Bills (89th Session)
- HB3 (ESA/school vouchers): Coauthors: Button, Hopper, Orr (signed 2/26/25). Ramos and Johnson NOT coauthors.
- HB44 / SB31 (abortion exceptions): Joint House sponsors: Johnson, Orr (among others). Signed 6/20/25.
- HB1806 (prohibit govt support for abortion travel): Coauthors include Hopper, Orr. Anti-abortion.
- HB5510 (expand Heartbeat Act, abortion pill trafficking): Coauthors include Hopper, Orr. Anti-abortion.
- HB5337 (proof of citizenship to vote): Coauthor: Orr. NOT Hopper, Button, Ramos, Johnson.
- HB3491 (election irregularities bill): Coauthor: Hopper. Anti-fraud conservative elections bill.
- HB1982 (limits on federal agents in Texas): Primary author: Hopper. Limits federal arrest authority.
- HB2657 (abolish Texas Education Agency): Primary author: Hopper. In committee.
- HB1613 (campaign contributions in special sessions): Author: Johnson. Campaign finance restriction bill.
- HB4124 (swipe fees on electronic payments): Coauthor: Button. Commercial/financial services.
