## Notable Pattern
Seyarto is a mainstream California Republican with consistent conservative votes. He abstains (NVR) more often than voting yes on politically sensitive Democratic bills, particularly on LGBTQ and housing issues. His NVR on ACA 5 (same-sex marriage) is the clearest example. He voted YES on some consensus/bipartisan bills (SB 1112 childcare, SB 773 CalWORKs homeless assistance) which passed unanimously.
