# Kathy Kimberlin — Fremont City Council District 3

**Role:** Appointed September 10, 2024 (replaced Jenny Kassan who resigned for husband's mayoral campaign). Term through November 2026.
**District:** District 3 (Centerville area) — NOTE: prompt said D4 but Tri-City Voice and appointment article consistently say D3.
**Party:** Democrat
**Background:** 45-year Fremont resident; Fremont Field Director for Alameda County Supervisor David Haubert; former FUSD employee 15+ years; President of Fremont Education Foundation, Fremont Rotary Club, Centerville Business & Community Association; co-founded Tri-City Nonprofit Coalition (2020); Chamber of Commerce Government Affairs Committee. Endorsed by CA AG Rob Bonta and Rep. Eric Swalwell.
