### Partisan Pattern
- All major abortion, immigration, trans rights, civil rights, voting restriction bills: R co-authors only, D voted against
- Property tax relief (SB 3 88R, SB 4 88R, SB 1 873 special): bipartisan with Menéndez and Hinojosa as co-authors
- Menéndez and Hinojosa are moderate TX Democrats — not progressive on all issues
- Hinojosa represents border district; ACLU award winner; NOW Legislator of Year; Vietnam vet
- Menéndez: son of immigrants, San Antonio; authored David's Law (cyberbullying); secured $17M Alamo Colleges
