## Notable Positions

- Abortion: Life-of-mother exception only; no rape/incest exceptions; opposed public funding; co-sponsored NE L.B. 675 (ultrasound display bill)
- Climate: Signed No Climate Tax Pledge; aide confirmed she believes climate change is from natural cycles not emissions; LCV=7%
- Healthcare: Calls for repeal and replace ACA with "free market reforms and competition"
- Social Security: Supports raising retirement age, means testing, limits benefits — "don't change for those over 40"; ARA rating=10%
- Childcare: Bipartisan Child Care Modernization Act (2025); Paid Family Leave Tax Credit — targeted tax credits approach
- Religious freedom: Supports voluntary prayer in public schools; welfare funding to faith-based orgs
- Tariffs: Supported USMCA (2020); voted to kill Ex-Im Bank (2015); more free-market oriented, not protectionist
- Voting: March 2026 resolution "Only U.S. Citizens Should Vote in U.S. Elections"; opposed Jan 6 commission
- Criminal justice: Mixed — supported First Step Act type language ("reduce recidivism & mass incarceration," 2018) but also supports death penalty and ending parole for violent repeat offenders
- Campaign finance: Limits PAC/corporate donations but not individual; cosponsored Stop Insider Trading Act (2026)
