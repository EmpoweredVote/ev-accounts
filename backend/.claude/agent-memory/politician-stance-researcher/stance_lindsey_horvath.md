## Background

- LA County Board of Supervisors, District 3 (West Hollywood, San Fernando Valley, Westside) — since December 5, 2022
- Former Mayor of West Hollywood (2015–16, 2020–21); served on WeHo City Council 2009–2022
- First millennial and only renter on the all-women Board
- Was Republican until 2003; Democrat since 2003
- Education: BA from University of Notre Dame
