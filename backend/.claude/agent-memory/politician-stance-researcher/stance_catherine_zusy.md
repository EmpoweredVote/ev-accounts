# Catherine (Cathie) Zusy — Cambridge MA City Councillor

**Office:** Cambridge City Council (at-large)
**Background:** Former museum curator; 30-year Cambridge resident in Cambridgeport; known as the "Magazine Beach Lady" for leading park revitalization; founded "If This House Could Talk..." community history initiative; President of Cambridgeport Neighborhood Association
**Entry to council:** Appointed Sept 2024 (sworn Sept 23) to fill Joan Pickett's vacancy; re-elected November 2025
**Ideological position:** Most neighborhood-protective / anti-rapid-development member of current council; endorsed by Cambridge Citizens Coalition and Cambridge Bicycle Safety; NOT endorsed by A Better Cambridge (pro-YIMBY), Our Revolution Cambridge, or Cambridge Residents Alliance
**Key quote:** "The city should reserve its money for increasingly tighter times."
