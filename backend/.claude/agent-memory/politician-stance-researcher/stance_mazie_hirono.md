### Profile

First immigrant woman elected to Congress; first U.S. senator born in Japan; member of Senate Armed Services, Judiciary, and Veterans Affairs committees. Consistently progressive across all topics — one of the most liberal Democrats in the Senate. Notable for her 2017 confrontation of Kavanaugh and her public fight with kidney cancer while maintaining Senate attendance record.
