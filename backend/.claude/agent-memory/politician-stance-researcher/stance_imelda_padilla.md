# Imelda Padilla — LA City Council District 6

**Party:** Democrat  
**Took office:** July 5, 2023 (won runoff against Marisa Alcaraz)  
**Background:** First-generation Mexican-American, community organizer; worked at LAANE (LA Alliance for a New Economy), Pacoima Beautiful (environmental justice), founded Adelante Latino Youth Summit. Former Field Deputy for CD6 under Nury Martinez.  
**Committees:** Government Operations (Chair), Planning and Land Use Management, Public Works, CREAID (Civil Rights, Equity, Aging, Immigration, Disability), Energy and Environment, 2028 Olympics Ad Hoc. Appointed to Metro Board of Directors, Feb 2025 (first Latina from East Valley on Metro Board).
