## Tim Flaherty — Cambridge City Councillor

**Office:** Cambridge, MA City Council (at-large)
**First elected:** November 2023
**Re-elected:** November 2025
**Background:** 30-year trial lawyer (criminal defense + white-collar), former prosecutor, adjunct professor at BC Carroll School 1994-2016. North Cambridge native.
**Criminal record note:** Pleaded guilty to a reduced state charge (originally federal witness-tampering) in 2015-2016; sentenced to one year probation.

### Political Profile
- Moderate-to-conservative by Cambridge standards
- NOT endorsed by A Better Cambridge (YIMBY) or Cambridge Citizens Coalition in 2025
- NOT endorsed by Our Revolution Cambridge in 2025
- NOT endorsed by Cambridge Bicycle Safety
- One of four 2025 candidates who pledged to repeal the February 2025 citywide four-plus-two upzoning
- Two-time failed state Senate candidate before winning council seat
