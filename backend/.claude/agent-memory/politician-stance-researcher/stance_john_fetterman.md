John Fetterman, US Sen PA (D, elected 2022, took office Jan 2023). Notable as one of the most rightward-shifted Democrats in the Senate since his 2022 stroke. 16 topics scored 2026-05-21.

## Stances

- abortion=1 (absolutist pro-choice, "between a woman and her physician," no restrictions incl. 3rd trimester)
- civil-rights=2 (officiated SSM as mayor 2013; LGBTQ rights; wage gap support)
- climate-change=3 (called it "existential threat" but co-sponsored Cornyn LNG export bill 2026; fracking evolved from "abomination" to "supports with stricter regs")
- deportation=3 (YEA Laken Riley Act cloture, vote 1, Jan 9 2025; called for border "reset")
- fossil-fuels=3 (co-sponsored LNG export protection bill with Cornyn May 2026; fracking shift)
- healthcare=1 (self-identified M4A supporter; "fundamental human need and right")
- immigration=3 (YEA Laken Riley cloture; called for bipartisan border "reset"; opposes abolishing ICE)
- medicare/aid=2 (supports M4A broadly but open to ACA expansion; NAY on RFK Jr. as HHS)
- misinformation=2 (co-sponsored Kids Off Social Media Act 2025)
- same-sex-marriage=1 (officiated marriages as Braddock mayor 2013; strong LGBTQ ally; wasn't in Senate for RespMA vote)
- social-security=2 (no privatization support; progressive safety net record)

- taxes=2 (supports wealth tax on yacht-owners; backed $15 min wage)
