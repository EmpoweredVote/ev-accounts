## Profile
**José Cisneros** — San Francisco Treasurer and Tax Collector (D), appointed by Mayor Gavin Newsom in 2004, elected 2005. One of SF's longest-serving city officials. Known for Bank On San Francisco, Kindergarten to College (K2C), Financial Justice Project, and socially responsible banking/investment policies.

Board membership: Alice B. Toklas LGBT Democratic Club. National roles: Vice Chair, President's Advisory Council on Financial Capability for Young Americans; Co-Chair, Cities for Financial Empowerment Coalition.
