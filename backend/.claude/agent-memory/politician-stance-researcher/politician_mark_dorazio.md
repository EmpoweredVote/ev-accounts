## Mark Dorazio — TX House HD-122 (Republican)

**Politician ID:** 207cb8e4-14f2-49e1-8786-9533dc9b9d31
**Office:** Texas House of Representatives, District 122 (San Antonio area)
**Party:** Republican
**Session:** 89th Legislature (2025)
**Committee assignments:** Homeland Security, Public Safety & Veterans' Affairs; Subcommittee on Defense & Veterans' Affairs (Vice Chair); Human Services

### Background
- Born Jan 4, 1957, Derry PA
- Construction business owner
- Won 2022 primary runoff defeating Elisa Chan; won general 56%
- Assumed office Jan 10, 2023

### Key Legislative Actions (89R)
- Co-author: HB3 (school vouchers/ESA) — 02/26/2025
- Co-author: HB8 (ICE 287(g) mandate for large-county sheriffs)
- Co-author: HB5580 (same ICE mandate — House version, Spiller primary author)
- Co-author: HB9 (raises business property tax exemption to $250K)
- Co-author: HB30 (removes emergency disaster tax rate increases)
- Co-author: HB12 (state agency sunset/efficiency audit reform)
