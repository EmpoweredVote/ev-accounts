## Politicians

**Aimee Winder Newton** — Republican, District 3, on council since 2014, ran for Gov 2020, 2026 Council Chair, also Sr. Adviser to Gov Spencer Cox. Chairs WFRC Budget Committee and Trans Com Vice-Chair.

**Carlos A. Moreno** — Republican, District 2 (West Valley, Kearns, West Jordan, South Jordan, Herriman). First Latino elected to SLC County. Venezuelan political asylee, freshman (sworn Jan 2025).

**Dea Theodore** — Republican, District 6 (Cottonwood Heights, Draper, Midvale, Sandy, Cottonwood Canyons). On council since 2021, 2025 Council Chair, on Criminal Justice Advisory Council, Unified Fire Authority, Redevelopment Agency Board.

## Key Votes

**Feb 2025 — 0.2% sales tax (7-2 YES)**
- Yes: Winder Newton, Theodore (+ 5 others)
- No: Moreno, Sheldon Stewart
- Funds: 50% state transit, 25% city transport, 25% county ($19M for 248 jail beds + recidivism programs)
- Winder Newton quote: "As we look at the growth in the county and the Olympics coming in 2034, this will be critical."
- Theodore quote: "A sales tax like this, yeah, it's burdensome, and I apologize, but again, I want a safe community and we are at a point where we have no other choice."

**Dec 2025 — 14.65% property tax increase (8-1 YES)**
- Yes: Winder Newton, Theodore (+ 6 others)
- No: Moreno (ONLY dissenting vote)
- Moreno: argued for budget cuts, didn't propose specific cuts during negotiations
- Winder Newton to Moreno: "work horses, not show horses"

**Aug 2024 — $507M jail bond ballot measure (8-1 YES to put on ballot)**
- All three supported putting it on ballot; voters rejected it ~48-52 in Nov 2024
