## Carrie Isaac — TX House District 73 (New Braunfels)
- Party: Republican, **Texas Freedom Caucus member**
- Member code: A4265 (TLO)
- Assumed office: January 2023; lost 2020 District 45 race to Erin Zwiener 49.5% vs 50.5%; won 2022 District 73 with 70.4%
- Background: First-gen college grad; BS Stephen F. Austin; MS Purdue Global; executive director Digital Education & Work Initiative of Texas
- Personal: Co-founded Hays County Republican Women; IRONMAN triathlete
