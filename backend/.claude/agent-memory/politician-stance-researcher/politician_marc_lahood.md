## Marc LaHood — TX House HD-121 (Republican)

**Politician ID:** 786ac925-59d3-40e3-a9ce-b63a54f4caf4
**Office:** Texas House of Representatives, District 121 (San Antonio)
**Party:** Republican
**Session:** 89th Legislature (2025)
**Committee assignments:** Judiciary & Civil Jurisprudence; Subcommittee on Family & Fiduciary Relationships; Transportation

### Background
