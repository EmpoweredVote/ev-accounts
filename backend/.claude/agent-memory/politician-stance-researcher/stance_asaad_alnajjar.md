# Asaad Alnajjar — 2026 LA Mayor Candidate

**Research date:** 2026-05-16
**Politician ID:** f05ec762-9053-45a0-be1a-a4b173a06015

## Background
- Civil structural engineer, 35+ years with City of Los Angeles, engineering manager
- Leads design/construction of 35 citywide light rail and rapid bus systems including LAX people mover
- Chairs Porter Ranch Neighborhood Council Safety and Transit Committee (since 2005)
- Previously ran for City Council District 12
- Arab-American, describes himself as "a non-politician civil servant engineer immigrant"
- Platform: "Engineering a Stronger Los Angeles"

## Notable Policy Details
- **General Order 40 / Special Order 40**: Proposes canceling on day one — LA's 45-year sanctuary policy banning LAPD from checking immigration status. Notable rightward departure for an LA Mayor candidate.
- **TPS pathway**: Pairs SO40 cancellation with 90-day plan to legalize undocumented residents through existing TPS programs — not mass deportation.
- **Convention Center**: Opposes $2.62B expansion; says it creates $89M annual shortfall and won't break even until 2058; frames as developer-priority vanity project.
- **"Unity through Diversity"**: Explicitly protects LGBTQ+ rights, immigrant rights, religious houses, and youth populations — no discrimination on any basis.
- **Infrastructure expertise**: Only engineer in the race; his policy lens is infrastructure-first for most issues.
- **90-day audit**: Core governance commitment across multiple areas (homelessness spending, city budget).
