## Politician
Mai Vang — Sacramento City Council District 8 (Meadowview/South Sacramento), Democrat, elected 2020, re-elected 2024. First Hmong-American elected to Sacramento City Council. Former SCUSD school board member (2016-2020). Running for CA-7 Congress (2026) against Doris Matsui. Endorsed by Working Families Party, National Nurses United, Justice Democrats.
