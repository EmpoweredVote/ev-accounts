## Mano DeAyala — TX House HD-133 (Republican)

**Politician ID:** 6121f45c-3508-43a7-a36e-871901303d73
**Office:** Texas House of Representatives, District 133 (Houston suburban)
**Party:** Republican
**Session:** 89th Legislature (2025)
**Committee assignments:** Appropriations; Appropriations S/C on Articles I, IV & V (Vice Chair); Culture, Recreation & Tourism

### Background
- Cuban-American; born Dallas TX; parents from Cuba
- UT Austin (BBA McCombs); SMU (JD)
- Partner at Houston law firm Buck Keenan LLP (commercial disputes)
- Won 2022 general 61.4%, 2024 general unopposed (100%)
