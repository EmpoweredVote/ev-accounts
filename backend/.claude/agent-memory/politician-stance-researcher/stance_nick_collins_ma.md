Nick Collins — MA State Senator, 1st Suffolk District (South Boston, South End). Born 1982, South Boston. Chairs Joint Committee on State Administration and Regulatory Oversight. More moderate Democrat; sometimes breaks with progressive caucus. Voted against original police reform bill (30-7 Senate vote) before supporting compromise. Blocked Boston property tax proposal (2024).
