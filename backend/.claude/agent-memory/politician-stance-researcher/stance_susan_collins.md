Susan Collins, US Senator from Maine (R, since 1997). One of the most moderate Republicans in the Senate. LCV lifetime score ~60% (highest among incumbent Senate Republicans).

**Scores (2026-05-20):**
- abortion=3 (pro-choice, co-drafted Reproductive Choice Act to codify Roe/Casey; opposed Women Health Protection Act as exceeding Roe)
- ai-regulation=3 (no strong specific position; moderate approach supports basic safety standards)
- campaign-finance=2 (backed Honest Leadership/Open Government Act; supports strict limits on corporate donations and dark money)
- childcare=3 (targeted tax credits and subsidies; not universal public childcare)
- civil-rights=2 (co-sponsored anti-discrimination bills, backed LGBTQ protections; withdrew from Equality Act co-sponsorship)
- climate-change=3 (LCV ~60%; works to reduce GHG emissions, backs clean energy jobs; no rapid fossil fuel phase-out)
- deportation=3 (voted against DREAM Act 2010, supported 2013 comprehensive reform; opposed Trump border emergency)
- fossil-fuels=3 (voted for ANWR drilling in TCJA after failing to remove it; bipartisan offshore drilling + renewables group)
- healthcare=3 (voted against ACA but decisive vote blocking repeal 2017; supports insulin cap $35, rural healthcare)
- homelessness=3 (addiction/mental health funding approach; co-sponsored Medicaid Addiction Recovery Expansion Act)
- housing=3 (co-sponsored anti-discrimination housing bills; voted NAY on hedge fund housing amendment 2025)
- immigration=3 (co-sponsored DREAMER pathways; supported 2013 reform; rejected Trump travel ban; opposed border emergency)
- judicial-criminal-justice=3 (supported First Step Act; backs rehabilitation and sentencing reform)
- judicial-interpretation=3 (moderate originalism emphasizing stability/precedent; confirmed nominees across spectrum)
- medicare/aid=3 (co-sponsored Medicaid Addiction Recovery Expansion; insulin cap; opposed ACA repeal)
- misinformation=3 (youth online safety; voluntary standards; not heavy regulation)
- redistricting=2 (supports independent commissions with equal party representation)
- religious-freedom=3 (backs religious exemptions but also strong LGBTQ anti-discrimination protections)
- same-sex-marriage=2 (YEA on Respect for Marriage Act Nov 2022; opposed Federal Marriage Amendment 2004)
- school-vouchers=3 (maintains public school funding; allows means-tested vouchers with accountability)
- social-security=3 (championed Social Security Fairness Act signed into law; small adjustments not privatization)
- tariffs=3 (selective tariffs; warned against trade wars; voted with Dems to undo Canada tariffs April 2025)
- taxes=3 (voted for 2017 TCJA with deficit concerns; 2025 voted to prevent ultra-wealthy tax cuts; close-loopholes approach)
- trans-athletes=4 (signed 2025 petition barring trans girls from school sports; voted to defund schools allowing trans athletic participation 2021)
- ukraine-support=2 (co-authored 2024 bipartisan national security supplemental; criticized Trump approach to Zelenskyy 2025)
- voting-rights=3 (opposed filibuster changes; backed bipartisan election security; standardized voter ID with free IDs)
