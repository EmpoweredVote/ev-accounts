## Patricia M. Nolan — Cambridge MA City Councillor

**Background:** Elected to Cambridge City Council Nov 2023 (first council term after 14 years on School Committee). Harvard College undergraduate; Yale SOM master's. Progressive bloc but with independent votes — notably sided with conservative bloc on Garden Street transportation reversion (April 2025). Finance Committee chair. [[stance_sumbul_siddiqui]]

### Character Profile
Progressive-leaning but pragmatic moderate on transportation and development process. Voted with the conservative bloc (Toner, Zusy, Simmons) on Garden Street transportation reversion despite having "run for council promising safer streets." Sided with the progressive bloc (Siddiqui, Sobrinho-Wheeler) on surveillance tech. Strong on environment/trees and housing supply. More process-focused than ideological on zoning — wanted to delay upzoning for procedural clarity but ultimately championed it enthusiastically.
