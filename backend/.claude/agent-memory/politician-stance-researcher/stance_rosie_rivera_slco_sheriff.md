## Rosie Rivera — Salt Lake County Sheriff

**Party:** Democrat | **Office:** Salt Lake County Sheriff since Aug 2017 (first female sheriff in Utah; first Latina sheriff) | **Background:** 29-year law enforcement career

### Scores (local topics, 2026-05-30)

- **public-safety-approach = 3** — Supports reform + enforcement balance: MAT in jail, pre-trial release, crisis intervention training, community policing. Led split from Unified Police Department (took effect July 1, 2024) to create a new Law Enforcement Bureau for unincorporated SL County. Championed $507M bond (failed Nov 2024), then backed 2025 $19M sales tax. Quotes: "The Salt Lake County jail is the largest mental health provider in the state"
- **jail-capacity = 3** — Supports both bed expansion AND diversion: backed $507M bond for 800+ beds, then 2025 sales tax for 248 beds at Oxbow Jail. Also co-designed crisis receiving center and Justice & Accountability Center. Wrote post-audit: "we will work with our criminal justice partners on interim solutions to ease capacity pressures and reduce reliance on overcrowd releases." Jail at same capacity since 2001 despite +300k population.
- **local-immigration = 3** — Explicitly stated "We are not a sanctuary jail"; cooperates with ICE detainer requests (up to 10 detainees). Created public "ICE Dashboard" for transparency. Deputies do NOT ask immigration status during public encounters. No 287(g) formal agreement. Expressed concern in Feb 2026 about ICE courthouse arrests without notice. Middle path: honors detainers, promotes transparency, but no proactive immigration enforcement.
- **homelessness-response = 3** — Co-leads county 5-yr Action Plan; jail serves as de facto mental health system; championed Receiving Center as jail diversion for SUD/MH crises; brought MAT to jail; led DEA Task Force expansion. Pairs enforcement accountability with diversion/reentry housing.

### Skipped topics
All other topics (housing, zoning, transportation, environment, economic-development, growth, city-sanitation, residential-zoning): no direct record found — she runs the jail/law enforcement, not county policy on these issues.
