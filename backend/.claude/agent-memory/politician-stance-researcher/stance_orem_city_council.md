## Jeff Lambson (City Council, began 2nd term Jan 2024)
- Endorsed by Stronger Orem PAC (pro-development bloc)
- Endorsed by FOP and Professional Firefighters
- housing=3, growth-and-development=3, residential-zoning=3, transportation=3, public-safety=4, economic-dev=3
- Voted YES on State Street apartment settlement (2024, 4-3) and Wilkerson Farm rezoning (May 2025, 4-3)
- Recommended BRT on State Street (transport advisory), then backed 2023 TMP that removed BRT — property-rights-deferential moderate
- Supporter of CARE Tax (arts/recreation/parks)
- standfororem.org/jeff-lambson is a working source; orem.gov/jeff-lambsons-hometown-perspective/ works

## Jenn Gale (City Council, 1st term began Jan 2024)
- Endorsed by Stronger Orem PAC
- housing=3, growth-and-development=3, residential-zoning=3, transportation=3, public-safety=3, economic-dev=3
- Voted YES on Wilkerson Farm rezoning (May 2025, 4-3)
- Supports higher-density near UVU with appropriate buffers; opposes high-density on residential neighborhoods
- Campaign platform: Trust, Listen, Collaborate; emphasizes process/collaboration over ideology
- standfororem.org/jenn-gale (login-protected); use heraldextra.com coverage as primary source

## LaNae Millett (City Council, re-elected 2025, term through 2029)
- Endorsed by FOP + Firefighters + Utah County Republican Party + Utah Taxpayers Association
- housing=4, residential-zoning=4, growth-and-development=4, transportation=4, public-safety=4, economic-dev=4, homelessness-response=4, local-environment=3, rent-regulation=4
- Voted NO on State Street apartment settlement (2024) and NO on Wilkerson Farm rezoning (May 2025)
- Eliminated State Street Master Plan (would have allowed 10,000 apartments)
- Leads Heart of Orem downtown arts district project (2024-2025)
- Serves on Utah Lake Commission, planted 300 trees at Hillcrest Park 2024
- votelanae.com works; heraldextra.com State of the City March 2025 article is best source

## Orem Political Blocs
- Stronger Orem PAC: Lambson, Gale, Killpack — pro-development, property-rights-deferential
- Stand For Orem PAC: Muhlestein, McKell, Rands — anti-density, single-family preservationist
- LaNae Millett aligns with Stand For Orem ideology but ran independently

## Notes
- Utah state law (Utah Code 57-20-1) prohibits cities from enacting rent control — rent-regulation topic should always be skipped or scored 4-5 for UT city council members
- Orem is 95% built out per multiple candidates; development debates focus on infill/density rather than sprawl
- Local immigration: No Orem-specific 287(g) agreement found; Utah County Sheriff has one; Riverton PD is only city with one
- Transport: 2023 Transportation Master Plan passed unanimously removing BRT/light rail references
