## Scored Topics (15)

- **taxes=4** — Constitutional amendment to cap income tax (S.12, 194th); multiple income tax reduction bills across sessions; co-sponsored income tax cuts
- **immigration=4** — S.1272 restricts emergency shelter to citizens + lawful immigrants with 12-month MA residency; S.1776 empowers ICE transfer for convicted criminals
- **deportation=4** — Focus on criminal immigrants; S.1776 enables ICE transfers for felons; S.1272 strips shelter access for undocumented
- **local-immigration=4** — S.1776 proactively empowers law enforcement to cooperate with ICE (not just court-ordered); criminal-conviction threshold
- **healthcare=3** — Healthcare insurance premium payment pool for working families (S.908 and prior versions); maintains private insurance system; no public option or M4A
- **climate-change=3** — Natural lands carbon sequestration (S.669); renewable energy tech (S.2321); clean energy bill (S.2834); but no emergency framing, no 2030 deadlines
- **fossil-fuels=3** — Supports clean energy alternatives; no drilling expansion bills; no moratorium on permits
- **redistricting=2** — Independent redistricting commission bills (S.324 in multiple sessions); hybrid model: Governor/AG/SoS appoint 3 experts + each legislative caucus leader nominates 3 candidates (one selected per caucus)
- **housing=4** — Streamlined permitting (S.1025 and 7+ prior versions); Housing Priority Zones without zoning variances; MBTA alternative compliance; no rent caps or public funding support
- **voting-rights=4** — Voter ID bills (S.320, S.339) requiring ID to receive ballot, excluding social security cards; not a co-sponsor of Voting Rights Restoration
- **campaign-finance=3** — Campaign finance task force (SD.2647); S.507 improving ballot committee reporting; disclosure-oriented approach
- **homelessness=4** — S.1272 caps shelter at 6 months, requires citizenship/lawful status proof + 12-month MA residency, criminal history screening; Inspector General oversight
- **civil-rights=3** — No reparations or racial equity mandates; operates within existing civil rights framework; no progressive civil rights bill co-sponsorships
- **school-vouchers=3** — Filed school choice bills (S.324, S.333); engaged with charter school reform; no universal vouchers
- **jail-capacity=4** — Pretrial detention expansion bills for dangerous persons (S.1270, S.1271); DNA database expansion; no bail reform, no prison moratorium co-sponsorship
