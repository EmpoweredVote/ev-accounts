## Kenneth Mejia — LA City Controller

**Office:** City Controller of Los Angeles (20th Controller, since December 12, 2022)
**Party:** Independent (left Democratic Party January 2024; previously Democrat then Green Party 2017-2018; DSA member)
**Background:** CPA, Filipino American activist. First Filipino American elected official in LA, first Asian American elected to citywide office, youngest ever Controller. Co-founded We Can Make a Difference (2016). LA Tenants Union member. Inspired by Bernie Sanders 2016.
