## Anna Caballero — CA State Senate SD-12 (Democrat, Central Valley)

**Office:** California State Senate, District 12 (formerly SD-14 pre-redistricting), Merced/Fresno/Salinas area. In May 2025 she launched a campaign for 2026 California State Treasurer.

**Background:** Former Salinas Mayor, former Assembly Member (AD-28, AD-30), former CA State & Consumer Services Agency Secretary under Gov. Jerry Brown. Attorney who co-founded low-cost legal services firm 1982. First represented farm workers and unions.

**District character:** Central Valley — heavy agricultural economy, large Latino immigrant population (70%+), rural hospital closures a major issue. Her legislation consistently reflects these regional priorities.
