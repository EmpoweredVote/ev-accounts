## Connie Chan — SF District 1 Supervisor

**Background:** Democrat, first elected 2020, re-elected 2024. Hong Kong-born, arrived in SF Chinatown as teenager. Currently running for Congress (Nancy Pelosi's seat) in 2026.
**Role:** Budget Committee Chair (since Feb 2023). Member, SF County Transportation Authority. Member, Free City College Oversight Committee.
**Bloc:** Progressive (aligned with Peskin bloc), anti-upzoning.
