# Cecilia Lunaparra — Berkeley City Council District 7

**Background:** First elected April 2024 in special election; first Latina and first openly queer woman of color on Berkeley City Council; daughter of Mexican immigrants; graduated UC Berkeley with Urban Studies degree May 2024 (first undergraduate elected to Berkeley Council). Labor attorney and tenant rights advocate. Endorsed by DSA and progressive labor groups. Committees: Facilities, Infrastructure, Transportation, Environment & Sustainability; Land Use, Housing & Economic Development; 4x4 Joint Task Force Committee on Housing; City/UC/Student Relations Committee. External boards: Ava Community Energy Authority Joint Powers Board; League of California Cities, East Bay Division. Prior roles: Chair of Environment and Climate Commission; Zoning Adjustments Board commissioner.
