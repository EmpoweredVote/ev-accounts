## Political profile
- Centrist/moderate Democrat, heir to Levi Strauss fortune, philanthropist/nonprofit founder

- Endorsed by Republican Briones Society, former mayor/police chief Frank Jordan, SF Chronicle
- Campaign focused on: public safety, homelessness, fentanyl, housing supply, government accountability
