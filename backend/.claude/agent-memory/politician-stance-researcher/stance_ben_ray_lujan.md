Ben Ray Luján, US Senator from New Mexico (D, since Jan 2021, former DCCC chair). All 26 federal topics scored 2026-05-20.

## Scores
- abortion=1 (100% NARAL, Women Health Protection Act cosponsor, supports public funding)
- ai-regulation=3 (Senate Commerce subcommittee chair on tech/broadband, platform accountability, basic safety standards)
- campaign-finance=1 (no corporate lobbyist money, voter vouchers, full disclosure, Democracy For All Amendment)
- childcare=2 (ARP childcare subsidies, expanded CDCTC)
- civil-rights=2 (Equality Act, VAWA, ENDA, LGBTQ strongest advocates)
- climate-change=2 (highest-ranking Green New Deal cosponsor, IRA, net-zero power sector legislation)
- deportation=2 (protects DACA, TPS, farm workers, asylum seekers; opposes mass deportation)
- fossil-fuels=2 (voted to ban offshore drilling Gulf of Mexico, shift tax breaks to renewables, Green New Deal)
- healthcare=2 (Medicare for All cosponsor but also public option; ACA defender)
- homelessness=2 (service-based solutions, decriminalize non-violent poverty offenses)
- housing=2 (ARP + IRA affordable housing provisions, rental assistance)
- immigration=2 (DREAMers/TPS citizenship pathways, opposes Muslim ban, comprehensive reform)
- judicial-criminal-justice=2 (George Floyd Act, end for-profit prisons/cash bail/mandatory minimums, First Step Act)
- judicial-interpretation=1 (judicially liberal, voted to remove Trump, Jan. 6 Commission)
- medicare/aid=2 (Medicare for All cosponsor, lower eligibility age, expand Medicaid, 100% ARA)
- misinformation=2 (platform accountability, mandated transparency requirements, net neutrality)
- redistricting=2 (For the People Act, Freedom to Vote Act - independent commissions with equal bipartisan representation)
- religious-freedom=3 (A from Freethought Equality + tribal sacred sites; balance religious/civil rights)
- same-sex-marriage=1 (voted Respect for Marriage Act, Equality Act, full federal protections)
- school-vouchers=1 (explicitly opposes private/religious voucher programs; supports fully funded public schools)
- social-security=1 (100% ARA, "Privatization means gutting the promise our seniors earned" Jul 2025, anti-privatization)
- tariffs=3 (opposes Trump tariffs as attacks on working families, backed USMCA, selective targeted tariffs only)
- taxes=2 (voted against 2017 TCJA, 30% minimum for $1M+ earners, keep inheritance tax)
- trans-athletes=1 (Equality Act covers gender identity, no restrictions on trans athletes)
- ukraine-support=2 (voted for Ukraine aid packages, mainstream Dem support position)
- voting-rights=1 (auto-registration, election day holiday, 15 days early voting, opposes photo ID, DC statehood)
