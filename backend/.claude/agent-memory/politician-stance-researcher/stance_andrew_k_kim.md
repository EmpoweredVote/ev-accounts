## Key Platform Positions (from kimforla.com/Issues)

- "Economic Promotion Zones" in ethnically diverse neighborhoods: loan assistance, mentoring for small businesses, attract high-tech/biotech to inner-city
- 30,000 shelter beds within first year; expand Emergency Rental Assistance
- Convert city-owned property to affordable housing or temporary shelter
- Acquire land using city borrowing power for permanent housing
- Expand Homekey and Roomkey projects
- First-time homebuyer down payment assistance
- Partner with private sector to reduce construction costs
- "Promote responsible growth, expand job opportunities, and implement practical economic policies that support working families"
- Prepare LA for 2028 Olympics; improve city reputation
