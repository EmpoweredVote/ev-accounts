## Martin Heinrich — US Senator, New Mexico (D, since 2013)

**Key profile:** Progressive Democrat. Member of Senate Energy and Natural Resources Committee. Co-founded Senate AI Caucus (2019). 88% LCV rating. 100% NARAL, 100% Alliance for Retired Americans, 100% AFL-CIO.

**Notable nuance:** Despite GND cosponsor status, blocked fracking ban with 6 other Dems in February 2021 — places him at climate=3 (invest and transition) rather than 2 (rapid phase-out). M4A cosponsor but primary record reflects public option/ACA expansion path.

**Deportation:** NAY on Laken Riley Act (S.5, 2025). Vote 119-1-00007 confirmed.
**Trans athletes:** NAY on S.9 (March 2025). Vote 119-1-00100 confirmed.
**Same-sex marriage:** Original DOMA repeal cosponsor; YEA on Respect for Marriage Act.
**Campaign finance:** Supports Democracy for All Amendment (overturn Citizens United) + voter vouchers.
**Social security:** Signed SSA Protectors Pledge; opposes privatization and raising retirement age.
