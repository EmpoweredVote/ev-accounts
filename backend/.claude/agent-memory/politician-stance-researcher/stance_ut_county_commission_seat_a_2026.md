## Brent Bowles (R, Springville)
Republican convention winner (64.67% of delegate votes); nurse anesthetist (CRNA) + former Sandy Fire Dept firefighter.
- **taxes=4**: Anti-tax-increase, cut commissioner salaries $111K→$60K, restructure to 5-member part-time commission saving $250K.
- **growth-and-development=3**: Pro-growth with infrastructure-first. Quote: "We need to have our infrastructure in place as if we were already in the 2040s and 2050s." Concerned about high-density without infra.
- **housing=4**: Skeptical of high-density housing without infrastructure; said rapid high-density growth risks losing "family-centered lifestyle."
- **homelessness-response=4**: Explicitly calls for private programs over government services.
- **local-environment=3**: Supports nuclear energy exploration for growth; no strong anti-environment stance, no conservation positions found.
- SKIPPED: jail-capacity, local-immigration, city-sanitation, public-safety-approach, economic-development, transportation-priorities, residential-zoning (insufficient direct evidence)
