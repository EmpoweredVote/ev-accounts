# Eric Guerra — Sacramento City Council District 6

**Profile:** Democrat, D6 (Fruitridge/Valley Hi/Campus Commons/Sierra Oaks/Tahoe Park/Little Saigon), elected 2015, serving 4th term as of Dec 2024. Mayor Pro Tem as of January 2025. First elected via Tahoe Park Neighborhood Association grassroots work. Only Latino council member during mid-career. Previously ran for CA Senate District 6 (2022 race — ericguerra.org redirects to guerraforsenate2022.com which is ECONNREFUSED). District covers east/southeast Sacramento.
