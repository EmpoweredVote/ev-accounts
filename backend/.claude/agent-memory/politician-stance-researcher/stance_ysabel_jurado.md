## Politician
Ysabel J. Jurado — LA City Council District 14
- Assumed office: December 9, 2024
- Background: Housing rights attorney (tenant eviction defense, wage theft), civil rights attorney, DSA member
- First Filipino-descended person elected to LA City Council
- Defeated incumbent Kevin de León (of 2022 racism scandal)
- Inspired by fellow DSA council members Raman, Hernandez, Soto-Martinez
