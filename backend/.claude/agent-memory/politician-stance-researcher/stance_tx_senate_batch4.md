### Notes

- **Zaffirini**: Longest-serving woman in TX Senate (~39 years). 76,843 consecutive votes. First Mexican-American woman in TX Senate. District 21 includes Laredo (border). Moderate-progressive D. Personally opposes abortion but voted against restrictions. Was sole dissenter on bathroom bill committee 8-1.
- **Sparks**: Oil & gas CEO (Discovery Operating), elected 2023. Chairs Natural Resources Committee. 47-county West Texas/Panhandle district. Fiscal conservative. Trump-aligned.
- **Kolkhorst**: 6th-generation Texan, SD-18. Former 13-year TX House member. Chairs HHS Committee. Authored 2017 bathroom bill. Strong anti-abortion, anti-trans record. School vouchers: value 4 not 5 (no direct SB 2 co-authorship confirmed). Capital gains tax ban co-author (SJR 18).
- **Middleton**: Most conservative TX legislator per multiple rankings. Oil company CEO. Founded TX Constitutional Enforcement. Former TX House Freedom Caucus Chair. Trump-endorsed. SD-11 covers Galveston/Brazoria/Harris counties. Author of prayer-in-schools bill. Most topics scored 5.
- **Cook**: Houston Democrat, ER nurse, MPH from Johns Hopkins. Elected 2024 (thin record). SD-15 Houston. Co-authored Medicaid expansion + assault weapons ban with Zaffirini. Many topics inferred from profile rather than direct bill evidence.
