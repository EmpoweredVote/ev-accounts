# Traci Park — LA City Council District 11

**Researched:** 2026-04-14  
**Office:** Los Angeles City Council Member, District 11 (Venice, Mar Vista, Del Rey, Playa del Rey, Westchester, El Segundo area)  
**Elected:** November 2022 (succeeded Mike Bonin)  
**Background:** Former attorney at Burke, Williams & Sorensen (public entities practice); former registered Republican who switched to Democratic Party; West LA Neighborhood Council member; organized against homeless housing conversion (Ramada Inn) in 2020.  
**Characterization:** LA Times describes her as "the most conservative member of the Los Angeles City Council."
