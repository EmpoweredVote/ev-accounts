## Bien Doan — San Jose City Council District 7

**Office:** San Jose City Council, District 7 (East San Jose, Little Saigon, Vietnamese community hub)
**Party:** Democrat (fiscal conservative; dual endorsement Silicon Valley Biz PAC + South Bay Labor Council)
**Tenure:** Elected Nov 2022, incumbent; running for reelection 2026
**Background:** 22-year San José Fire Captain; Vietnamese immigrant; one of first Vietnamese-Americans elected to SJ council; defeated progressive incumbent Maya Esparza (53.7%)

## Key Quotes
- On homelessness audit: "The audit we are recommending evaluates our internal structure and practices and aims to find ways to improve our ability to help our unsheltered residents, be more accountable and efficient with taxpayer dollars, and improve our response times to quality of life issues."
- On RV crackdown: "We know there are bad actors out there that put dilapidated, nonworking van trailers or RVs out there, and then manipulate our unsheltered residents to rent these places in unsafe conditions, without any preparation for biohazard or hazardous waste or even a fire hazard."
- On immigrants (2025): "After more than 30 years serving our city as a firefighter and councilmember, I believe now more than ever we must support and protect our immigrant communities and ensure every resident feels safe and valued."

- On public safety (2026 forum): "We are the safest city in America. Why? Because we work hard at it."
