## John Lee — LA City Council District 12

**Background:** Son of South Korean immigrants; born 1970; Granada Hills HS; attended Cal State Northridge. Worked 20 years for City of LA under councilmembers Joel Wachs, Greig Smith, and Mitchell Englander (chief of staff). Elected 2019 special election; re-elected 2020 and 2024. Formerly registered Republican, changed to independent/declined-to-state in 2020. Only non-Democrat on the council. District covers Chatsworth, Granada Hills, West Hills, Porter Ranch, Northridge.

**Ethics:** Charged with 10 ethics violations (Oct 2023) related to 2017 Las Vegas trip with predecessor Mitch Englander. Ethics Commission fined him $138,424 (Dec 2025, maximum fine). He sued the Commission and plans to appeal.

**Key coalitions:** LA police officers' union (largest PAC contributor), LA firefighters' union, LA Area Chamber of Commerce PAC ($500k+), Hollywood Chamber, LA County Business Federation. Endorsed by business interests across the board.

**Critical source:** `https://laist.com/news/politics/2024-election-california-primary-los-angeles-city-council-district-12` — most detailed policy comparison available

**Key votes documented:**
- Voted AGAINST 2020 LAPD budget cuts (one of only 2 opposing votes)
- Voted AGAINST November 2023 rent cap reduction (Housing Committee, 3-2)
- Voted AGAINST January 2025 fire eviction protections
- Voted AGAINST November 2025 rent control reform (stated: "sends the message: Do not build here")
- INTRODUCED the resolution opposing SB 79 (August 2025, voted 8-5 against)
- Used 41.18 anti-camping law more than any other council member
- Voted unanimously (with full council) to place independent redistricting on 2024 ballot
- 2019: Introduced resolution urging Newsom to close Aliso Canyon gas storage facility
