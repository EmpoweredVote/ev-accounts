# Rafael Mandelman — SF District 8 Supervisor

**Role:** SF Board of Supervisors District 8 (Castro/Noe Valley/Glen Park/Diamond Heights), Board President since Jan 2025. Longest-serving current supervisor (since 2018). Openly gay Democrat. Ran for mayor 2024 then dropped out.

**ideology:** Moderate-progressive. Aligned with Mayor Lurie's moderate agenda in 2025. Votes "no" only ~4 times per year out of ~1,500-2,000 votes. Authors most laws of any current supervisor (23 passed in 2025).
