KEY BIOGRAPHY:
- Born 1978 Los Angeles; parents Hong Kong immigrants (Taishan ancestry)
- Occidental College 2000 (Public Policy, magna cum laude); UC Berkeley MPA 2003 (PPIA fellow)
- Deputy Director, Mayor's Office of Public Policy and Finance 2004-2007
- Appointed D4 Supervisor Sept 25, 2007 (replacing suspended Ed Jew); elected Nov 2008; re-elected Nov 2010
- Budget & Finance Committee Chair 2011-2013; closed $380M deficit 2011-12; first 2-yr budget 2012-13
- Appointed Assessor-Recorder Feb 6, 2013 by Mayor Ed Lee; won 2014 with 98% of vote
- Appointed City Administrator by Mayor London Breed, confirmed Jan 2021, sworn in Feb 2, 2021
- First Asian American woman as SF City Administrator
- UC Regents appointed July 2022 by Gov. Newsom
