**Tenure:** Since July 1, 2017; re-elected 2022 with ~68%

## CRITICAL Disambiguation

The Monica Rodriguez who authored AB-228 (firearms), AB-524 (Greek orgs), AB-2356 (theft aggregation), and other 2021-2022 state assembly bills is a DIFFERENT Monica Rodriguez (CA State Assemblywoman AD57, later redistricted to other districts). CD7 Rodriguez came from the LA Board of Public Works. Do NOT conflate these two people.
