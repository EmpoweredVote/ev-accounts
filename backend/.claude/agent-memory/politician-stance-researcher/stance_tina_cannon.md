Tina Cannon, Utah State Auditor (R, sworn Jan 6 2025). First woman elected to the position. NOT a former state legislator — served as Morgan County councilmember 2015-2021 and ran twice for US House UT-1 (2020 convention, 2022 primary, both lost). Worked as Deputy State Auditor 2024 before election.
