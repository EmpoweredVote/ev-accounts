## Cynthia S. Creem — MA Senate Majority Leader, Norfolk and Middlesex District

**Researched:** 2026-05-17

**Primary sources:** actonmass.org/legislators/cynthia-creem; malegislature.gov (Profile CSC0); commonwealthbeacon.org; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Creem

**Key pattern:** Long-serving senator (Newton area) with exceptional civil liberties record: forfeiture reform (S.1049), youth interrogation protections (S.108), juvenile record expungement (S.1058), prisoner rehabilitation (S.1651). Co-sponsored ONLY 2 of 31 Act on Mass tracked bills: Medicare for All and Age of Criminal Majority to 21. Did NOT co-sponsor Abortion Access Act, Safe Communities Act, 100% RE 2045, etc. But her own bills show genuine commitment to healthcare (Medicare for All selectively co-sponsored), housing (S.960 Brookline rent stabilization), voting rights (led automatic voter registration 2018, same-day voter reg S.505), and climate (net-zero 2050 op-ed). Senate Majority Leader position explains some restraint on co-sponsorship.

**Co-sponsorships per Act on Mass (yes):** Medicare for All, Age of Criminal Majority to 21

**Co-sponsorships per Act on Mass (no):** Everything else (29 of 31 bills)
