## Juanita Lopez — 2026 LA Mayor Candidate

**Background:** Native Angeleno, accountant with degrees in political science and behavioral science; former Federal Reserve Bank employee. Campaign website: https://www.juanitaformayor.com. Social: @juanita4mayor26 (Instagram), @JLopez4Mayor26 (X).

**Confirmed campaign priorities:** "Clean city … financial transparency … [and] tax reform."

## Researched (2026-05-06)

### economic-development → value 4
Market/tax-reduction oriented. Proposes cutting city sales tax to 8% to benefit small businesses and restaurants. Criticizes current administration for using "taxpayer funds to secure votes rather than tackle critical issues." Emphasizes efficient government over public investment programs.
Sources: https://www.juanitaformayor.com/issues, https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-la-mayor

### local-environment → value 3
"Clean city" platform = 24/7 sanitation, no-littering law enforcement, inmate street-cleaning program. Frames environment as cleanliness/maintenance not climate policy. No mention of air quality, renewable energy, climate emergency, or green infrastructure.
Sources: https://www.juanitaformayor.com/logistics, https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-guides/2026-election-california-primary-la-mayor
