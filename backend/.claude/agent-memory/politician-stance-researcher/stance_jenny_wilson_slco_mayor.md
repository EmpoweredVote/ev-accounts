## Jenny Wilson — Salt Lake County Mayor

**Party:** Democrat | **Office:** Salt Lake County Mayor since Jan 2019 (re-elected Nov 2024 over Erin Rider by ~49k votes) | **Prior:** 10 years SL County Council, 2018 US Senate candidate

### Scores (local topics, 2026-05-30)

- **housing = 2** — $25M Housing Trust Fund, 1,000 supportive units in 5 yrs, June 2024 ADU ordinance
- **homelessness-response = 2** — 5-yr Human Services/Homelessness/CJ Action Plan; crisis receiving center; HOME Court; goal: "brief, rare, non-recurring"
- **local-immigration = 2** — Opposes ICE detention facility (2026), calls for "path to citizenship," supports "secure and orderly border" but anti-local-deportation-infrastructure
- **local-environment = 2** — Created Office of Environmental Services; Green Vision initiative; Flip the Strip water conservation; EV fleet transition; Great Salt Lake advocacy
- **economic-development = 2** — Active public investment: sold 6.5 acres to Smith Entertainment Group for SECC district; bottom-up incentives framing
- **growth-and-development = 3** — "Responsible growth" via regional planning; Wasatch Front Regional Council member; pro-growth but with environmental guardrails; opposes gondola/quarry
- **transportation-priorities = 2** — TRAX ambassador program (~$9M/yr); Millcreek sidewalks; opposes gondola; 2025 sales tax paired jail+transportation; personal transit week 2019
- **residential-zoning = 2** — June 2024 ADU ordinance relaxing restrictions; Housing Trust Fund for infill; "key is increasing supply across all housing types"
- **public-safety-approach = 3** — Championed failed $507M bond (Nov 2024); backed 2025 sales tax for 248 beds + CJ programs; pairs enforcement with treatment/reentry
- **jail-capacity = 2** — Led 5-yr plan with Justice & Accountability Center (100 reentry beds + 250-300 supportive housing) + crisis receiving center as diversion; said plan "will change" but is "road map"
