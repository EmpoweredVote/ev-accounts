**Office:** Massachusetts State Senate, Worcester and Hampden District (Sutton, Webster area)
**Party:** Republican (Assistant Minority Leader)

## Key Bills Found
- **S.1096 (SHIELD Act)** — Mandates MA law enforcement compliance with ICE detainers for aggravated felons, violent criminals, gang members, terrorism suspects; applies to state police, municipal police, sheriffs, courts, corrections
- **S.1683 ("ensuring safety of communities")** — ICE transfer authorization with supervisory approval + 48-hour judicial review; prohibits registries based on religion/national origin; recurring bill across multiple sessions
- **S.350** — Prohibits public schools from requiring single-sex sports team athletes to compete against opposing teams with athletes of the opposite sex (trans athlete ban); Education Committee said "ought NOT to pass" July 2025
- **S.1315** — E-verify for Massachusetts workers (immigration status employment verification)
- **S.1015** — Recover emergency assistance funds used on non-residents with financial sponsors
- **S.1976/S.1989/S.1995** — Abolish estate tax ("death tax"); recurring across sessions
