## John Bryant — TX House HD-114 (Democrat)

**Politician ID:** 70920806-a146-4dc3-8cc7-4566f9caf903
**Office:** Texas House of Representatives, District 114
**Party:** Democrat
**Session:** 89th Legislature (2025)
**Committee assignments:** Pensions, Investments & Financial Services; Public Education

### Background
- Born Feb 22, 1947, Lake Jackson TX
- US House TX-5th District 1983–1997
- Returned to TX House 2023 after 24-year absence, citing alarm over "Trump forces" influence on Texas
- Currently pursuing SMU Perkins School of Theology degree

### Key Legislative Actions (89R)
- Co-authored HB5 (dementia research fund — bipartisan)
- Co-authored HB50 (STD screening informed consent — bipartisan)
- NOT a co-author of HB3 (school vouchers), HB8/HB5580 (ICE cooperation), HB44 (abortion exceptions), SB16 (voter ID)

### Housing (important outlier)
