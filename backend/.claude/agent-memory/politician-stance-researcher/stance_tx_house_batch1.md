## TX Legislature Research Notes

### Key 89th Session Bills (for future research)
- **SB2/HB3**: ESA/school voucher bills — signed May 3, 2025. Louderback + Schoolcraft cosponsored; Davis, Allen, Hernandez did NOT
- **SB8**: Mandatory ICE 287(g) for counties 100k+ — signed June 20, 2025. Louderback was House sponsor; others not listed
- **SB31/HB44**: Medical exceptions to abortion ban — SB31 signed June 20, 2025; HB44 left pending in committee. Davis + Allen coauthored HB44; Davis + Allen + Hernandez cosponsored SB31
- **HB2**: $8.5B school funding — Louderback coauthor; others not listed
- **HB200**: Juvenile sentencing reform for under-18 offenses — Davis cosponsor
