Suzanne Harrison, Salt Lake County Council At-Large Seat B (D, elected Nov 2022, assumed office Jan 2023, term through Dec 2028). Former UT House Rep HD-32 (Sandy/Draper, 2019–2022). Physician. Minority Leader 2024.

## Scored local topics (2026-05-29)

- **jail-capacity=3** — Voted with 8-1 majority to put $507M public safety bond on 2024 ballot; bond would consolidate Oxbow+Metro jails, add mental health beds, build Justice and Accountability Center for diversion. Reform-modernization approach, not pure expansion or reduction.
- **housing=2** — Voted for $70M+ affordable housing in Legislature; supports smart density near transit; backs county 5-year plan for 1,000 housing units (incl. permanent supportive housing).
- **local-immigration=2** — Democratic caucus statement (Harrison + Johnson + Pinkney + Romero) strongly opposing ICE mega-detention center in Salt Lake City (2026); cited civil liberties, dignity, community safety.
- **public-safety-approach=2** — Mental health/diversion-first framing; Justice and Accountability Center model; bond positioned as mental health + job training + housing pathways; physician background.
- **local-environment=2** — Co-chaired UT Legislature Bipartisan Clean Air Caucus 4 years; founded Tier3Gas.org; opposed Parley's Canyon mine; fought mining-loophole legislation; prioritizes air quality and water conservation on County Council.
- **growth-and-development=3** — Supports managed/responsible growth through collaboration with cities; smart density near transit; opposes extractive expansion in sensitive areas; preserves open space and trails.
- **homelessness-response=2** — Championed 2024 five-year homelessness plan (1,000 housing units including 300 group homes + 300 permanent supportive); housing-first with services orientation; supported $507M bond.
- **transportation-priorities=2** — Opposes gondola since Day One; advocates redirecting gondola funds to fare-free UTA in winter; prioritizes transit, protected bike/pedestrian routes for all residents; "transportation projects should benefit all county residents, not just ski resorts."
- **residential-zoning=2** — Explicitly supports increased density near transit; campaign platform: "Smart density is vital to making that an affordable reality"; fought mining-loophole zoning legislation.
