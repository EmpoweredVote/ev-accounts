# Burhan Azeem — Cambridge, MA City Councillor / Vice Mayor

**Office:** Cambridge City Councillor (elected 2021, re-elected 2025); Vice Mayor 2025-present
**Age:** Born ~1991; youngest Cambridge councillor when first elected at age 30 in 2021
**Background:** Founded a housing advocacy nonprofit; data engineering professional; Democrat
**Key roles:** Chair of Housing Committee; co-chair of Finance Committee (with Siddiqui and Nolan)

## Defining Political Identity

Known YIMBY (Yes In My Backyard) housing advocate. Strongest pro-density voice on the council. Paired YIMBY stance with "tenant protections to prevent displacement." Transportation: strong cycling/transit advocate who earned "bike champion" status from Cambridge Bicycle Safety. On surveillance/public safety: voted FOR police tech (license plate readers, phone hacker) unlike progressive bloc.
