## Key 89th Session Bills Used as Evidence

- **SB2** (school vouchers/ESA): Signed 5/3/2025. All House R's voted for, all D's voted against.
- **SB31** (abortion exceptions — physician judgment): Signed 6/20/2025. Passed committee 11-0, broad bipartisan House vote. Expanded medical exceptions under Texas near-total ban.
- **SB11** (Bible/prayer in public schools): Signed 6/20/2025. House vote taken RV#3315 but individual totals not yet published.
- **SB12** (DEI ban + social transitioning ban in schools): Signed 6/20/2025. House vote: **102-42**. Democrats voted nay (42 nays = most of the 65-member Democratic caucus, some absent/not voting).
- **SB8/HB5580** (ICE-sheriff cooperation agreements): Signed 6/20/2025. Committee vote 6-4; floor vote pending publication.
- **SB16** (proof of citizenship for voter registration): Passed Senate, in House Calendars as of 5/25/2025. Not yet signed but expected to pass.
- **SB4** (homestead property tax exemption increase): Signed 6/16/2025. Bipartisan.

## Member Notes

### Mary Ann Perez (D, HD-144)
- Insurance agent, Harris County, district includes Pasadena
- House Appropriations Committee
- Incumbent since 2017 (lost 2014, regained 2016)
- Strong progressive; opposes all restrictions and vouchers
- Topics scored: abortion (2), school-vouchers (1), healthcare (2), immigration (2), deportation (2), civil-rights (2), taxes (2), voting-rights (2)

### Mary Gonzalez (D, HD-75)
- First openly pansexual elected official in US
- Chair, TX House LGBTQ Caucus (since 2019)
- Secretary, House Border Caucus; VP, Mexican American Leg Caucus
- Equality Texas Champion of Equality 2015
- El Paso County border district
- Topics scored: abortion (2), same-sex-marriage (1), trans-athletes (1), school-vouchers (1), immigration (2), deportation (2), civil-rights (2), healthcare (2), religious-freedom (3)

### Matt Morgan (R, HD-26)
- First-term member (assumed office Jan 2025)
- Defeated incumbent Jacey Jetton in 2024 Republican primary as more conservative challenger
- Insurance adjuster, Sam Houston State alum
- Campaign platform: lower property taxes, border security, God-given freedoms
- Fort Bend County / Katy area
- Topics scored: abortion (4), school-vouchers (5), immigration (4), deportation (4), taxes (4), trans-athletes (4), religious-freedom (5), civil-rights (5), voting-rights (4)

### Matt Shaheen (R, HD-66)
- Founding member, Texas Freedom Caucus (most conservative bloc)
- Incumbent since Jan 2015 (District 66, Collin County)
- Endorsed Ted Cruz 2016, supported Ken Paxton impeachment
- Has authored election crime prosecution bill, authored anti-quorum break legislation
- Topics scored: abortion (4), school-vouchers (5), immigration (5), deportation (5), taxes (5), trans-athletes (4), religious-freedom (5), civil-rights (5), voting-rights (5), redistricting (5)

### Mihaela Plesa (D, HD-70)
- First Democrat to win Collin County seat in decades
- Romanian-American, first generation
- Vice Chair, TX House Democratic Caucus (unanimously elected 2025)
- Named Freshman of the Year by TX Legislative Study Group
- Focuses on kitchen-table issues, bipartisan water/tax work, avoids culture war issues
- KEY QUOTE: "Can I pay my car insurance? Can I go to the doctor and it be affordable?" — healthcare framing
- Explicitly stated: supports public school funding with "guardrails" against voucher programs
- 55 bills filed in freshman term, 2 became law
- Topics scored: abortion (2), school-vouchers (1), healthcare (3), immigration (3), deportation (3), taxes (3), civil-rights (2), voting-rights (2)
