## David Spencer (R, former Orem City Council member, convention route)
- 5 topics scored 2026-05-31
- taxes=4 (opposes increases; "tax increases must be put on the ballot for citizens to decide"; cut commissioner salaries/travel)
- growth-and-development=4 (county "lacks water and road infrastructure to sustain" growth; strongly pro-slowing)
- residential-zoning=4 ("I don't like high density on State Street and I never will"; voted to roll back Orem State Street Master Plan 2022)
- housing=4 (consistent opposition to high-density; protect neighborhood character; let cities decide)
- economic-development=3 (pro-business, pro-grant-seeking, commercial-friendly while anti-density)

## Isaac Paxman (R, former Provo Deputy Mayor + asst. AG, signature-gathering route)
- 7 topics scored 2026-05-31
- taxes=4 ("taxpayer dollars must be treated with strict discipline"; balanced Provo budgets without tax hikes)
- growth-and-development=3 (case-by-case with each city; diverse land uses; "Growth must not erase what makes us distinct")
