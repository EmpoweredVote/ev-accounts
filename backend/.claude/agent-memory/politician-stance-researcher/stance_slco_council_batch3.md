## Salt Lake County Council Batch 3 — 2026-05-30

**Key vote anchor:** Aug 2024 council voted 8-1 to place $507M Public Safety Bond on Nov ballot (consolidated jails, +444 beds, mental health, transitional). Voters rejected it ~52-48%. Feb 2025 7-2 vote to levy new sales tax (~$76M/yr: 50% state transit, 25% city transport, 25% county public safety incl. $6.4M for 184 Oxbow Jail beds + recidivism programs).
