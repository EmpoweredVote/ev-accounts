## TX House Batch 25 — Research Complete 2026-05-18

### Richard Hayes (R, HD-57, East Texas)
- Republican attorney; district covers East Texas (formerly Trent Ashby's seat)
- **abortion=3**: HB44 co-author (bipartisan medical exceptions bill)
- **school-vouchers=5**: HB3 co-author (universal ESA/voucher bill, signed 02/26/2025)
- **taxes=4**: co-authored HB9 (personal property tax exemption), HB22 (intangible property tax exemption), HB24 (zoning/property)
- **fossil-fuels=4**: co-authored HB14 (nuclear energy expansion; Texas Advanced Nuclear Deployment Office)
- **voting-rights=4**: co-authored HB196 (elections bill by Vasut); Republican baseline
- **healthcare=4**: Republican, no evidence of public option support

### Richard Raymond (D, HD-31, Laredo)
- Long-serving moderate Democrat representing Texas's largest inland port
- **abortion=3**: HB44 co-author (bipartisan medical exceptions)
- **school-vouchers=1**: NOT on HB3; co-authored HB2 (public education finance) — strong public school defender for border community
- **taxes=3**: co-authored HB8 (school tax rate reduction) and HB9 (property tax exemption) — moderate tax pragmatist
- **immigration=2**: Laredo border district; pro-legal immigration, pro-farmworker, expanded pathways
- **deportation=3**: border Democrat; prioritize recent crossers/criminals, protect long-term residents
- **healthcare=3**: moderate Democrat; cost regulation but not single-payer

### Robert Guerra (D, HD-41, Edinburg/Rio Grande Valley)
- Democrat since 2012; attorney and former TV reporter; committees: Energy Resources, Environmental Regulation
- **abortion=3**: HB44 co-author
- **school-vouchers=1**: NOT on HB3; co-authored HB20 (Applied Sciences Pathway, public education)
- **fossil-fuels=4**: HB49 co-author (oil/gas waste treatment and liability limits); Energy Resources committee
- **immigration=2**: RGV Democrat representing heavily immigrant district; supports expanded legal pathways
- **healthcare=2**: Democrat in most-uninsured TX region (Rio Grande Valley); supports public option/coverage expansion

- **abortion=2**: Wikipedia confirms "supports abortion rights"; HB44 co-author; Progressive Caucus = pro-choice
- **school-vouchers=1**: NOT on HB3; HB2 co-author; strongly opposes as Black Caucus chair (majority-minority public schools)
- **healthcare=2**: Wikipedia: advocates "expanding Medicare to address Texas's high uninsured rate"

- **voting-rights=2**: participated in 2021 Democrat walkout to block voting restrictions; arrested at Capitol pro-voting-rights demo
- **taxes=2**: proposed $15/hr minimum wage; reparations legislation; Progressive Caucus founder
- **immigration=2**: Democrat from majority-minority Fort Bend; supports expanded legal pathways
- **climate-change=2**: Progressive Caucus + environmental-justice district (Fort Bend)

- **school-vouchers=5**: HB3 co-author (universal ESA/voucher bill)
- **taxes=4**: co-authored HB8 (school tax reduction), HB9 (property tax exemption)

### Research Notes
- HB44 (abortion exceptions) was a bipartisan bill — all 3 Dems co-authored it AND so did Hayes (R). The bill is the 89th session's attempt to add medical exceptions; co-authorship signals moderate (3) not extreme on abortion for both parties
- HB3 (school vouchers) = universal ESA; Hayes and Guillen (R) are on it; all 3 Dems are NOT
- HB5 (Dementia Research Institute, 119 co-authors) = bipartisan research bill — not policy-signal evidence
- HB2 (public ed finance) = broad bipartisan bill — Guillen, Reynolds, Raymond co-authored; not a policy signal specifically
- Guillen: voted with Republicans on social issues even as Democrat — weight Republican record heavily since 2021
