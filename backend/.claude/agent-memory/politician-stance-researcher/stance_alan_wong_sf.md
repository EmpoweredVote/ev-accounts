## Alan Wong — SF District 4 Supervisor (Sunset)

**Appointed:** November 30, 2025 by Mayor Lurie (replacing Beya Alcaraz). Sworn Dec 1, 2025. Running in 2026 election.

**Background:** Born 1986/87, lifelong Sunset resident, children of Hong Kong immigrants. Abraham Lincoln HS → UC San Diego (BA) → USF (MPA). CA National Guard First Lieutenant. Former: City College SF Board of Trustees (elected 2020, president 2x); legislative aide to Supervisor Gordon Mar; policy director Children's Council of SF; union organizer.

**Ideological arc:** 2020 campaign = strongly progressive (defund SFPD -25%, anti-YIMBY, expand rent control, CEO pay tax, fossil fuel divestment, public bank). 2025 role = moderate aligned with Lurie agenda (fully staff SFPD, end open-air drug markets, YIMBYish zoning vote). Described by SF Standard as one of Lurie's "reliable six" moderate votes.

**Committee:** Public Safety and Neighborhood Services Committee; SF County Transportation Authority.
