## Kerry Thomson — Mayor of Bloomington, Indiana

**Role:** 33rd Mayor of Bloomington, IN. Took office January 1, 2024. Former CEO Habitat for Humanity Monroe County; former ED IU Center for Rural Engagement. Democrat.

## Verified Stances

### homelessness — Score: 2
$9M+ invested in affordable housing, eviction prevention, rapid rehousing since 2024. $600K committed to Beacon Center (opens 2027). Convened local providers for housing action plan. Quote (July 2025 press conference): "The need is great and growing. Our housing support services work hard, but demand continues to outpace capacity." Also said "we cannot arrest our way out of homelessness" (July 2025, March 2026 State of the City). Cleared encampments but with 30-day notice and outreach workers. Criticized Indiana's 2026 law criminalizing homeless camps.
- Source: https://bloomington.in.gov/news/2025/07/30/6313
- Source: https://bloomington.in.gov/sites/default/files/2025-11/Housing_Investment_Report_20251120_chk.pdf

### redistricting — Score: 2
Called Trump's mid-decade Indiana congressional redistricting "all a political stunt" (IPM Ask the Mayor, Nov 19 2025). Said "even Trump said his motivation was to try to get two extra Republican seats." Opposes partisan legislative control of redistricting.
- Source: https://www.ipm.org/show/askthemayor/2025-11-19/bloomingtons-thomson-on-new-jail-stadium-district-best-lit-square

### childcare — Score: 2
Platform explicitly includes childcare near affordable housing. Attended Dec 2024 BHA groundbreaking for co-located childcare (28 infants/toddlers) + affordable apartments. Treats childcare as workforce/economic development issue.
- Source: https://kerryforbloomington.com/platform

### religious-freedom — Score: 2
Bloomington received a perfect 100/100 HRC Municipal Equality Index score for the 10th consecutive year (Nov 2024), grading city leadership's public stance on LGBTQ+ equality and anti-discrimination. Thomson's Nov 2024 statement committed to "a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for all residents" with no religious exemptions. Aligns with stance 2 (protect religious freedom while not allowing it to override anti-discrimination protections).
- Source: https://bloomington.in.gov/news/2024/11/27/6108
- Source: https://bloomington.in.gov/news/2024/11/08/6093

### voting-rights — Score: 2
Thomson publicly supported converting Monroe County's 29 polling places to a vote center system (April 2025), where any registered voter could vote at any county location — aligns with expanding access (stance 2). No evidence of support for voter ID restrictions or roll-purging.
- Source: https://www.idsnews.com/article/2025/04/residents-support-monroe-county-vote-system-change
- Source: https://bloomingtonian.com/2025/05/21/press-release-monroe-county-commissioners-slam-election-board-for-blocking-vote-centers-call-decision-a-blow-to-voter-access/
