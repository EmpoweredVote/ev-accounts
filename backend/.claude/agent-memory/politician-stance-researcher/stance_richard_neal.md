- Abortion=4: CRITICAL — voted for partial-birth abortion bans 2000 and 2003; voted for Stupak-Pitts Amendment (restricting abortion funding in ACA); stated "I have always opposed taxpayer funding of abortion. I'd keep Roe v. Wade and restrict it." Co-sponsored Women's Health Protection Act 2021 but overall record is distinctly conservative for MA Democrat.
- Ukraine=4: Joined 49 Democrats voting FOR a measure to ban sending cluster munitions to Ukraine in 2023, breaking with Biden.
