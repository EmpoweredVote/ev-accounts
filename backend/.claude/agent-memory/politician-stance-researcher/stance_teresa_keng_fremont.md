Teresa Keng (Fremont-District-1-Council-Member, D, first elected 2020, re-elected 2024)

District: 1 (west Fremont area)
Party: Democrat
Background: Previously ran for Alameda County Board of Supervisors (District 2, 2022). On council since 2020.

## Stances Researched (4 topics)

- **homelessness** = 4: Voted YES with 6-member majority on Feb11-2025 camping ban (6-1 final). Also voted YES with majority on Dec2024 property-removal ordinance. Supported Mar2025 amendment removing aiding-and-abetting clause while keeping ban.
- **homelessness-response** = 3: Quoted in Mar2026 emergency shelter crisis declaration expressing frustration about delayed Measure W funding: "Our taxpayers have paid towards Measure W and we have this huge need." Supports services investment alongside enforcement.
- **public-safety-approach** = 3: Voted Aye on routine consent items Jul2024 including police equipment. Supported FY2025-26 budget with 40+ new police officers. No defunding advocacy, no dramatic expansion push. Standard centrist position.
- **local-immigration** = 2: In Feb2025 questioned the "Fremont-First" neutrality policy, specifically concerned it would restrict "charitable initiatives, such as a 2021 fundraiser for Afghan refugees." Humanitarian sympathies on immigration; not a sanctuary advocate but protective of charitable capacity.
