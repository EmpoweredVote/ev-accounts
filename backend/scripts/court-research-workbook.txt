PURPOSE
-------
For each law firm that donated to a City Attorney candidate, search lacourt.org
to find whether that firm appears as a party in LA Superior Court cases.
A City Attorney is the City's lawyer — firms that appear in those cases could
create a recusal-worthy conflict if the CA is elected.

Pricing: $1.00/search (registered) | $4.75 flat (guest)
