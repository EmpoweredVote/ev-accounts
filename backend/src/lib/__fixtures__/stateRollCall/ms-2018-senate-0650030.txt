Mississippi State Senate
2018 Regular Session
YEAS AND NAYS On H. B. No. 1083. On motion of Senator Hopson, the rules
were suspended, the bill considered engrossed, read the third time and, the yeas and
nays being taken, it passed as amended, title standing as stated, by the following vote:
Yeas--Blackwell, Browning, Bryan, Burton, Carmichael, Carter, Chassaniol,
Clarke, DeBar, Doty, Fillingane, Gollott, Harkins, Hopson, Jackson S. (32nd), Jolly,
McMahan, Michel, Moran, Parker, Parks, Polk, Tollison, Whaley, Wiggins, Wilemon,
Younger. Total--27.
Nays--Blackmon, Blount, Butler, Dawkins, Dearing, Frazier, Hill, Horhn, Jackson
G. (15th), Jackson R. (11th), Massey, McDaniel, Norwood, Seymour, Simmons D. T.
(12th), Turner-Ford, Watson, Witherspoon. Total--18.
Absent and those not voting--Caughman, Hudson, Jordan, Kirby. Total--4.
Voting Present--Branning. Total--1.
Senator Simmons W. (13th), who would have voted yea on H. B. No. 1083,
announced a pair with Senator Barnett, who would have voted nay.
