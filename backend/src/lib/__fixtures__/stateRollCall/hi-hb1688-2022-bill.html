Measure details
Measure Title: RELATING TO REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES.
Report Title: Motor Vehicle Registration; Counties; Registration Fees; U-drive Motor Vehicles; Tourism; Traffic Congestion
Description: Subjects U-drive motor vehicles to the same motor vehicle registration fees as other motor vehicles. Authorizes the counties to use certain motor vehicle registration fees to mitigate and address the impacts of tourism-related traffic congestion. Effective 1/1/2023. (CD1)
Companion:  SB2572
Package: None
Current Referral: TRS/GVO, WAM
