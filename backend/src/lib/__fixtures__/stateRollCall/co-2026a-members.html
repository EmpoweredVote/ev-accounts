The General Assembly consists of 100 members - 35 Senators and 65 Representatives.

Senators serve four-year terms, while Representatives serve two-year terms. All members are limited to serving for eight consecutive years in their chamber - four terms for Representatives and two terms for Senators.

As of the 2020 census, State Senators serve an average of 164,693 residents and State Representatives serve an average of 88,826 residents.

Download Member Directory (Updated 01/13/2026)

The President of the Senate, Speaker of the House of Representatives, and the Majority and Minority Leaders of each chamber serve as the primary leadership for the legislature.

These six members are responsible for the day-to-day operations of the House and Senate as well as serving as the oversight authority for the legislative service agencies. Each house elects additional leadership positions as well
