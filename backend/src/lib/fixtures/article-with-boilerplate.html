Council approves library levy after tense debate

By Sam Reporter | Updated Tuesday

The city council voted 5 to 2 on Tuesday to approve a new library funding levy, Mayor Jane Doe said after the meeting, ending months of contentious negotiation over how to keep neighborhood branches open.

"This levy keeps our branches open and our doors staffed," Doe said, adding that construction on the long-delayed east-side branch would begin in the spring once the county certifies the results.

Opponents argued the measure raised property taxes at the wrong time, but supporters countered that the library system had absorbed a decade of flat budgets while serving record numbers of residents.

The levy is expected to raise about twelve million dollars a year for five years, with the first collections beginning next January, according to the city finance office.
